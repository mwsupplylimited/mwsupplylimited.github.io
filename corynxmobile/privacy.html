Privacy Policy

Corynx: System Monitor · Last updated 2 July 2026

Corynx: System Monitor (“Corynx”, “the app”, “we”) is developed by MW Supply Limited. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle. We designed Corynx to keep your information on your device.

The system metrics stay on your device

Corynx reads CPU, memory, storage, network, battery, thermal and device information using Apple’s public, on-device APIs. These measurements are shown to you and are never transmitted, uploaded, or shared. We do not have a server that receives them, and we cannot see your device’s statistics.

We do not require an account

There is no sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location, or any personal information to use the app.

Advertising in the free version

The free version of Corynx displays advertising through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation to support development. To serve and measure ads, the advertising SDK and its demand partners may access and process device-level identifiers (such as an advertising identifier) and technical information (such as device type, OS version, coarse region and general ad-interaction data). This data is collected and used by those advertising providers, not by us. We do not receive or store it.

  • Your choice (App Tracking Transparency). On first launch of the free version, iOS asks whether Corynx may track you across apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not used for cross-app tracking and you will see less-personalized ads.
  • Personalized ads. Corynx has a Personalized ads switch in Settings → Ad privacy inside the app. It is off by default: unless you turn it on, Corynx asks its advertising providers not to use your activity to choose ads, and you see non-personalized ones instead.
  • No ads with Pro. Subscribing to Corynx Pro removes all advertising, and the advertising SDK is never started.

Unity’s handling of this data is governed by the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. You can also review your device-level controls in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising and Tracking.

Purchases

Subscriptions (Corynx Pro) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple provides us only anonymized, aggregate sales information.

Preferences

Your in-app settings (such as the chosen refresh rate and display options) are stored locally on your device using iOS’s standard preferences and are not transmitted to us.

Children

Corynx is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
