RoadBook

A simple, private GPS trip & mileage logger. Version 1.2

About

RoadBook is a personal trip logger for your drives. It records the route, distance, duration, speed and elevation of every drive, can start and stop itself automatically when you set off and arrive, and keeps a bird's-eye view of everywhere you've ever driven. Your trips stay on your device, or in your own iCloud account — we never receive them.

RoadBook is free with ads. The optional RoadBook Pro subscription removes all ads and unlocks automatic recording, the interactive Speed/Altitude charts, GPX/JSON export, the Explore world map, live Driving Mode overlays, and iCloud sync across your devices.

Driving Mode

Tap the steering-wheel button on the map (Record tab) to open Driving Mode — a big, navigation-style map built for the road ahead, with your live speed front and centre. Where local open data allows, it shows nearby speed cameras and red-light cameras and gives you a chime as you approach one. These camera alerts are free.

Camera locations are shown only where it is legal in your region — RoadBook detects the country you're driving in and hides camera locations where local rules don't allow them. The data comes from public open-data sources: government open data in Hong Kong, and OpenStreetMap elsewhere; it may be incomplete or out of date, so always follow the road and posted signs.

With RoadBook Pro, Driving Mode also overlays live parking-space availability and traffic cameras (tap a camera for a recent snapshot), where that open data is available.

Getting started

  • Grant location "While Using" the first time you open the app to record a drive manually. For background auto‑start, grant "Allow Always" — iOS needs that to wake the app when you start driving (the app shows a reminder if it's still set to "While Using").
  • Tap Start Recording on the Record tab to begin. The big number is your live speed; the grid below tracks distance, duration, altitude, max and average speed, and elevation gain.
  • Leave the app or lock the phone — recording keeps going. RoadBook records in the background.
  • Auto‑start has three choices in Settings: Off (record manually), Only While Open (starts on its own while the app is open), or In the Background (starts even when the app is closed). For background mode, pick Battery Saver (lightest on battery) or Full Accuracy (captures a drive from the very first metre). Auto‑stop saves the trip once you've been parked a while.
  • Trips tab lists every saved drive. Tap one for the route map, full stats, and interactive Speed‑over‑time and Altitude‑over‑time charts (drag sideways to replay your drive). Export any trip as GPX or JSON, and expand the map to full screen.
  • Explore tab overlays every trip on one world map. Move and zoom with two fingers; with one finger, tap or drag a route to see which trips passed through that spot. It also lists the countries and cities you've driven through, and the map expands to full screen.

iCloud Sync & Backup

With RoadBook Pro, if you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled, your trips are stored in your own iCloud Documents container (iCloud.com.edfd.DrivingRecorder) and follow you across your devices automatically. There is no separate account or password — iOS uses your Apple ID. The Settings → iCloud Sync section shows the current state: Syncing… while trips are downloading, and a green Synced with iCloud once they're all backed up; there's a one‑tap reload too. The first time you open RoadBook on a new device (or after reinstalling), it shows Syncing… for a few seconds while your drives download from iCloud, then they all appear. If iCloud is not available, trips are kept on the device.

FAQ

I reinstalled the app (or got a new device) — where are my trips?

If you're a RoadBook Pro member signed in to the same iCloud account, your trips download back automatically — open the app and wait a few seconds while it shows Syncing…, then they reappear (you can also tap the reload control in the Trips tab or Settings). On the free plan trips are stored only on the device, so deleting the app removes them — upgrade to Pro to back them up to your own iCloud.

Why is altitude / elevation gain showing 0?

Altitude comes from a combination of GPS and the device's built-in barometer. The iOS Simulator has no barometer and reports only latitude and longitude, so altitude reads 0 there. On a real device outdoors, altitude populates within a few seconds of the first GPS fix.

Why is my average speed lower than I expected?

Average speed is computed over elapsed recording time, including any time you spent stationary (red lights, traffic). It's never higher than your max speed by construction.

Auto‑start didn't fire when I started driving.

Make sure Auto‑start is set to In the Background in Settings, and that location access is Always (not just While Using) — iOS needs that to wake the app. In Battery Saver mode the app relies on iOS significant-location-change monitoring, which can take a short distance after you set off before recording kicks in; choose Full Accuracy to capture from the very first metre. You can also pick Only While Open, or just tap Start Recording manually.

Where is my trip data sent?

Nowhere we control. Trips live on your device, and (if enabled) inside your personal iCloud account — we never receive them. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where does Driving Mode's camera and parking data come from?

From public open-data sources, read-only: government open data in Hong Kong (speed/red-light cameras, live car-park availability and traffic-camera snapshots, via data.gov.hk), and OpenStreetMap for speed cameras in other regions. RoadBook sends these providers no account, identifier or trip data; for the OpenStreetMap lookup it sends only a coarse (~1 km rounded) coordinate so it can find cameras near you. Camera locations appear only where local law permits, and the data may be incomplete or out of date — always follow the road and posted signs. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I get my trips off the phone?

Open a trip from the Trips tab and use Export GPX (for mapping tools like Google Earth, Strava, RideWithGPS) or Export JSON (for raw data). Either option opens the standard iOS share sheet, so you can send to Files, Mail, AirDrop, or any compatible app.

Will it drain my battery?

While recording, the app uses GPS continuously, which is energy-intensive. When you're not driving, it depends on your background mode: Battery Saver keeps GPS off and relies on Apple's very-low-power significant-location-change wake-up, while Full Accuracy keeps GPS active so it never misses the start of a drive (at a higher battery cost). With auto‑start Off or set to Only While Open, nothing runs in the background at all.

Why am I seeing ads, and what is the tracking prompt?

On the free plan RoadBook shows ads through Google AdMob — a banner on the Trips screen and a short video ad when you save a manually recorded drive (your drive always saves, even if no ad is available). On first launch iOS asks whether RoadBook may track you: choosing Allow permits personalized ads, while Ask App Not to Track still shows ads but non-personalized ones. Subscribe to RoadBook Pro to remove ads entirely. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
