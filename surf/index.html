Surf

A fast, private web browser for iPhone & iPad. Version 1.0

About

Surf is a private web browser for iPhone and iPad. Browse with multiple tabs, block ads and trackers, and keep your bookmarks, history, and Top Sites on your device. A secure-connection indicator shows when a site is encrypted, and a one-tap Clear Data wipes your browsing data whenever you want. There is no account and no sign-up, and nothing you browse is sent to MW Supply Limited.

Surf keeps your browsing on your device. Your tabs, bookmarks, history, and Top Sites live only on your iPhone or iPad — MW Supply Limited runs no servers that receive them, and there is no account to sign in to.

Getting started

  • Open a new tab and type a search term or a web address to start browsing.
  • Tap the star to bookmark pages you want to keep, and organise them into folders.
  • Ad & tracker blocking is on by default for a cleaner, faster, more private web — adjust it any time in Settings.
  • Choose your search engine in Settings — Google, DuckDuckGo, or Bing.

Features

  • Private web browser with multiple tabs and a grid tab switcher
  • Private tabs that keep no history and clear their session when you close them
  • Bookmarks and folders, plus history and Top Sites — all kept on your device
  • Optional ad & tracker blocking for a cleaner, faster web
  • Secure-connection (HTTPS) indicator in the address bar
  • Choice of search engine: Google, DuckDuckGo, or Bing

FAQ

Is my browsing kept private?

Yes. The pages you visit, your cookies, and anything you type — including logins you enter on websites — are handled by Apple's WebKit and stay on your device. They are never sent to us, and there is no account to sign in to. You can clear everything any time in Settings.

Does Surf block ads and trackers?

Yes, with an optional on-device content blocker that removes many ads and known trackers from the web pages you load, for a cleaner and faster web. It runs entirely on your device and reports nothing about the sites you visit. (The app's own free-tier ads are separate — see Plans & ads above.)

What is a private tab?

A private tab keeps no history, and its cookies and site data are held only in memory and cleared when you close your private tabs — so it leaves nothing behind on your device. Open one from the tab switcher or the browser menu. Private browsing keeps this activity off your device, but it doesn't make you anonymous to the sites you visit, your network, or your internet provider.

Can I choose my search engine?

Yes. In Settings you can pick Google, DuckDuckGo, or Bing as the engine used when you type a search into the address bar.

Do I need an account?

No. Surf has no account and no sign-up. If a website you visit requires a login, you log in to that site in the browser — those credentials stay on your device and are never sent to us.

How do I clear my browsing data?

Open Settings inside Surf and tap Clear Data to wipe your history, cookies, and cache. Bookmarks are kept unless you remove them.
