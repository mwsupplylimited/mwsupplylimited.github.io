About

Surf is a private web browser for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with a real file manager inside it. Browse with multiple tabs, block ads and trackers, read articles without the clutter, and keep your files in folders with tags and search — including a private area locked behind Touch ID or Face ID. A secure-connection indicator shows when a site is encrypted, and Clear Browsing Data wipes it whenever you want — after asking first, so it is never a slip. There is no account and no sign-up, and nothing you browse is sent to MW Supply Limited.

Surf keeps your browsing in your own hands. On the free plan your tabs, bookmarks, history, and Top Sites stay on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac; with Surf Pro they are kept in step across your devices through your own iCloud account. Either way MW Supply Limited runs no servers that receive them, there is no account to sign in to, and we cannot see any of it. Cookies and anything you type into a website are never synced, on any plan.

Getting started

  • Open a new tab and type a search term or a web address to start browsing.
  • Use the star to bookmark pages you want to keep, and organise them into folders.
  • Ad & tracker blocking is on by default for a cleaner, faster, more private web — adjust it any time in Settings.
  • Choose your search engine in Settings — Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Yahoo, Ecosia, Brave Search, Startpage, Mojeek, Perplexity, Wikipedia or YouTube — or paste your own.

Plans

Surf is free to download and use, and shows no advertising. The free plan is a complete browser: unlimited tabs, private tabs, ad & tracker blocking, bookmarks, history, Reader, and the file manager.

Surf Pro (US$4.99/month or US$29.99/year in the US storefront, with a 3-day free trial for eligible subscribers) keeps your bookmarks, history, reading list and open tabs in step across your devices through your own iCloud account. It also lifts the free plan's limits: unlimited saved articles instead of 5, no daily cap on the files you save, several at once instead of one at a time, and per-site settings that persist. On a Mac it also lets a compatible local assistant work in its own background tabs, or a tab you explicitly hand over, using the free Surf Connector. One subscription covers iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

On a Mac there is also a one-time purchase: buy Surf Pro once, with nothing to renew. Subscriptions are sold through Apple and renew automatically until cancelled; manage or cancel any time in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on iPhone or iPad, or App Store → your name → Account Settings on a Mac. Prices are always shown in the app in your local currency.

FAQ

Is my browsing kept private?

Surf stores browser data locally, with optional continuity through your own iCloud, and includes no developer analytics SDK. Websites and search engines receive the requests needed to browse. Saved sign-ins use your device keychain, with optional iCloud Keychain sync. If you connect an assistant on Mac, the page content it requests goes to that assistant’s provider. MW Supply Limited does not operate a service that receives your browsing history. See the privacy policy for these boundaries and data controls.

Does Surf block ads and trackers?

Yes, with an optional on-device content blocker that removes many ads and known trackers from the web pages you load, for a cleaner and faster web. It runs entirely on your device and reports nothing about the sites you visit. Surf itself shows no advertising on any plan.

What is a private tab?

A private tab keeps no history, and its cookies and site data are held only in memory and cleared when you close your private tabs — so it leaves nothing behind on your device. Open one from the tab switcher or the browser menu. Private browsing keeps this activity off your device, but it doesn't make you anonymous to the sites you visit, your network, or your internet provider.

Can I choose my search engine?

Yes. In Settings you can pick Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Yahoo, Ecosia, Brave Search, Startpage, Mojeek, Perplexity, Wikipedia or YouTube as the engine used when you type a search into the address bar, or paste the address of any search you use and Surf will use that.

Do I need an account?

No. Surf has no account and no sign-up. If a website you visit requires a login, you log in to that site in the browser — those credentials stay in your own hands — on your device, or in your own iCloud Keychain if you switch that on — and are never sent to us.

How do I clear my browsing data?

Open Settings inside Surf and choose Clear Browsing Data to wipe your history, cookies, and cache. Surf asks you to confirm first, because it signs you out of every site you are signed in to. Bookmarks are kept unless you remove them.

Can ChatGPT, Codex or Claude use Surf on my Mac?

Yes, with Surf Pro, the free Surf Connector, and your own compatible assistant software and account. The connection guide explains local ChatGPT desktop and Codex setup; these steps do not connect chatgpt.com. Surf 1.14 for Mac adds Settings › AI Assistants with ChatGPT & Codex, Claude, and other-client instructions. Earlier versions call this page Claude. From Mac build 138, AI Assistants remembers permission to read the selected connector across relaunches and distinguishes current and older connected sessions. If you selected the connector in an earlier build, use Verify Installed Connector… when prompted and choose the program already installed. This requests remembered read-only access; Surf only reads the selected file to verify it, and no reinstall is needed. A verified current connector shows Up to date — no update needed, with an optional Download Again action. The assistant works in tabs it opens or you explicitly hand over; private tabs stay excluded. Ordinary assistant tabs share your website sign-ins, and requested page content goes to your assistant’s provider. Turn access off with Automation › Stop Automation. Keep Surf open on the same Mac while using it. This feature is not available on iPhone or iPad.
