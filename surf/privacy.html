Surf Privacy Policy

Effective 11 July 2026 · Last updated 11 July 2026

Summary: Surf has no account and no first-party analytics, and MW Supply Limited runs no servers that receive your data. Your tabs, bookmarks, history, Top Sites, cookies, and anything you type into websites — including logins — stay on your device. The free tier shows ads through Google AdMob and asks App Tracking Transparency permission before an advertising identifier is used. Surf Pro removes all ads.

1. Information we collect

Surf has no account system, no login to us, and no first-party analytics, and MW Supply Limited operates no servers that receive your data. Your browsing data is stored locally in the app's own storage on your device and is not uploaded anywhere. The only data processing beyond your own device is done by the advertising provider on the free tier, described in section 5.

2. The browser & your browsing data

Surf is built on Apple's WebKit. The pages you visit, your tabs, bookmarks, history, Top Sites, cookies, cache, and anything you type — including usernames and passwords you enter to log in to a website — are handled by WebKit and stored only in the app's own storage on your device. This is used solely to load the pages you ask for and to remember your bookmarks, history, and Top Sites for you. It is never sent to MW Supply Limited, and we cannot see your browsing or your logins. You can clear it at any time in the app's Settings using Clear Data, which wipes your history, cookies, and cache.

3. Ad & tracker blocking

Surf offers an optional on-device content blocker that removes many ads and known trackers from the web pages you load. This runs entirely on your device using rule lists bundled with the app; it does not report the sites you visit to us or to anyone else. This is separate from the app's own advertising on the free tier, described below.

4. Subscriptions & payments

Surf Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple's In-App Purchase using StoreKit. Your purchase, payment, and renewals are handled entirely by Apple under your Apple Account; MW Supply Limited never sees your name, your Apple ID, or your card or payment details. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled any time in your Apple Account settings.

5. Advertising (free tier only)

The free tier of Surf shows advertising provided by Google AdMob. If you subscribe to Surf Pro, the app shows no ads at all and the AdMob SDK is not started.

  • What AdMob may process: a device advertising identifier, approximate (coarse) device information, IP address, and ad-interaction data (which ads were shown or tapped), used to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Your consent: the app asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission before any identifier is used for personalised ads, and shows a consent form where required (for example in the EEA/UK). If you decline, you still get ads — just non-personalised ones.
  • Managing your choices: you can change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • This advertising data is collected and processed by Google as an independent controller, not by MW Supply Limited. Google's practices are described in Google's privacy policy and Google's advertising policy.

6. Third-party websites & your responsibility

Surf is a general-purpose web browser and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any website you visit. The websites you load have their own privacy practices and terms, which are outside our control. You are responsible for complying with the terms of service of the sites you visit and with applicable law.

7. Data we do NOT collect

Surf does not collect or transmit your browsing history, bookmarks, tabs, Top Sites, or search terms; it has no account and asks for no personal information; and it performs no first-party analytics or crash tracking. The app requests no access to your photos, files, contacts, camera, microphone, or location. The only data that leaves your device is the AdMob advertising data described in section 5, and that only on the free tier.

8. Children

Surf is not directed to children under 13 and is age-rated accordingly in the App Store. The free tier shows ads through Google AdMob, and the app itself collects no personal data, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how Surf handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy labels for the affected version of the app.

10. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
