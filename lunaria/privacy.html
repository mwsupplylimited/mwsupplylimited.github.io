Lunaria Privacy Policy

Effective 7 June 2026 · Last updated 7 June 2026

Summary: Your cycle, symptom, mood, and intimacy data are sensitive health data, and they stay private. Lunaria stores them on your device — and, only if you turn it on, in your own end-to-end encrypted iCloud. We have no servers that hold your health data and no way to read it. We never sell your reproductive health data and never share it with advertisers or analytics services. The free version shows ads via Google AdMob, but the ad network never receives your health data. You can export or permanently delete everything at any time.

1. Who we are

Lunaria is developed by MW Supply Limited ("we", "us"). For questions about this policy or your data, contact mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.

2. The health data you enter

Lunaria is built around the information you choose to log: your periods and flow, symptoms, mood and energy, cervical fluid, basal body temperature, sexual activity and contraception, notes, and your cycle settings and goal. We treat all of this as sensitive health data.

This data is stored on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, it is also stored in your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, protected by end-to-end encryption, so it stays in sync across your devices. That data lives in your Apple Account; we cannot access it, and we never receive a copy. We do not operate our own servers that store your health data.

We do not sell your health data, and we never share it with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.

3. Health information & medical disclaimer

Lunaria is a general wellness and education product. It is not a medical device, does not provide a medical diagnosis, and is not a method of contraception. Its predictions are estimates and can be wrong. Lunaria does not replace professional medical care. See our Terms of Use for the full disclaimer.

4. Advertising (free version)

The free version of Lunaria shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, Google may process certain device and usage information (such as a device advertising identifier, general app activity, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interactions). This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We never send your cycle, symptom, mood, intimacy, or other health data to Google or any advertiser. Ad technology is kept strictly separate from your health data. On iOS, the app asks for permission before any tracking that uses the advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, ads still appear but are not personalised using that identifier. You can also limit ad personalisation in Settings → Privacy & Security on your device. Subscribing to Lunaria Plus removes all ads.

5. Subscriptions & purchases

Lunaria Plus subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your name, payment card, or billing details — Apple handles payment. We receive only the anonymous receipt information needed to confirm whether a subscription is active.

6. Partner sharing

Lunaria lets you choose to share specific cycle information with a partner. Sharing is always something you start, never automatic or hidden. You decide exactly which categories a partner can see, and you can change or revoke that access at any time with one tap. We never share your data with anyone without a specific action by you.

7. Notifications

Reminders (such as an upcoming-period reminder) are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. They are not sent through us or any third party.

8. Information we do not collect

We do not run an account system and do not collect your name, email, contacts, or photos. We do not collect analytics about how you use the app, and we do not build advertising or marketing profiles from your health data. Apart from the advertising identifier used by AdMob in the free version (Section 4), we do not track you.

9. Your choices & rights

  • Access & export: your data is on your device and available to you in the app.
  • Delete: use Me → Delete all data to permanently erase everything from your device and your iCloud. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data.
  • Consent: collecting your health data and sharing any of it are separate, optional choices, and you can withdraw consent at any time by turning off a feature or deleting your data.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (such as access, correction, deletion, and to withdraw consent) under laws like the GDPR or US state privacy laws. Because your health data lives on your own device and iCloud, you can exercise most of these yourself in the app; contact us for help.

10. Consumer Health Data (Washington "My Health My Data" and similar laws)

For users covered by consumer-health-data laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act, this section serves as our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy:

  • What we collect: only the reproductive- and cycle-related health data you choose to enter, stored on your device and (optionally) your end-to-end encrypted iCloud.
  • Sources: directly from you.
  • How it's used: solely to provide the app's features to you on your device. We do not use it for advertising.
  • Sharing: we do not share your consumer health data with third parties, except a partner you explicitly choose to share specific data with. We do not collect it on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to disclose to others.
  • Selling: we do not and will not sell your reproductive or other consumer health data.
  • Your rights: you may access, delete, and withdraw consent for this data at any time in the app, and may contact us to exercise these rights or appeal a decision.
  • We do not use geofencing around any healthcare facility.

11. Law enforcement & legal requests

Because your health data is stored on your device and in your own end-to-end encrypted iCloud rather than on our servers, we do not hold a copy that we could disclose, and we cannot read your data. We have designed Lunaria to minimise the data we hold so that there is as little as possible to ever be requested. For an extra layer of protection you can lock the app with Face ID.

12. Children

Lunaria is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Adolescents who menstruate may use Lunaria; the same on-device, private handling applies to everyone.

13. Data retention & security

Because we do not collect your health data on any server we control, we have nothing to retain or delete on our side. Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud and can be removed at any time. On-device data is protected by your device passcode and optional Face ID lock; iCloud sync is end-to-end encrypted.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change how Lunaria handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above, and reflect material changes in the App Store privacy details for the affected version.

15. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
