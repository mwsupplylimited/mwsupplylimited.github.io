Ironreach Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

In short: Ironreach does not collect, store, or sell your personal information itself. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics SDKs. The game is free with ads: on the free plan a third-party advertising provider (Google AdMob) shows ads on the menus and may collect device and ad-interaction data as described below. The optional Ironreach Pro subscription removes all ads. If you enable iCloud, your settings and progress sync through Apple's iCloud, which we cannot read.

Information we do not collect

Ironreach has no user accounts and no servers that store your data. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, browsing history, or any analytics about how you play. The one exception is the third-party advertising provider on the free plan (see “Advertising” below), which collects data under Google’s control — not ours — to deliver ads.

Data stored on your device

Your game saves, match progress, and app settings (such as your chosen options and appearance) are stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud enabled and are signed in to your Apple ID, your settings and progression may sync across your devices through Apple’s iCloud. That data is stored in your private iCloud container under your Apple ID and is not accessible to us; its handling is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. Large match save files stay on your device only.

Subscriptions

Ironreach Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Payments and billing are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app checks your subscription status through Apple’s StoreKit to decide whether to show Pro features and hide ads. Manage or cancel any time in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Advertising (free plan) and App Tracking Transparency

On the free plan, Ironreach shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) — a banner shown only on the app’s menus, never during a match. To select, deliver, measure and cap the frequency of these ads and to prevent fraud, the Google SDK may collect device and usage information — including a device advertising identifier (IDFA, only with your consent), an IP address (and the coarse region derived from it), and information about your interaction with the ads.

Before any tracking, iOS shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt asking your permission. If you decline, the app requests non-personalized ads only. You can change your choice any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

This advertising data is collected and processed by Google as an independent controller, not by MW Supply Limited. We never receive it. Google’s handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Ironreach Pro removes all ads and the ad SDK is not started for subscribers.

Children

Ironreach is not directed at children. The app itself does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Ads on the free plan are served by Google; when tracking permission has not been granted, the app requests non-personalized ads only.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.
