Blackout Camera Privacy Policy

Effective 31 May 2026 · Last updated 31 May 2026

Summary: Blackout Camera collects no personal data, contains no advertising, uses no analytics or third-party SDKs, and has no accounts. Everything happens on your device. The only thing it sends anywhere is the photos and videos you capture, saved into your own photo library.

1. Information we collect

None. Blackout Camera does not ask for, collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information. There is no account system, no login, no user profile, and no usage tracking.

2. Camera and microphone

The app uses your device's camera to show a live preview and to take photos and record video, and uses the microphone to record sound with your videos. The camera and microphone feeds are processed entirely on your device for the sole purpose of capturing the media you choose to capture. No image, video, or audio is ever sent to MW Supply Limited or any third party.

3. Your photos and videos

When you take a photo or record a video, the app saves it to your device's photo library using iOS's “Add Only” Photos authorization. This permission lets the app add your new captures to your library but does not allow it to read, browse, or access any of your existing photos and videos. Captured media is stored in your own photo library, under your control. If you have iCloud Photos enabled, your library is backed up by Apple under your Apple ID according to Apple's own privacy practices — the app plays no part in that.

4. Network activity

Blackout Camera makes no network requests. It has no servers, sends no telemetry, and does not require an internet connection to work. Your captures and settings never leave your device except when you yourself save, share, or sync them through iOS.

5. On-device settings

Your preferences (such as auto-blackout, night-vision mode, grid, and video quality) are stored locally on your device using the standard iOS preferences system. They are not transmitted anywhere.

6. Third-party services

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics frameworks, no advertising networks, and no crash-reporting services other than Apple's own first-party crash reporting (which only runs if you have opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

7. Children

Blackout Camera collects no data of any kind, so it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how Blackout Camera handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy labels for the affected version of the app.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
