Dashward Privacy Policy

Effective 21 June 2026 · Last updated 1 October 2026

Summary: Dashward records on your device and keeps your recordings there unless you turn on a feature that sends something elsewhere. Every such feature is listed below, is off until you choose it, and goes through Apple’s iCloud (under your own Apple Account) or Apple Photos — MW Supply Limited cannot see your recordings, location or pictures. The one server it runs, the Remote Viewing live-video relay (section 6), only passes on video that is encrypted on the vehicle and that it cannot decrypt. The free tier shows ads from Unity LevelPlay.

1. What leaves your device, and when

Dashward has no account system, no login and no first-party analytics. Data leaves your device only in these cases:

  • Save to Photos — clips you save (or that the app saves because you turned on “Save important clips to Photos”) go to your Apple Photos library.
  • Evidence vault (Cloud Backup, Pro) — locked clips are uploaded to your own private iCloud database.
  • Alerts to your other devices (Pro) — when the parking sentry records an event, the short event clip and a description are sent through your private iCloud database to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Live View of your own devices (Pro) — if you turn on “Allow live view of this device”, still pictures from its cameras and its storage and temperature status are sent through your private iCloud database while one of your other devices is watching.
  • Sharing sentry clips with another person (Pro) — if you invite someone, parking-sentry event clips (which can include audio and, if “Stamp location on clips” is on, location) are shared with them through iCloud sharing.
  • Remote Viewing (sharing is free; viewing needs Dashward Pro or Business Pro) — if you share a vehicle, the information you choose, including live video if you allow it, is sent to the people you invite. See section 6.
  • Advertising (free tier) — see section 8.

Dashward for Mac is a viewer for vehicles shared with you. It has no camera, microphone or location access and records nothing; it only reads the Remote Viewing updates described in section 6 and the subscription status from the App Store.

2. Camera & microphone

Dashward uses the front and/or rear camera to record your drive and the microphone to record sound with your clips. Recording is processed on your device. Pictures or clips leave the device only through the features you turn on in section 1. MW Supply Limited never receives images or audio, and never receives video it can see: Remote Viewing’s live video passes through its relay encrypted (section 6).

3. Location

If you allow location, Dashward embeds GPS position and speed in the clips you record, shows them on screen, and computes today’s driving summary (distance, time, harsh braking, hard acceleration, sharp turns and time over a speed you set) on your device. Location leaves the device only inside clips you save or share, or through Remote Viewing if you turn on “Location, speed and today’s driving” (section 6). Dashward asks only for location “While Using the App” and never tracks you in the background. You can turn location off in the app’s Settings or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

4. On-device storage & automatic deletion

Clips are stored in the app’s own storage on your device. Dashward loop-records into segments and keeps within a storage limit you choose, deleting the oldest unlocked clips to make room. Locked clips and parking-sentry event clips are kept until you delete them. Today’s driving summary is kept on the device and replaced at midnight. Preferences are stored locally.

5. iCloud (CloudKit)

The iCloud features in section 1 use Apple’s CloudKit under your Apple Account and are governed by Apple’s privacy policy. Data in your private iCloud database, and data you share with invited people, is not accessible to MW Supply Limited. Clips alerted to your other devices are removed from iCloud after 14 days; evidence-vault clips stay until you remove them. You can turn iCloud off for Dashward in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

6. Remote Viewing

Remote Viewing lets the people you invite see a vehicle in which Dashward is running. It is off until the person setting up the vehicle turns it on and invites people by their Apple Account email address or phone number.

  • What can be shared (each is chosen separately and is off by default): live location, speed, heading, today’s route and driving summary and events; pictures from the road camera; live video from the road camera and from the cabin camera, sent only while someone is watching live (if “Allow live video” is on; the cabin camera shows the inside of the vehicle, including the driver and any passengers); and whether the vehicle is recording, its battery level and its temperature. If a possible crash is detected, the people viewing the vehicle are sent an alert, which can include the location.
  • Who can see it: only the people the vehicle’s iCloud account invites and who accept (on iPhone, iPad or Mac), and that account’s own devices if “Also show on my other devices” is on. Invitations are private to the people named and can’t be forwarded.
  • How it travels: the updates are encrypted on the vehicle’s device with a key that only the vehicle and the invited people hold (the key travels inside the iCloud share), and are relayed through Dashward’s CloudKit public database operated by Apple. MW Supply Limited does not hold the key and cannot read the updates. CloudKit does record that an encrypted update exists, when it was last changed, and an anonymous iCloud identifier of the account that wrote it. Live video is encrypted on the vehicle’s device with the same key and passed through a relay server that MW Supply Limited operates on Cloudflare (dashward-live.mwsupplylimited.workers.dev). The relay forwards the encrypted video to the invited people who are watching, cannot decrypt it, and does not store it. So that only subscribers can use the relay, a viewer’s device sends it the Apple-signed record of its Dashward subscription (which contains no name, email or Apple Account), which the relay checks. For a Business Pro team this can be the team owner’s subscription record. The relay temporarily holds a subscription identifier and network address information to limit concurrent viewers and prevent abuse; it keeps no request logs. Subscription-renewal responses can be cached in memory for up to five minutes, as described below. Cloudflare processes connection data such as IP addresses to run the service.
  • Only while Dashward is open: nothing is sent while Dashward is in the background or closed.
  • The person in the vehicle is told: the vehicle’s screen shows a notice when sharing starts and each day, and an indicator whenever sharing is on or someone is watching live, from which sharing can be paused at any time.
  • Retention: each update replaces the previous one; live video is never stored; the route is replaced each day; crash alerts are deleted after 24 hours; stopping sharing (or removing a person) deletes the shared data and replaces the key, so former viewers can no longer read anything new. A viewer can remove a vehicle at any time, which deletes it from their device.
  • Your responsibility: if you set up Remote Viewing on a vehicle that someone else drives or rides in, you are responsible for telling them and for following the laws that apply to you about recording, location sharing and workplace monitoring.

Business Pro teams (from version 1.4)

A Business Pro subscriber can create a team and invite people through iCloud sharing using their Apple Account email address or phone number. People who accept can view the vehicles the subscriber includes, on their own iPhone, iPad or Mac. Each vehicle’s driver must acknowledge the updated sharing notice before those team members can view it.

  • Inside the team’s iCloud share: the team name; the subscriber’s and members’ iCloud display names and anonymous iCloud identifiers; and each included vehicle’s key. Only the subscriber and the invited people in the team can open this share.
  • What the vehicle receives: the team’s name and its people’s names, encrypted with the vehicle’s key in Dashward’s CloudKit public database. The vehicle uses these to show its driver who can view it.
  • Checking the subscription: the team holds the subscriber’s Apple-signed Business Pro subscription record. It contains subscription identifiers, product and date information, but no name, email, Apple Account or payment details. A member’s app can send it to the relay, which asks Apple’s App Store Server API for the latest subscription state and returns Apple’s signed answer. Renewal responses are cached in memory for up to five minutes and are not written to a database or request log.
  • Leaving or removing someone: members can leave and the subscriber can remove them. The affected vehicles replace their keys when they process the change, so former members cannot read subsequent updates. Keys also change when a vehicle is removed from the team or the team is stopped.
  • Seats: Business Pro includes a number of people. Optional Team Seats subscriptions add people to that team; they do not change what a vehicle shares.

Crash detection is best-effort and does not replace emergency services.

7. Subscriptions & payments

Dashward Pro and Dashward Business Pro are auto-renewable subscriptions sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase and shared by your iPhone, iPad and Mac under one Apple Account. Business Pro also covers invited team members for the team’s vehicles. Optional Team Seats packs are separate auto-renewable subscriptions and require an active Business Pro plan. Purchases and renewals are handled by Apple under your Apple Account; MW Supply Limited never sees your name, Apple Account or payment details. Manage or cancel any time in your Apple Account settings.

8. Advertising (free tier only)

The free tier shows advertising served through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource, a Unity Technologies company) and the ad networks it mediates. Dashward Pro shows no ads and the ad SDK is not started.

  • What these partners may process: a device advertising identifier, coarse device information, IP address and ad-interaction data, used to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Your consent: the app asks for Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission before any identifier is used for personalised ads, and has a personalised-ads switch in Settings. If you decline, ads are not personalised.
  • Your choices: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and the personalised-ads switch in the app’s Settings.

This advertising data is processed by Unity Technologies (ironSource) and its mediated networks as independent controllers, not by MW Supply Limited. Unity’s practices are described in Unity’s privacy policy.

9. Children

Dashward is not directed to children under 13. MW Supply Limited collects no personal data itself.

10. Deleting your data

Delete clips in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on the device. Evidence-vault clips can be removed in the app or with iCloud storage management. To stop Remote Viewing, use “Stop sharing with everyone” in Settings → Remote Viewing on the vehicle; a viewer can remove a vehicle from its Vehicles screen.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how Dashward handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date, and the App Store privacy details for the affected version.
