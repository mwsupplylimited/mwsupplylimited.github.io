Check your setup before you subscribe

  • Software: iOS or iPadOS 17 or later for recording; macOS 14 or later for the Mac viewer. Sign in to iCloud with your Apple Account for sharing.
  • Cameras: simultaneous Road and Cabin capture needs a compatible camera combination. Pro or Pro Max branding alone does not establish compatibility; test the free previews on the actual device.
  • Power, mounting and data: use a secure mount that leaves both cameras clear, a charger and a suitable data allowance. Keep the phone shaded and ventilated.
  • Foreground use: recording and Parking Sentry require Dashward to stay open on the recording device. Heat, battery, storage or connection problems can interrupt capture or viewing.

Common questions

What do the paid capture tools add?

Pro adds single-camera recording up to 4K/60fps, configurable Parking Sentry, timelapse, driving reports, highlights and ad-free capture. Dual recording supports up to 1080p per camera, subject to hardware and heat limits. Account-specific private iCloud backup and SOS need the dashcam's own eligible subscription.

Do all devices need a subscription?

No. Basic recording and sharing are free. A paid plan supplies monitoring capacity; approved Business teammates and sources can use the owner's applicable coverage. Connecting a device alone does not enable a paid plan or start sharing.

Can I leave it recording with the screen locked?

No. Keep Dashward open in the foreground. Optional auto-dimming lowers screen brightness and wakes on touch or a detected incident. It does not remove camera, heat or battery limits.

Will recording ever fill my storage?

Loop recording removes the oldest unlocked clips up to your configured storage limit. Locked clips remain protected and can fill storage, stopping recording. Export important footage and remove unneeded clips.

Are video, maps and events synchronized?

Live video, snapshots and vehicle data arrive separately. Check each freshness label. Event links open related clips when available; remote transfers need the source online.

Something is not working. What should I do?

Open our troubleshooting guide, including crash-report steps. Do not buy another plan or delete the app just to try to fix a connection; deleting it can remove local recordings.

Original Personal subscriptions and earlier versions

The original Personal monthly and yearly subscriptions closed on 6 October 2026 and will not renew. Already-paid access remains available through its paid term; there is no automatic move to a new plan. Check your expiry in Apple's subscription settings.

Version 1.1's same-account Live View used near-live snapshots rather than the 1.4 remote-video setup. Update both devices to 1.4 for the current connection guide, or contact support if you need help with an earlier version.
