Dashward

A dash cam that watches the road — and your car. Version 1.3

About

Dashward turns your iPhone into an always-on dash cam: it records the rear and/or front camera — or both the front and rear cameras at the same time, free — loop-records into short segments, automatically manages storage, and stamps every clip with your GPS location. With Remote Viewing, the people you choose can check on the vehicle from their own iPhone, iPad or Mac. Dashward Pro adds up to 4K/60fps, a parking sentry that keeps watching your car while you're away, Emergency SOS, Driving Reports, and a cloud evidence vault for your incident clips.

Getting started

  • Mount the phone securely on your windshield or dash, with the camera you want to record facing the road.
  • Connect to power so the phone charges while it records.
  • Tap record to start. Dashward can keep the screen awake and run for the whole drive while it's mounted.
  • Keep the phone in a shaded, cool spot to avoid overheating — direct sun on a windshield can get very hot.

Recording & storage

Dashward records continuously using loop recording: the drive is broken into short, fixed-length segments so clips stay easy to find, save, and share. You set a storage cap, and once it's reached Dashward automatically deletes the oldest clips to make room for new ones, so it can run indefinitely without filling up your phone.

You can lock any clip you want to keep — locked clips (and event clips from the parking sentry) are protected from the automatic deletion and stay until you remove them yourself. You can also save clips to Apple Photos (free), or with Pro back locked clips up to your own private iCloud evidence vault.

Parking sentry Pro

When you park and leave the phone mounted, Dashward Pro can switch into parking sentry mode and keep an eye on your car:

  • Detects motion or impact around the vehicle and automatically saves and locks an event clip so it won't be overwritten.
  • Alerts you on the device when you come back, so you can see what happened while you were away.
  • Notifies your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account, so you can get a heads-up even before you reach the car.
  • An optional timelapse shows the whole time you were away.

Remote Viewing New in 1.3

Remote Viewing lets the people you choose see a vehicle while Dashward is running in it — for families, or for a business keeping an eye on its fleet. Sharing a vehicle is free. Each person who views needs Dashward Pro (one vehicle) or Business Pro (5, 25 or 100 vehicles).

Share a vehicle (on the iPhone or iPad mounted in it)

  • Open Settings → Remote Viewing, name the vehicle and choose what to share: location, speed and today's driving; the road camera (pictures while moving, and live video while someone watches); the cabin camera (live video, only while someone watches); and whether to allow live video at all.
  • Tap Invite People and add each person by the email address or phone number of their Apple Account. You can remove anyone later from the same sheet.
  • Using one Apple Account on all your devices? Turn on Also show on my other devices instead — no invitation needed.
  • Before anything is shared, the driver sees a notice listing what's shared and with whom — again each day and whenever that changes. While sharing, an indicator shows on screen (it reads LIVE VIDEO while someone watches), and tapping it lets the driver pause at any time.

View a vehicle (on iPhone, iPad or Mac)

  • Open the invitation. Dashward opens and adds the vehicle to Vehicles. On a Mac, use Dashward for Mac from the Mac App Store — the same subscription works there.
  • Open a vehicle to see where it is, its speed, today's route and harsh braking, acceleration and turns, and live video. A live view lasts 5 minutes; tap Keep live to continue.
  • If a vehicle detects a hard impact, you get a crash alert. It's best-effort — it needs Dashward open and online in the vehicle — and it doesn't replace emergency services.
  • Not sure yet? See a sample vehicle on the Vehicles screen — no account or subscription needed.

Privacy

Updates are encrypted on the vehicle's phone with a key only the vehicle and the people it's shared with hold. Live video passes through Dashward's relay server, which can't see it and stores nothing. Sharing works only while Dashward is open in the vehicle. Stop sharing any time with Stop sharing with everyone in Settings → Remote Viewing. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Does it record in the background?

iOS only allows camera recording while the app is open, so Dashward can't record from the background like a built-in dash cam. Instead, Dashward keeps the screen awake so it records for the whole drive while it's mounted. To save power and reduce heat, you can dim the screen independently from the iOS brightness — the camera keeps recording at full quality while the display stays dark. Remote Viewing likewise works only while Dashward is open in the vehicle.

How is storage managed?

Dashward loop-records into short segments and enforces a storage cap you choose. When the cap is reached it automatically deletes the oldest clips to make room, so it never fills up your phone. Clips you lock, and event clips saved by the parking sentry, are kept safe from automatic deletion.

Will it overheat my phone?

Dashward monitors your device's temperature and warns you if it's getting too hot. To cool things down it can automatically lower the frame rate or resolution, and live video for Remote Viewing drops to a lighter stream, then pauses. The best fix is physical: keep the phone in airflow and out of direct sun — a shaded spot near a vent stays much cooler than a phone baking on the dash.

A vehicle I view says "No signal".

The vehicle's phone hasn't sent an update when it should have — usually no mobile signal, or Dashward was closed on it. Updates resume on their own when it's back online with Dashward open.
