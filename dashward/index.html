About

Dashward turns your iPhone into an always-on dash cam: it records the rear and/or front camera — or both the front and rear cameras at the same time, free — loop-records into short segments, automatically manages storage, and stamps every clip with your GPS location. During an existing paid term, Dashward Pro includes up to 4K/60fps, a parking sentry while Dashward stays open on the mounted phone, Emergency SOS, Driving Reports, and a cloud evidence vault for your incident clips.

Getting started

  • Mount the phone securely on your windshield or dash, with the camera you want to record facing the road.
  • Connect to power so the phone charges while it records.
  • Tap record to start. Dashward can keep the screen awake and run for the whole drive while it's mounted.
  • Keep the phone in a shaded, cool spot to avoid overheating — direct sun on a windshield can get very hot.

Recording & storage

Dashward records continuously using loop recording: the drive is broken into short, fixed-length segments so clips stay easy to find, save, and share. You set a storage cap, and Dashward deletes the oldest unlocked clips to make room. Locked clips are protected, so storage can still fill up and recording can stop. Export clips you want to keep, then remove unneeded clips from Dashward to free space.

You can lock any clip you want to keep — locked clips (and event clips from the parking sentry) are protected from the automatic deletion and stay until you remove them yourself. You can also save clips to Apple Photos (free), or with Pro back locked clips up to your own private iCloud evidence vault.

Parking sentry Pro

When you park and leave the phone mounted, Dashward Pro can switch into parking sentry mode and keep an eye on your car:

  • Detects motion or impact around the vehicle and automatically saves and locks an event clip so it won't be overwritten.
  • Alerts you on the device when you come back, so you can see what happened while you were away.
  • Notifies your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account, so you can get a heads-up even before you reach the car.
  • An optional timelapse shows the whole time you were away.

Live View on your own devices Pro

Version 1.1 lets existing Pro subscribers see near-live camera snapshots from another of their devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Snapshots and remote commands use your private iCloud database. Keep both devices online and Dashward open on the mounted device.

  • On the device you want to watch, open Settings → Live View and turn on Allow live view of this device.
  • On your other device, open Settings → Live View → View & control another device, then select the device.
  • Choose the front or rear camera when available. The view also shows recording and sentry status, storage, temperature and recent sentry events.
  • You can remotely start or stop recording and arm or disarm the sentry. Synced sentry clips can be played from the event list; syncing needs Alert my other devices enabled on the recording device.
  • To stop allowing this device to be watched, turn off Allow live view of this device on it.

Updates depend on the devices and iCloud connection. A recent snapshot is not a guarantee of current conditions; Live View and alerts do not replace emergency services.

FAQ

Does it record in the background?

iOS only allows camera recording while the app is open, so Dashward can't record from the background like a built-in dash cam. Instead, Dashward keeps the screen awake so it records for the whole drive while it's mounted. To save power and reduce heat, you can dim the screen independently from the iOS brightness — the camera keeps recording at full quality while the display stays dark. Live View likewise needs Dashward open on the device being watched.

How is storage managed?

Dashward loop-records into short segments and enforces a storage cap you choose. It deletes the oldest unlocked clips when space is needed. Clips you lock, and event clips saved by the parking sentry, are protected from automatic deletion. If protected clips fill the available space, recording can stop. Export important clips and remove unneeded clips yourself.

Will it overheat my phone?

Dashward monitors your device's temperature and warns you if it's getting too hot. To cool things down it can automatically lower the recording frame rate or resolution. The best fix is physical: keep the phone in airflow and out of direct sun — a shaded spot near a vent stays much cooler than a phone baking on the dash.

Live View is waiting for a camera.

Check that both devices are online and signed in to the same Apple Account for iCloud. The device being watched needs Dashward open and Allow live view of this device enabled. An old snapshot may remain visible when updates stop; check its age before relying on it.
