Days Counter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

In short: Days Counter is built privacy-first. It contains no advertising, no third-party SDKs and no tracking of any kind. The events you create stay on your device and, if you enable it, sync only through your own private iCloud account. We (mwsupplylimited) do not collect, see, store on our own servers, or sell your data. There are no accounts and no login.

Information you enter

The events you create — titles, dates, notes, categories, chosen colours and any photo you attach — are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device database, in a shared container so the app's widgets can display them. If you enable iCloud (a Pro feature), this data is also stored in your private iCloud database so it syncs across your devices. It is governed by Apple's privacy policy and is not accessible to us.

Information we collect

None directly. Days Counter does not require an account, has no servers of its own, and does not transmit your events to us.

Advertising and tracking

There is none. Days Counter contains no advertising, no advertising SDK and no third-party SDK of any kind. It does not read your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, and does not link anything about you with data from other companies for advertising or share anything with a data broker. Because the app does not track, it never presents a tracking permission prompt.

Earlier versions of Days Counter (1.1 and before) showed a banner ad through Google AdMob to users on the free tier. That was removed entirely in version 1.2 — the ad SDK is no longer part of the app.

Subscriptions

Days Counter Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and manages the subscription; we never receive your payment details. The app only learns whether your device currently has an active Pro entitlement.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No reminder content is sent to us or any third party.

Diagnostics

Days Counter does not include third-party analytics or crash-tracking SDKs. Apple may provide us anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics if you have opted in to share them with developers in iOS Settings; these contain no personal data.

Children

Days Counter is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your control

You can edit, archive or delete any event at any time, and deleting the app removes its on-device data. To remove iCloud copies, sign out of iCloud or delete the app's data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
