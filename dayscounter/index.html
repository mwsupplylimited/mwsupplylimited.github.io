What it does

Days Counter tracks how many days are left until an upcoming event, or how many days have passed since a moment that matters. Trips, weddings, birthdays, exams, retirement, a due date, a sober streak, an anniversary — each becomes a beautiful card you can pin to your Home and Lock Screen.

Features

  • Count down & count up — “days until” and “days since”, with real time-of-day precision.
  • Flexible units — show days, weeks, months, years, or a full breakdown.
  • Recurring events — birthdays and anniversaries roll forward automatically every year.
  • Beautiful themes — a curated gallery of gradients and solids, your own photos (Pro), emoji and symbol icons.
  • Home & Lock Screen widgets — every size, self-updating, one glance away.
  • Reminders — get a nudge on the day, and milestone reminders (100 days, 1 year, 1000 days…) with Pro.
  • Share cards — turn any countdown into a shareable image.
  • Celebration — a little confetti when the big day arrives, then it flips to counting up.
  • iCloud sync — your events on all your devices (Pro).
  • Dark mode, iPhone & iPad.

Free & Pro

Days Counter is free to use with up to 3 active countdowns and an ad on the list screen. Days Counter Pro unlocks unlimited countdowns, per-event photo backgrounds, every widget, iCloud sync, milestone reminders, and removes ads.

Pro is available as a Monthly or Annual subscription (the Annual plan includes a 7-day free trial). Subscriptions are billed to your Apple Account and renew automatically until cancelled; you can manage or cancel anytime in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Payment is charged at confirmation of purchase; a free trial converts to the paid plan unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends.

Frequently asked

How is a “day” counted?
By whole calendar days between today and the event date, so the number always matches your calendar — no off-by-one surprises across time zones.

The widget isn't updating.
Widgets refresh at the start of each day. Make sure the app has run at least once and that Background App Refresh is on. Removing and re-adding the widget forces a refresh.

I don't see my subscription's benefits.
Open the app → Settings → tap Restore purchases.

Will archiving an event delete it?
No. Archived events are kept and don't count toward the free limit — you can restore them anytime from Settings → Archived events.
