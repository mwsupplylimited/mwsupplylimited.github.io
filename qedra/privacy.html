Qedra Privacy Policy

Effective 19 August 2026 · Last updated 19 August 2026

Summary: the mathematics you type never leaves your device. Qedra’s solver is a complete computer algebra system built into the app, so there is no server to send a problem to — it works with the phone in aeroplane mode. There are no Qedra accounts and no login. If you use the free tier, a third-party network supplies banner ads and, only with your permission, may use your device’s advertising identifier. Qedra Pro removes ads entirely.

1. Who we are

Qedra is published by MW Supply Limited (“we”, “us”). We do not operate any servers of our own and we do not run any account system. There is no Qedra login, and there is nothing to delete from our systems because we hold nothing.

2. Your mathematics stays on your device

This is the part most people want to know, so it comes first. Every problem you enter is parsed, solved, verified and explained entirely on your device. The whole computer algebra system — the exact arithmetic, the simplifier, the calculus and linear algebra engines, the step recorder — is compiled into the app. Nothing about your problem is transmitted anywhere for solving, not to us and not to anyone else. You can confirm this yourself: turn on Airplane Mode and Qedra still solves.

3. Information we collect

None that reaches us. We have no servers, no database, and no analytics of our own. We cannot see the problems you solve, your history, or how you use the app. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, or location.

4. iCloud history (optional)

Qedra can keep a history of the problems you have solved and sync it between your own devices using Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit).

  • It is your iCloud, not ours. The history is stored in the private CloudKit database tied to your Apple Account. We have no access to it and receive nothing from it.
  • It is not shared. Qedra does not use CloudKit sharing, so no part of your history is made available to another person.
  • This data is handled under Apple’s Privacy Policy and your iCloud terms. Deleting an item in the app, or turning off iCloud for Qedra in iOS Settings, removes it from your private iCloud.

5. Advertising on the free tier

Qedra’s free tier is supported by banner advertising supplied by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), a mediation service that may in turn request an advert from other advertising networks. Qedra Pro subscribers and Lifetime owners see no advertising at all, and for them the advertising software is never started.

  • What the ad network can receive. Coarse, non-identifying technical information needed to select and display an advert — for example device type, operating system version, screen size, coarse locale and country, and a count of adverts shown. Qedra never passes it anything you type.
  • The advertising identifier, only with your permission. On first launch iOS asks whether Qedra may track you across apps and websites. If you say no — or ignore it — the advertising identifier is unavailable and the adverts you see are not personalised to you. If you say yes, the identifier may be used to measure and personalise advertising. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising is only served to App Store installs. Qedra checks its App Store receipt at runtime and will not start the advertising software at all on a simulator, a development build, or a TestFlight build.
  • Unity’s handling of what it receives is governed by the Unity Player and App User Privacy Policy.

6. Purchases

Qedra Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription, and as a one-time Lifetime purchase, through the App Store. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. Your entitlement is read from the App Store on your device and is tied to your Apple Account, not to any Qedra account. Apple may share aggregate, non-identifying sales information with us.

8. Third-party software

The only third-party software in Qedra is the Unity LevelPlay advertising SDK described above, and it is not started for Pro users or outside the App Store. The mathematics engine has no third-party dependencies of any kind. There is no Google advertising software in this app. Apple’s own crash reporting runs only if you have opted in under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, and what it collects goes to Apple.

9. Permissions

Qedra requests the App Tracking Transparency permission described in section 5, and nothing else. It does not request your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, or health data.

10. Children

Qedra is designed for university-level mathematics and is not directed to children. It is not registered as a child-directed app with any advertising network. We collect no personal data ourselves, consistent with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

11. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or erase. Data held by Apple on your behalf (your iCloud history, your purchase records) is subject to Apple’s own privacy controls, and you can manage it in iOS Settings. Advertising data is subject to Unity’s policy, and the tracking permission in section 5 is the control that governs it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how Qedra handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy labels for the affected version.

13. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
