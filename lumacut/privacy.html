Lumacut Privacy Policy

Effective 29 May 2026 · Last updated 29 May 2026

Summary: Lumacut does not collect personal data, contains no advertising, and uses no analytics or third-party SDKs. Your videos are edited entirely on your device, and the only optional cloud feature stores projects in your own private iCloud.

1. Information we collect

None. Lumacut does not ask for, collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information. The app has no account system, no login, and no user profile.

2. Your videos and photos

The app reads videos and writes media with the minimum permissions iOS provides:

  • Importing a video uses Apple's system photo picker (PHPicker). The app receives only the clips you explicitly select and has no other access to your photo library.
  • Saving an export uses iOS's "Add Only" Photos authorization. The app can add your finished video to your library but cannot read existing photos.
  • Imported clips are copied into the app's own project storage on your device so they can be edited, and remain entirely under your control.

3. On-device processing

All editing — trimming, speed, color, pixelation — and all face and object tracking is performed locally on your device. Face detection and object tracking use Apple's on-device Vision framework. No frame of your video is ever sent off the device for processing.

4. iCloud project sync (Pro feature)

If you subscribe to Pro and enable iCloud sync, your projects (including imported clips and edit settings) are stored in Lumacut's private container in your iCloud, under your Apple ID. This lets you back up projects and continue them on your other devices. MW Supply Limited has no access to, and never receives a copy of, the contents of your iCloud storage. iCloud sync follows Apple's own privacy practices. If you leave sync off, everything stays in local on-device storage.

5. Subscriptions

Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription via Apple's In-App Purchase. All purchase and payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We receive aggregated, anonymous sales reports from App Store Connect; we do not receive your name, email address, billing details, or any personally-identifying information about subscribers.

6. Network activity

Lumacut makes no network requests to servers owned by MW Supply Limited. The only network activity the app participates in is Apple-operated traffic for the Photos picker, StoreKit (subscription verification), and iCloud (Pro project sync). All of that traffic stays between your device and Apple.

7. Third-party services

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics frameworks, no advertising networks, and no crash reporting services other than Apple's own first-party crash reporting (which only runs if you have opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

8. Children

Lumacut collects no data of any kind, so it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how Lumacut handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy nutrition labels for the affected version of the app.

10. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
