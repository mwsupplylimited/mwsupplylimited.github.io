A Splitwise alternative for iPhone & iPad

If you're looking for a simpler way to split group bills — without making everyone sign up — Spliddy is built around three ideas: no account, no limits, and everything's free.

Download Spliddy on the App Store Free on iPhone & iPad · No sign-up

Why people switch to Spliddy

  • No account for anyone in the group. You create a trip and share an 8-character code; friends join from their own iPhone — no email, no password, no sign-up. Many bill-splitters require every person to create an account first; Spliddy doesn't.
  • No daily limit on expenses. Log as many expenses as you like — rent, dinners, a whole trip — with no cap on the free version.
  • Every split feature is free. Equal, exact, percentage and shares splits, split-by-item, multi-currency, recurring bills, receipt photos and insights are all free for everyone. Spliddy Premium ($11.99/year) only removes ads and adds CSV export — it never gates a split feature.
  • Settle up in the fewest payments. Spliddy works out the simplest set of transfers to clear every debt, and can open Venmo, PayPal or Cash App pre-filled so you pay your friend directly.
  • Your data stays in your iCloud. There's no Spliddy account because there's no Spliddy server holding your trips — a group syncs through Apple's iCloud, keyed by the invite code.

How it works in 30 seconds

  • Create a trip and give it a name and currency.
  • Share the 8-character code — friends join from their own phone in seconds.
  • Everyone adds what they paid; pick how to split each expense.
  • Open Balances to see who owes who and the fewest payments to settle up.
