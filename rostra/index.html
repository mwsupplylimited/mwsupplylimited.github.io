Rostra — Support

The business manager for solo instructors. Track your students and classes, take attendance, and know exactly what each student owes — this month and next — and which classes actually make a profit.

Who it's for

Dance & ballet teachers, personal trainers, sports coaches, music teachers and private tutors who run their own classes. Rostra adapts its wording to what you teach (students or clients, classes or sessions).

Features

  • Students & classes — profiles, group classes and 1-on-1s, with each person's balance at a glance.
  • Recurring schedules — set a class once (e.g. every Tuesday 5 PM) and Rostra fills in every session.
  • Attendance & a fair cancellation policy — mark a whole class present in one tap; students who cancel in time aren't charged, late cancels and no-shows are — all automatic.
  • Fees — per student or per class, per-session or monthly, with discounts and make-up credits.
  • Expenses & profit — record your room/studio rent and see whether each class makes money, with a clear keep-or-grow verdict.
  • Monthly statements — know exactly what every student owes this month and a projection for next month; share a clean PDF.
  • CSV import & export — bring in your student list and back up everything to a spreadsheet.
  • Dashboard — revenue, expenses, net profit and your most- and least-profitable classes.
  • iCloud sync, iPhone & iPad.

How the cancellation policy works

In Settings you choose how much notice you need (for example 24 hours). If a student cancels at least that far ahead, that class is free for them; if they cancel late or don't show up, it's charged — and you can grant a make-up credit. You can override any single charge by hand. Every statement line shows the reason, so the numbers are always easy to explain.

Frequently asked

Is my data private?
Yes. Your students, fees and attendance stay on your device and, if you turn it on, sync through your own private iCloud. We never see or sell your data — see the Privacy Policy.

Can I change a charge?
Yes — open any session and set a student to present, waived, late-cancel or no-show, or override the charge entirely.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Rostra works fully offline at the studio; iCloud sync catches up when you're back online.
