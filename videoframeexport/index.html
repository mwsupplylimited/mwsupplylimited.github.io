Video Frame Export

Pull a pixel-perfect photo from any video. iPhone & iPad. Version 1.0.2

About

Video Frame Export lets you scrub through any video on your iPhone or iPad, step frame by frame to the exact moment you want, and export it as a high-quality photo. Built for moments where a screenshot won't do — sports finishes, kids' first steps, product photography from footage, screen recordings, anything you shot in 4K and want a single sharp still from.

Getting started

  • Tap Select Video and pick any video from your library.
  • Scrub the slider or use the step buttons (±1, ±10 frames) for precision.
  • Tap Export Frame, choose format, quality and resolution, then Save to Photos or Share.

Free vs Pro

Video Frame Export is free to use, with ads. Every core capability — including full native-resolution exports — works on the free plan; the free plan exports as JPEG and is ad-supported (a banner on the Home screen, and a short opt-in video you watch to unlock exports for a while).

The optional Pro subscription unlocks:

  • Lossless PNG and efficient HEIC export formats (the free plan exports JPEG).
  • An in-app Frame Library that stores every exported frame and syncs across your iPhone and iPad via iCloud.
  • No ads — removes the Home-screen banner and lets you export instantly, without watching a video.

Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription:

  • Pro Monthly — $1.99 / month
  • Pro Yearly — $10.99 / year
  • Both plans include a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

Managing your subscription

Subscriptions renew automatically and can be managed or cancelled at any time:

Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Video Frame Export

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. To restore a previous purchase, tap Restore in the paywall.

FAQ

Is the exported frame the exact frame I see in the preview?

Yes. The preview and the export are both decoded with zero time tolerance, so the on-screen image is the same frame that gets saved.

What resolutions are supported?

Up to the full native resolution of the source video — including 4K and above. Full resolution is free; Pro adds lossless PNG and HEIC output formats alongside JPEG.

Does it work with portrait video?

Yes. Orientation and aspect ratio are preserved for all orientations, including iPhone portrait footage stored with a 90° rotation transform.

What happens to my exported frames if I cancel Pro?

Frames you previously saved to your Photo Library are unaffected — those are normal photos in your library. The in-app Library remains visible, but new exports won't be added to it until Pro is reactivated.
