Video to Photo: Frame Grab Privacy Policy

Effective 28 May 2026 · Last updated 22 October 2026

Summary: Video to Photo: Frame Grab has no app account or login. It sends product analytics tied to a random installation identifier, as described in Section 6. Advertising services may collect identifiers and connection data; the details and available controls are described below. Your videos and exported frames stay on your device, or inside your own private iCloud database. The app is free with ads: on the free plan a third-party advertising provider (Unity LevelPlay, formerly ironSource) shows ads and may collect device and ad-interaction data as described in Section 5. The optional Pro upgrade removes all ads.

1. Information we collect

The app has no account system, login or user profile. Its first-party analytics do not include your name, email address, Apple Account, videos, photos or file names. They do include a random installation identifier, usage events and purchase product identifiers. Advertising services may process device identifiers, IP addresses and ad interactions as described in Section 5.

Network requests can disclose an IP address and normal connection information to their recipient. The app uses Apple services, product analytics and, on the free plan, advertising services. Version 1.0.7 also verifies the daily export allowance using the support website, as described in Section 7.

2. Photos & videos

The app reads videos and writes photos with the minimum permissions iOS provides:

  • Choosing a video uses Apple's system photo picker (PHPicker). The app receives only the single video you select for that session and has no other access to your photo library.
  • Saving an exported frame uses iOS's "Add Only" Photos authorization (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription). The app can add a photo to your library but cannot read existing photos.
  • Videos you pick from Photos or the Files app are copied to a temporary location on your device only so the app can decode them. That copy is short-lived: the app clears leftovers each time it launches, and iOS reclaims the rest.
  • Videos you share into the app — using the Share button in Photos, Files, Messages or any other app — are kept for longer, on purpose. A share hand-off cannot open the app for you, so the video is copied into the app's own storage and waits there until you come back to it. It stays until you delete it. You can see everything that is waiting, and its total size, on the app's Imported videos screen, and remove items individually or all at once; Settings shows the same total. Deleting removes the app's copy only — the original stays wherever you shared it from, untouched. These copies are working copies, so they are excluded from device backups.
  • Neither kind of copy leaves your device. The app never uploads a video anywhere, and never modifies or deletes your original.

3. iCloud sync (Pro feature)

With Pro, you can save frames to the in-app Library and sync them across your own Apple devices via Apple CloudKit. Cloud copies are stored in your private CloudKit database, under your Apple ID. MW Supply Limited has no access to, and never receives a copy of, the contents of your Library. iCloud sync follows Apple's own privacy practices.

4. Subscriptions and one-time purchases

Pro is sold two ways, both through Apple's In-App Purchase: as an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly), or as Lifetime, a single one-time purchase that is not a subscription and never renews. All purchase and payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We receive aggregated, anonymous sales reports from App Store Connect; we do not receive your name, email address, billing details, or any personally-identifying information about subscribers or buyers.

5. Advertising (free plan) and App Tracking Transparency

On the free plan, Video to Photo: Frame Grab shows ads using Unity LevelPlay (the mediation platform formerly known as ironSource), which selects ads from Unity Ads, ironSource Ads and their demand partners. Three kinds of ad appear:

  • A banner on the Home screen only. It never covers a control, and it disappears entirely if no ad is available.
  • An opt-in rewarded video before export. If no ad can be loaded or presented, an otherwise eligible export proceeds without one. In version 1.0.7, the daily free allowance still applies and requires internet verification (Section 7). Watching an ad does not increase that allowance.
  • A full-screen ad shown at most once when you return to the Home screen after the app has been in the background for several minutes, limited to a few times per day. It never appears while you are choosing a frame, and never on top of another screen.

To select, deliver, measure and cap the frequency of these ads and to prevent fraud, the advertising SDK may collect device and usage information — including a device advertising identifier (IDFA, only with your consent), an IP address (and the coarse region derived from it), and information about your interaction with the ads.

Settings inside the app includes a Personalised ads switch, which is off by default. Leaving it off means you still see ads, but they are not personalised based on your activity.

The first time the free version prepares to show an ad, iOS shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt:

  • If you allow tracking, the advertising identifier may be used to show you personalized ads, which can involve combining this data with data from other apps and websites for advertising — “tracking” as Apple defines it.
  • If you ask the app not to track (or take no action), you still see ads, but non-personalized ones, and the advertising identifier is not used to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

This advertising data is collected and processed by Unity Technologies (which operates LevelPlay and ironSource) as an independent controller, not by MW Supply Limited. We never receive it. Unity’s handling of this data is governed by the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Pro removes all ads. While you have Pro the app does not display ads, does not start the advertising SDK at all, and never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

6. Product analytics

We record a small number of events about how the app is used — for example that a screen was opened, that an export finished, or that a purchase completed or was cancelled. We use these only to understand which parts of the app work and which do not.

What identifies these events. Each installation generates a random identifier the first time the app runs. It is not your Apple Account, not the advertising identifier (IDFA), not the vendor identifier (IDFV), and not derived from your device or from you in any way. It exists so we can tell “one person opened this screen five times” from “five people opened it once”. It lets us associate events from the same installation. We do not use it to link activity across other companies’ apps or websites. Deleting and reinstalling the app produces a new identifier.

What is never included. No name, email address, or account. No contents of your videos or exported photos. No file names. No location. No advertising identifier.

Who processes it. Analytics are processed on our behalf by TelemetryDeck GmbH (Germany), acting as our data processor under the TelemetryDeck privacy policy. There is no analytics SDK in the app; our own code sends the event payload. As with other network services, the recipient can receive an IP address and normal connection information.

This is not “tracking”. Apple defines tracking as linking your data with data from other companies for advertising or sharing it with data brokers. We do neither, so these analytics are not covered by the App Tracking Transparency prompt in Section 5 and are not affected by your answer to it.

Analytics are sent only from real App Store and TestFlight installations — never from a developer build or the Simulator.

7. Network activity

The app uses Apple-operated services for the Photos picker, StoreKit purchases and entitlement verification, and CloudKit Library sync; the product analytics described in Section 6; and advertising requests from the Unity LevelPlay SDK on the free plan.

Daily allowance verification in version 1.0.7. Before a free export, the app sends an HTTPS request to our support website hosted by GitHub Pages and uses its response date to verify the UTC day. The request contains no video, photo, file name, purchase receipt or analytics identifier. GitHub and its delivery infrastructure can receive the IP address and normal connection information. The last used allowance day is stored in the device Keychain; it is not uploaded by this check. Pro exports bypass this allowance request. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of connection information.

8. Third-party services

The only third-party SDKs in Video to Photo: Frame Grab are Unity LevelPlay and the Unity Ads adapter, used solely to show ads on the free plan (Section 5). There is deliberately no analytics SDK: the events in Section 6 are sent by our own code. Service providers also receive normal network connection information. There are no crash-reporting services other than Apple's own first-party crash reporting (which only runs if you have opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

9. Children

Video to Photo: Frame Grab is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+ on the App Store. The app is not directed at children. The analytics and advertising data practices described above apply to use of the app. Ads on the free plan are served through Unity LevelPlay; the app is declared as not directed to children, and unless you have both granted tracking permission and turned on the Personalised ads switch, it requests non-personalised ads only.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Video to Photo: Frame Grab handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy nutrition labels for the affected version of the app.
