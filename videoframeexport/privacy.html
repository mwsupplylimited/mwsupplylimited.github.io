Video Frame Export Privacy Policy

Effective 28 May 2026 · Last updated 5 July 2026

Summary: Video Frame Export does not collect personal data itself — there is no account, no login, and no analytics. Your videos and exported frames stay on your device, or inside your own private iCloud database. The app is free with ads: on the free plan a third-party advertising provider (Google AdMob) shows ads and may collect device and ad-interaction data as described in Section 5. The optional Pro subscription removes all ads.

1. Information we collect

None directly. Video Frame Export does not ask for, collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information. The app has no account system, no login, and no user profile. The one exception is the third-party advertising provider on the free plan (Section 5), which collects data under Google’s control — not ours — to deliver ads.

2. Photos & videos

The app reads videos and writes photos with the minimum permissions iOS provides:

  • Choosing a video uses Apple's system photo picker (PHPicker). The app receives only the single video you select for that session and has no other access to your photo library.
  • Saving an exported frame uses iOS's "Add Only" Photos authorization (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription). The app can add a photo to your library but cannot read existing photos.
  • Videos you pick are copied to a temporary location on your device only so the app can decode them, and are removed by iOS when the app no longer needs them.

3. iCloud sync (Pro feature)

If you subscribe to Pro, frames you export are also saved to an in-app Library that syncs across your own Apple devices via Apple CloudKit. This data is stored exclusively in your private CloudKit database, under your Apple ID. MW Supply Limited has no access to, and never receives a copy of, the contents of your Library. iCloud sync follows Apple's own privacy practices.

4. Subscriptions

Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription via Apple's In-App Purchase. All purchase and payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We receive aggregated, anonymous sales reports from App Store Connect; we do not receive your name, email address, billing details, or any personally-identifying information about subscribers.

5. Advertising (free plan) and App Tracking Transparency

On the free plan, Video Frame Export shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob): a banner on the Home screen and an opt-in rewarded video you watch to export a frame. Rewarded ads are opt-in — you choose to watch one (or subscribe to Pro) to export a frame; if no ad can be loaded (for example, offline), your export proceeds without one. To select, deliver, measure and cap the frequency of these ads and to prevent fraud, the Google SDK may collect device and usage information — including a device advertising identifier (IDFA, only with your consent), an IP address (and the coarse region derived from it), and information about your interaction with the ads.

The first time the free version prepares to show an ad, iOS shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt:

  • If you allow tracking, the advertising identifier may be used to show you personalized ads, which can involve combining this data with data from other apps and websites for advertising — “tracking” as Apple defines it.
  • If you ask the app not to track (or take no action), you still see ads, but non-personalized ones, and the advertising identifier is not used to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

This advertising data is collected and processed by Google as an independent controller, not by MW Supply Limited. We never receive it. Google’s handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Pro removes all ads. While you are a Pro subscriber the app does not display ads, does not start the advertising SDK, and never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

6. Network activity

Video Frame Export makes no network requests to servers owned by MW Supply Limited. The app's network activity is Apple-operated traffic for the Photos picker, StoreKit (subscription verification), and CloudKit (Pro Library sync) — plus, on the free plan only, ad requests made by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Section 5).

7. Third-party services

The only third-party SDK in Video Frame Export is the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), used solely to show ads on the free plan (Section 5). The app contains no analytics frameworks and no crash-reporting services other than Apple's own first-party crash reporting (which only runs if you have opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

8. Children

Video Frame Export is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+ on the App Store. The app itself collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and it is not directed at children. Ads on the free plan are served by Google; when tracking permission has not been granted, the app requests non-personalized ads only.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how Video Frame Export handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy nutrition labels for the affected version of the app.

10. Contact
