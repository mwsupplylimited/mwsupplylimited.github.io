Big Two 鋤大D — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

In short: Big Two keeps your game data — statistics, token balance and settings — on your device and, if you enable it, in your own private iCloud account. We (mwsupplylimited) do not collect, see, store on our own servers, or sell your data. There are no accounts and no login.

Information you create

Your game statistics, token balance, preferences and rule settings are stored locally on your device. If iCloud is enabled, your token balance and settings also sync through your private iCloud database, governed by Apple's privacy policy and not accessible to us.

Information we collect

None directly. Big Two has no account system, no servers of our own, and does not transmit your personal data to us.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect device information and, with your permission, your device's advertising identifier. On first launch the app asks, via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether you allow tracking:

• If you allow, ads may be personalised.
• If you don't, you still see ads, but non-personalised ones.

You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy. Big Two Pro and the one-time Remove Ads purchase remove all ads, and those users' devices do not initialise the ads SDK.

Tokens and in-app purchases

Tokens are a virtual in-game currency with no cash value that cannot be redeemed for money. You can earn them free (a daily top-up, playing, and optional rewarded ads) or buy token packs. Subscriptions (Big Two Pro) and one-time purchases are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase — Apple processes the payment and we never receive your payment details. The app only learns whether your device currently has an active entitlement and how many tokens it holds.

Online play

Ranked tables are matched using Apple's Game Center where available; your Game Center identity is governed by Apple's privacy policy. When a live opponent isn't ready, tables are filled with the app's built-in AI players so games start instantly.

Diagnostics

Big Two does not include third-party analytics or crash-tracking SDKs. Apple may provide us anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics if you have opted in to share them with developers in iOS Settings; these contain no personal data.

Children

The app is a card game suitable for a general audience and is not directed at young children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your control

You can reset your data by deleting the app, which removes its on-device data. To remove iCloud copies, sign out of iCloud or delete the app's data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves; material changes will be reflected on this page with a new date.
