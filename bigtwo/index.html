FAQ

Are the deals fair?

Yes. Every deal uses a standard, transparent shuffle. We never give any player better cards, and we don't sell an in-game advantage.

What do tokens do?

Tokens are a virtual in-game currency used to enter ranked online tables and for extra coach hints. You get a free daily top-up, can earn more by playing and watching optional ads, or buy token packs. Tokens have no cash value and are not redeemable for money.

What does Big Two Pro include?

No ads, unlimited coach hints and post-game reviews, Master-level bot opponents, advanced statistics, exclusive table themes, and a higher daily token floor. It never affects the cards you're dealt.

How do I restore a purchase or manage my subscription?

Open Settings → Big Two Pro → Restore Purchases, or manage/cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Does my progress sync across devices?

Your tokens and settings sync privately through your own iCloud account. There are no accounts and no login.
