Why a separate helper?

macOS does not allow a sandboxed Mac App Store app to read the System Management Controller (SMC), where precise component temperatures and fan RPM come from. On its own, Corynx shows the system thermal-pressure level (Nominal → Critical). The Sensor Helper reads the SMC locally and hands the readings to Corynx on your Mac, so you get exact °C and live fan speeds.

Private by design

  • Runs entirely on your Mac and makes no network connections.
  • Talks to Corynx only over a private, on-device connection.
  • Free — it simply adds the sensor readings to Corynx Pro.
