Corynx Fan Helper

Take manual control of your Mac's fans — boost-only, with automatic safety revert.

Download Fan Helper

Free with Corynx Pro · Apple-notarized · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon

Boost-only & safe. The Fan Helper can only make your fans spin faster than macOS would — it never sets them below your Mac's minimum and never turns them off. macOS still forces full cooling if things get hot. Everything returns to automatic the moment you turn it off, quit Corynx, or restart your Mac.

Install

  1. Download the file above and unzip it (you'll get a CorynxFanHelper folder).
  2. Right-click install.command → Open (right-click the first time, so macOS lets you run it), then click Open again to confirm.
  3. Enter your Mac password when prompted — this installs a small background helper that runs as a system service.
  4. Open Corynx → Fans. Switch on Manual boost and set each fan's speed.

Restore automatic control

Any one of these hands your fans straight back to macOS:

  • In Corynx, turn Manual boost off (or click Return to Automatic).
  • Quit Corynx — the helper reverts to automatic on its own within a few seconds.
  • Restart your Mac — the helper starts each boot in automatic and applies nothing until you ask.
  • Run uninstall.command (from the same folder) to remove the helper entirely.

Why a separate helper?

Controlling fans means writing to the System Management Controller (SMC), which macOS does not allow a sandboxed Mac App Store app to do. The Fan Helper is a small, notarized background service that performs those writes locally and takes commands only from Corynx on your Mac.

Private & safe by design

  • Runs entirely on your Mac with no network connections.
  • Boost-only: an error can only ever make your Mac cooler, never hotter.
  • A built-in safety override forces fans to maximum if any sensor approaches a thermal limit.
