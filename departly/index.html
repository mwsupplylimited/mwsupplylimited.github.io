Departly — Support

Hong Kong bus arrivals, done right. Live ETAs for KMB, LWB and Citybus, nearby stops, favourites, and a reminder that wakes you before your stop.

Features

  • Search any route by number (1A, 269D, N269) or destination, across KMB · LWB · Citybus.
  • Live arrival times for every stop on a route, refreshed automatically.
  • Nearby stops — see the stops closest to you and a live departure board for each.
  • Favourites — pin the routes and stops you use, synced across your devices with iCloud.
  • Get-off reminder — Departly alerts you as you approach your destination stop, even with the screen locked, so you can relax on the journey.
  • English, 繁體中文 and 简体中文.

Using the get-off reminder

Open a stop (from a route or the Nearby tab) and tap Remind me to get off here. Departly watches your location in the background and sounds an alarm as you get close. Allow location access (“While Using” is enough; “Always” lets the reminder fire even if the app was closed) and notifications for the most reliable alert.

Departly Pro

Departly is free, with all route search, live ETAs and the get-off reminder available to everyone. Departly Pro (a monthly or yearly subscription) removes ads and adds unlimited favourites, Home- and Lock-Screen widgets, Live Activities and recurring commute reminders. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Frequently asked

Why is an arrival showing “—”?
That route has no live prediction right now — often a peak-only or night route that isn’t currently running.

Are the times official?
Departly shows the same real-time estimates the operators publish as Hong Kong public sector information. Times are estimates and can change with traffic.

Is Departly affiliated with KMB / Citybus?
No. Departly is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any transport operator or the Government of the HKSAR.
