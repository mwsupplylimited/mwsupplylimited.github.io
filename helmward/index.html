About

Helmward is a private, Apple-native remote desktop. Leave your Mac at home, and reach its screen from your iPhone, iPad, or another Mac while you travel — see the live screen and control the mouse and keyboard as if you were sitting in front of it. Helmward is built for people, not licensing departments: no false “commercial use” lockouts, no session time limits, and end-to-end encryption so the connection relay never sees your screen.

How it works

  • On the Mac you want to reach: install the free Helmward Host (a notarized Mac download). Grant Screen Recording and Accessibility when asked, and note the Host ID and password it shows.
  • On your iPhone, iPad, or other Mac: open the Helmward app, enter that Host ID and password, and connect.
  • Keep the home Mac awake and logged in while you're away so it stays reachable. Helmward keeps it from sleeping while it's connectable.

Two parts

  • Helmward — the app you connect from (iPhone & iPad, on the App Store; and a Mac viewer).
  • Helmward Host — the free app on the Mac you connect to. Because controlling a whole Mac needs Screen Recording, Accessibility, and a background helper that Apple's App Sandbox doesn't allow, the Host is distributed as a notarized direct download (just like other professional remote-desktop tools), not through the Mac App Store. It is signed and notarized by Apple.

FAQ

Is it secure?

Yes. The screen and control data are end-to-end encrypted between your own devices. When a direct connection isn't possible, traffic is forwarded through a relay — but the relay only sees encrypted packets it cannot read. Pairing is protected by your Host ID and password.

Can I control my iPhone from my Mac?

Helmward controls Macs and PCs. Because of Apple platform restrictions, an iPhone or iPad can connect out to a computer, but cannot itself be remotely controlled.

Does it work over cellular / away from home Wi-Fi?

Yes — Helmward is designed for exactly that. It connects your devices over the internet (cellular or any Wi-Fi), finding the most direct path it can and falling back to a secure relay when networks are strict.

Will I ever be flagged for “commercial use”?

No. Helmward does not police how you use it and will never lock you out with a false commercial-use flag.
