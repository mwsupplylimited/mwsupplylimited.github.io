Questions

FAQ

Why is there a separate Mac app?

To control a whole Mac, the host needs Screen Recording, Accessibility, and a background helper — capabilities Apple's App Sandbox forbids, and every App Store app must be sandboxed. So the Mac host ships as a notarized direct download (just like TeamViewer, AnyDesk, and Chrome Remote Desktop). The iPhone app is the remote control, which is the right place for it.

Is it secure?

Yes. The screen, audio, and control data are end-to-end encrypted between your own devices. Pairing is protected by a password shown on your Mac — knowing the Host ID alone is not enough to connect.

Can I control my iPhone from my Mac?

Overhand controls Macs. Because of Apple platform restrictions, an iPhone or iPad can connect out to a computer but cannot itself be remotely controlled.

Does it work over cellular?

Yes — it's designed for exactly that. It connects over cellular or any Wi‑Fi, finding the most direct path and falling back to a secure relay when networks are strict.

Will I be flagged for "commercial use"?

No. Overhand does not police how you use it and will never lock you out with a false commercial-use flag.
