Chronograde

Turn your iPhone or iPad into a timegrapher and track your watch's accuracy against atomic time. Version 1.5

About

Chronograde measures how a mechanical watch is running — live rate and beat error read acoustically through your device's microphone — and tracks each watch's daily drift against true atomic time over days and weeks. It runs on both iPhone and iPad. Add as many watches as you like, see how each grades against COSC and METAS chronometer standards, compare accuracy by position across sessions, log wear and service history, set your watch against an NTP-corrected clock, and export everything to CSV or Excel. Chronograde is free to use and shows no ads at all; an optional Chronograde Pro upgrade (subscription or a one-time Lifetime unlock) adds unlimited measurements, Regulation Coach, Accuracy by Position, your Chronometer Score, the Atomic Clock, wear & service logs and export. Your watch data stays on your device and, if you choose, your own iCloud.

Getting started

  • Add a watch with the + button. Brand and model are required; everything else (reference, serial, caliber, purchase price, photo) is optional.
  • Measure the rate (Timegrapher tab). Rest the phone on the caseback so it can hear the balance wheel, and watch the live rate and beat error settle. Use AUTO beat-rate detection or set the BPH yourself.
  • Track drift over time. Tap Record Measurement, pick the time your watch is about to show — down to the exact second (e.g. 14:30:20) — and press RECORD the instant the second hand touches it. After your second reading the drift chart and average rate per day fill in automatically.
  • Pull-to-sync atomic time from the toolbar. Chronograde talks to public NTP servers (time.apple.com, time.google.com, pool.ntp.org) to correct for device-clock drift.
  • Log wear with one tap, and log service with type, provider, cost, and notes — set a service interval and the detail screen shows when the next one is due.
  • Personalize under Settings → Theme Shop. Two themes are free; premium themes can be unlocked with a one-time purchase or with Crowns.

FAQ

Does Chronograde show ads?

No. Chronograde shows no advertising of any kind — no banners, no interstitials and no rewarded videos — on the free tier or on any paid plan. Earlier versions carried a small banner; that has been removed entirely. The app is funded by the optional Chronograde Pro upgrade.

What is free, and what needs Pro?

The Timegrapher is free for everyone — measure any watch's rate and beat error at no cost, with no account. You can also add your whole collection and record up to 10 accuracy measurements free. Chronograde Pro adds unlimited measurements and sessions, Regulation Coach, Accuracy by Position, your Chronometer Score, the Atomic Clock, wear & service logs, and export to CSV & Excel.

What are Crowns?

Crowns are an optional in-app currency for users on the free tier. You can earn them for free (a welcome grant, plus a Crown each day you open the app) or buy a pack, and spend them on extra measurements beyond the free allowance, a 24-hour Atomic Clock pass, or premium themes. Crowns are entirely optional and never lock a feature that is otherwise free. Pro subscribers don't need Crowns.

How does Chronograde Pro work?

You can record up to 10 measurements across all watches for free. Chronograde Pro unlocks unlimited measurements and the full Pro feature set. It's available as a monthly or annual auto-renewable subscription (each with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers), or as a one-time Lifetime unlock — pay once and own Pro forever, with no subscription. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; manage or cancel anytime from iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Tap Restore Purchases on the membership screen if you've bought Pro before on the same Apple ID.

What counts as an "accurate" measurement?

For drift tracking, Chronograde uses SNTP (RFC 4330) to query a public time service and applies the offset to your device's clock — accurate to well under a tenth of a second. The bottleneck is your reaction time pressing RECORD as the second hand touches the target; press it consistently and the math averages out over multiple readings. For the acoustic Timegrapher, rest the phone firmly on the caseback in a quiet room and let the reading stabilize for a few seconds.

What's a good rate?

It depends on the watch. COSC chronometer-certified mechanical watches must run within −4/+6 seconds per day. ETA standard movements typically run ±10–30 s/day. Quartz watches usually run ±0.5 s/day or better. Chronograde colors the rate green up to ±10 s/day, orange up to ±30, and red beyond that.

The sync button shows "Could not reach a time server."

Chronograde uses UDP on port 123 for SNTP queries. Some corporate, hotel, or captive Wi-Fi networks block outbound UDP. Try a different network or use cellular, then tap the sync icon in the top-right of the Watches tab. Without a successful sync, measurements fall back to the device clock and are marked accordingly.

Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?

If iCloud (CloudKit) is enabled for Chronograde and you're signed in with the same Apple ID on both devices, your watches, measurements, wear log, and service history sync automatically. Settings → iCloud shows the current status.

Can I export my data?

Yes. From the Watches tab menu, choose Export to Excel (a workbook with Watches, Measurements, Wear, and Service sheets) or Export to CSV. You can also import a watches CSV the same way; Chronograde detects duplicates by serial number or brand + model + nickname.
