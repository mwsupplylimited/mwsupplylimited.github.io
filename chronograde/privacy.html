Chronograde Privacy Policy

Effective 28 May 2026 · Last updated 24 July 2026

Summary: Your watch data stays on your device (and in your own iCloud, if you enable sync) — MW Supply Limited operates no servers that receive it and has no account system. Chronograde shows no advertising of any kind, on any plan, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and contains no analytics or tracking SDKs. The app queries public NTP time servers to keep your measurements accurate, and Apple handles all purchases.

1. Who we are

Chronograde is published by MW Supply Limited (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles, what stays on your device, and the limited information Apple receives to provide payments and iCloud sync. Questions: mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.

2. Data you create (stored on your device)

Chronograde stores the records you create locally using Apple’s SwiftData framework. We never receive this data on any server of ours:

  • Watches you’ve added (brand, model, optional nickname, reference number, serial number, caliber, crystal, movement type, notes, optional purchase price and date, optional photo).
  • Measurements (target time, accurate time, computed offset/rate, and Timegrapher readings — rate and beat error — with optional notes).
  • Wear log entries and service history entries (date, type, provider, cost, notes).
  • App preferences (selected theme, sort order, reminder settings, and your Crown token balance).

This data is yours. It is removed when you delete the app, and you can export it at any time from the Watches tab menu (CSV or XLSX).

3. iCloud sync (optional)

If iCloud is enabled for Chronograde on your device, the records above are synchronized through your private CloudKit container (iCloud.com.edfd.WatchAccuracy) so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. We have no access to this data — it is stored under your Apple ID in Apple’s infrastructure and is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can turn iCloud off for Chronograde at any time in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

4. Microphone (the Timegrapher)

The Timegrapher feature listens to your watch ticking to measure its rate and beat error. Audio is processed live, on your device only, to detect tick timing. No audio recording is saved, transmitted, or sent to any server — only the computed numeric results are stored, and only if you choose to save the reading. The microphone is used solely while the Timegrapher screen is active and you have started a measurement.

5. Advertising (none)

Chronograde does not show advertising. There is no banner, no interstitial and no rewarded video, on the free tier or any paid plan. No advertising network receives any information about you from this app.

Earlier versions of Chronograde were ad-supported via Google AdMob. That advertising has been removed. In the interest of full disclosure: the Google Mobile Ads framework is still linked into the app binary from that period, but it is never initialised — the app does not start the ads SDK, request an ad, load a consent form, or read any advertising identifier — so it neither collects nor transmits anything. If advertising is ever reintroduced, this policy and the App Store privacy labels will be updated before that version ships.

6. Tracking (none)

Chronograde does not track you. It never presents Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, never accesses the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not link any data to you or your device for advertising or measurement purposes — by us or by anyone else. Because nothing is tracked, there is no consent dialog to show and nothing for you to opt out of.

7. Purchases, subscriptions & Crown tokens

All purchases are processed entirely by Apple’s StoreKit. We never see your payment card or Apple ID; Apple only tells the app which product IDs your Apple ID is entitled to. Chronograde offers:

  • Chronograde Pro — an auto-renewable subscription (monthly and annual options, with an introductory 7-day free trial for new subscribers) that removes ads and unlocks unlimited measurements and Pro features. It renews automatically until cancelled; manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals but does not refund the current period.
  • Chronograde Pro — Lifetime — a one-time non-consumable purchase that unlocks all Pro features permanently. It does not auto-renew.
  • Theme unlocks — one-time non-consumable purchases for premium app themes.
  • Crown token packs — consumable in-app purchases. Crowns are an optional in-app currency that free users can also earn for free (a starter grant, a bonus each day you open the app, and occasional one-off grants). Crowns can be spent on extra measurements, a 24-hour Atomic Clock pass, or premium themes. They are stored on your device and in your own iCloud, and have no cash value.

Purchase records and entitlements are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

8. Network activity

Chronograde makes outbound network requests for only three purposes: (a) stateless SNTP (RFC 4330) time queries to public servers (time.apple.com, time.google.com, pool.ntp.org) to compute clock drift — these contain no personal information; (b) Apple StoreKit calls for purchases; and (c) Apple CloudKit calls to sync your own records to your own iCloud, if you have iCloud enabled. No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting requests are made. There are no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs other than Apple’s own first-party reporting (which runs only if you opted in under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

9. Third-party services

Chronograde uses Apple’s StoreKit, SwiftData/CloudKit, WidgetKit, App Intents and UserNotifications frameworks, which are first-party Apple services. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform remain linked in the binary from the app’s previously ad-supported versions but are never initialised or called (see section 5). No other third-party SDKs are included, and no third party receives data from this app.

10. Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder in Settings, Chronograde schedules a local notification on your device using iOS’s UserNotifications framework. No notification content is sent to any server.

11. Children

Chronograde is a general-audience utility intended for adult watch owners and is not directed to children. It shows no advertising and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will assist.

12. Your choices

  • Ads and tracking: nothing to opt out of — there is no advertising and no tracking on any plan.
  • Delete your data: delete records in-app, or delete the app to remove all local data; disable iCloud to stop sync.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how Chronograde handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy labels for the affected version of the app.

14. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
