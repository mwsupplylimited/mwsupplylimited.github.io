Voltway

Battery charge management and power intelligence for Mac, iPhone and iPad.

What Voltway does

On Mac, Voltway draws a live power-flow diagram — how many watts are coming out of the wall, how many the Mac is using, and whether the battery is charging or supplying — and opens up battery forensics the system never shows you: learned capacity per cell, cell voltages, impedance trend, cycle count, and how long the pack has been sitting at a high state of charge.

On iPhone and iPad, Voltway keeps an honest charge timeline, fires alert rules when the level crosses a threshold you set, and gives Shortcuts a set of actions so you can automate around your charging.

Things Voltway is careful not to claim

No iPhone or iPad app can limit charging. Apple provides no way for a third-party app to stop, slow or delay charging, so Voltway does not pretend to. It alerts you, and it can switch a smart plug if you have one. Newer iPhones have a built-in charge limit in Settings › Battery › Charging.

iOS reports the battery level in 5% steps. Since iOS 17 that is all any app is given, so every gauge and alert threshold in Voltway sits on a 5% grid. A threshold like 82% is simply not observable.

iOS exposes no battery health data to apps — no cycle count, capacity, voltage or temperature. Those readouts exist only in the Mac app, where the system does provide them.

No Mac lets software set a charging speed. There is no charge-rate control on any Mac, so Voltway offers scheduled and delayed charging instead, which achieves the same goals — less heat and less time parked at a high charge.

Getting alerts when Voltway is closed iPhone & iPad

iOS never lets an app watch the battery in the background — that applies to every app, not just Voltway. What does work is a Shortcuts personal automation, which you create once:

  1. Open Shortcuts and tap the Automation tab.
  2. Tap +, then choose Battery Level.
  3. Set the slider to your level, pick Rises Above, and choose Run Immediately.
  4. Tap Next, search for Voltway, and add the Check Charge Alerts action.
  5. Tap Done.

Apps cannot create personal automations for you — Apple requires you to build it yourself. Without it, alerts fire while Voltway is open or on the next refresh the system grants, which is not guaranteed.

Charge control on Mac optional

Limiting charge means writing to your Mac's system power controller, which needs privileges a Mac App Store app is not allowed to have. Rather than ask for them, Voltway keeps that work in a small, separate, notarized helper that you install yourself. The App Store app never escalates and never installs anything.

See the Voltway charge helper page for the download and instructions.

Voltway Pro

Voltway is free to use. The Mac app's entire read-only side — the power-flow diagram, the forensics, the history — is free forever, and on iPhone you get a charge timeline, one alert rule, and all the Shortcuts actions.

Voltway Pro adds unlimited alert rules, full history with CSV export, capacity-trend charts, smart-plug control and schedules, Live Activity, and the advanced Mac charge tools. One purchase covers Mac, iPhone and iPad.

Managing or cancelling

On iPhone or iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On Mac: App Store › your name › Account Settings › Subscriptions. You can also tap Manage Subscription on Voltway's Membership screen. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Restoring a purchase

Open Voltway's Membership screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with — a purchase cannot be moved between Apple Accounts.

Common questions

Why does the level jump 80% → 85% and never show 82%?

Because iOS only reports 5% steps. This is a platform limit, not a bug, and it applies to every app.

Why does my Mac show three different battery health numbers?

Because macOS genuinely reports three. Apple's figure is smoothed and rounded to a whole percent; the other two come straight from the fuel gauge. Voltway shows all three rather than hiding the ones that disagree — track the trend, not any single reading.

Why is my Mac plugged in but not charging at 80%?

Recent Macs have their own charge limit built into macOS. Voltway shows you when that is what is holding the battery, so you know it is the system and not a fault.

Does Voltway drain my battery?

It samples only while you are looking at it, and stops when its screens are off-screen. A battery app that costs you battery would be a poor trade.
