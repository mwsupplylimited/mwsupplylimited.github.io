Voltway

Battery charge management and power intelligence for Mac.

Download on the Mac App Store

What Voltway does

Voltway draws a live power-flow diagram of your Mac — how many watts are coming out of the wall, how many the Mac is using, and whether the battery is charging or supplying — and opens up the battery data macOS keeps to itself: learned capacity per cell, cell voltages, impedance trend, cycle count, and how long the pack has been sitting at a high state of charge.

With the optional helper it also controls charging: hold a limit below 80%, set separate stop and resume levels, or run from the adapter without charging at all.

Voltway is a Mac app. There is no iPhone or iPad version, and that is deliberate: Apple provides no way for any third-party app to limit, stop or slow charging on iOS, and iOS exposes no battery health data to apps at all. An iPhone app could only have watched a 5%-rounded number and nagged you, so we did not ship one. Newer iPhones have a built-in charge limit in Settings › Battery › Charging.

No Mac lets software set a charging speed. There is no charge-rate control on any Mac, so Voltway does not claim one.

Charge control on Mac optional

Limiting charge means writing to your Mac's system power controller, which needs privileges a Mac App Store app is not allowed to have. Rather than ask for them, Voltway keeps that work in a small, separate, notarized helper that you install yourself. The App Store app never escalates and never installs anything.

See the Voltway charge helper page for the download and instructions.

Voltway Pro

Voltway is free to use. The entire read-only side — the power-flow diagram, the battery forensics, the charge-residency histogram and CSV export — is free forever. Voltway Free keeps the last 7 days of charge history. No helper, no privileges, no nagging.

Voltway Pro adds the charge-control tools — limits below 80%, separate stop and resume levels, and running on the adapter without charging — plus unlimited alert rules, quiet hours and repeat reminders, and it keeps your full charge history instead of 7 days. It is available monthly, annually with a 3-day free trial, or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

Managing or cancelling

App Store › your name › Account Settings › Subscriptions. You can also use Manage Subscription on Voltway's Membership screen. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Restoring a purchase

Open Voltway's Membership screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with — a purchase cannot be moved between Apple Accounts.

Common questions

Why does my Mac show three different battery health numbers?

Because macOS genuinely reports three. Apple's figure is smoothed and rounded to a whole percent; the other two come straight from the fuel gauge. Voltway shows all three rather than hiding the ones that disagree — track the trend, not any single reading.

Why is my Mac plugged in but not charging at 80%?

Recent Macs have their own charge limit built into macOS. Voltway shows you when that is what is holding the battery, so you know it is the system and not a fault.

Does Voltway drain my battery?

It samples only while you are looking at it, and stops when its screens are off-screen. A battery app that costs you battery would be a poor trade.
