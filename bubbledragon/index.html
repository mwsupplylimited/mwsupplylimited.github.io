Bubble Dragons

Aim, match, and hatch dragons. A bubble shooter for iPhone & iPad. Version 1.0

About

Bubble Dragons is a hex-grid bubble shooter with 100 hand-made levels across four acts — Hatchling Hollow, Ember Caves, Frost Spire and Dragon's Roost. Match three or more bubbles of the same colour to pop them, and knock away whatever they were holding up. Every level was checked by a solver before it shipped, so every one of them can be cleared inside the shots you are given.

How to play

  • Drag to aim. A dotted line shows exactly where the bubble will go, including the bounce off a wall.
  • Lift your finger to shoot. Match 3 or more of a colour and they pop.
  • Watch the highlight. Bubbles that are about to pop pulse before you shoot — that is the shot planning itself.
  • Cut the supports. Only the ceiling holds bubbles up. Anything left unattached falls, and drops score far more than pops.
  • Mind the line. The ceiling drops lower every few shots that hit nothing. If bubbles cross the red line, the level ends.

Stars

Stars come from how many shots you have left when the board clears: three stars for finishing with 30% of your shots unused, two for 12%, one for clearing at all. Finishing faster is always worth more.

Special bubbles

  • Stone — cannot be matched. Drop it by clearing what holds it.
  • Ice — pop a bubble beside it and it thaws into that colour.
  • Web — bubbles caught in a web cannot fall. They have to be popped.
  • Armour — takes two matches beside it to strip.
  • Bomb — hit it with any colour and it clears everything around it.
  • Rainbow — matches any colour.
  • Lightning — clears its whole row.
  • +5 — hit it for five extra shots.

Dragon Run

An endless mode with one bubble bank instead of per-level shots. Clear a stage quickly and you get back more bubbles than you spent; the run ends when the bank empties. Dragon Run never costs a life, so there is always something to play.

Lives

You hold up to five lives and one refills every 30 minutes. A life is only ever spent on a failed attempt — winning never costs one — and no level before level 10 costs a life at all. If you are out, you can watch a short optional video for one, or play Dragon Run.

Dragon Keeper membership

Bubble Dragons is free, and every one of the 100 levels is free. Dragon Keeper is an optional subscription that adds unlimited lives, removes interstitial ads entirely, grants 300 coins immediately and 100 every day, a free booster each day, exclusive dragon and cannon skins, a permanent two-bounce aim line, 20 subscriber-only bonus levels and iCloud progress sync.

Monthly is US$4.99 and annual is US$29.99, with a 3-day free trial on the annual plan. Prices in other countries are set by the App Store. A subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; manage or cancel it any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Dragon Keeper never changes level difficulty or shot budgets. It is convenience and cosmetics only — a paying player and a free player solve exactly the same puzzles.

Ads

The free version shows a full-screen ad only after a failed attempt, never after a win, never during a level, and never before level 10. Everything else is opt-in: you choose to watch a video for extra shots, a free booster, double coins or a life. There is no banner over the board.

Your progress & iCloud

Your levels, stars, coins and settings are saved on your device and mirrored to your own private iCloud, so they follow you between your devices when you are signed in. We never see this data.

Accessibility

  • Colourblind mode adds a distinct shape to every bubble colour — turn it on in Settings.
  • Reduce Motion is respected: particles are cut back and screen shake is disabled.
  • Sound, music and haptics each have their own switch.
  • VoiceOver reads the cannon's current and next bubble.

FAQ

Is every level actually beatable?

Yes. Every level is run through a solver before release and must be clearable within its shot budget, with room to spare. If a level ever proves otherwise, please email us the level number.

Does it work offline?

Yes — all 100 levels and Dragon Run play with no connection.

I subscribed but do not see my benefits.

Open Membership from the main menu and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

I am stuck on a level.

Look for a bubble that is holding up a large group — dropping it scores five times what popping does and clears far more board. The pre-shot highlight shows what a shot would pop before you take it.

How do I turn off sound or haptics?

Settings, from the main menu.
