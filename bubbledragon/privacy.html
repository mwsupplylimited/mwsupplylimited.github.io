Privacy Policy

Bubble Dragons for iPhone & iPad · Last updated 18 August 2026

The short version

Bubble Dragons has no accounts and no login. We do not collect your name, email address or contacts. Your game progress is stored on your device and in your private iCloud, which we cannot read. The free version shows advertising, and the ad provider receives limited device information in order to serve and measure those ads. That is the whole of it.

What the app stores on your device

  • Which levels you have cleared, your stars and best scores
  • Your coin balance, lives and Dragon Run best score
  • Your settings — sound, music, haptics, colourblind mode, left-handed aim

This is written to the app's own storage and mirrored to your personal iCloud key-value store so it follows you between your own devices. It travels through Apple's iCloud under your Apple Account. We have no server and no copy of it. Deleting the app removes the local copy; removing the app's iCloud data is done through Apple's settings.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource, a Unity Technologies company) to show ads. To serve and measure an ad, the SDK may process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when asked
  • coarse device information — model, operating system version, language, and country
  • a general (non-precise) location derived from your IP address
  • which ads were shown, and whether they were viewed or tapped

If you decline the tracking request, ads still work; they are simply less relevant. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Unity's privacy policy is at unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Subscribers see no advertising at all, and the ad SDK is never started for them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We receive from Apple only whether a subscription is currently active — never your card details, billing address or Apple Account credentials. Purchase records live with Apple under your Apple Account.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not use precise location, the camera, the microphone, photos, or health data.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • We do not build advertising profiles ourselves, and we run no analytics of our own.

Children

Bubble Dragons is rated 4+ and contains no user-generated content, chat, or loot boxes. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app and remove its iCloud data in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud. To limit advertising use of your device identifier, decline the tracking request or turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted here with a new date, and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.
