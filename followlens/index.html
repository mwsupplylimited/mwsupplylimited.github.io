About

FollowLens turns Instagram's own “Download your information” export into a clear picture of who followed you, who unfollowed you, and who does not follow you back. You import the Followers and following file that Instagram gives you, and FollowLens compares each import to the ones before it — so every time you bring in a fresh export, you get a running history of what actually changed. Relationship comparisons run on your device from a file you already own. You enter a handle to label your library; FollowLens does not ask for your Instagram password, and it never logs in or connects to your account.

The big idea: Instagram lets you download your own data. FollowLens reads that export and compares one import against the next, so you can see new followers, unfollowers, and the gaps between who you follow and who follows you — without ever handing over your login.

What FollowLens shows you

  • New followers — accounts that started following you since your last import.
  • Unfollowers — accounts that followed you before and don't anymore.
  • Not following you back — people you follow who don't follow you back.
  • Fans — people who follow you that you don't follow back.
  • Mutuals — accounts where you follow each other.
  • Follower-count trends — how your totals move over time across your snapshots.

Each import becomes a dated snapshot. Keep importing over the weeks and months and FollowLens builds the timeline for you.

How to download your Instagram data

You get the file straight from Instagram — this is the only thing FollowLens needs. On the Instagram app or on the web:

  1. Open Accounts Center (Instagram → your profile → menu → Accounts Center).
  2. Tap Your information and permissions.
  3. Tap Export your information or Download your information. Instagram may vary these labels.
  4. Choose your account, then under the information to download, select “Followers and following” (you can deselect everything else to keep the file small).
  5. For format, choose JSON (recommended; HTML also works). Pick All time for the date range.
  6. Submit the request. Instagram prepares the file and emails you (or notifies you in-app) when it's ready to download — this can take from a few minutes to a while.
  7. Download the ZIP from Instagram, then open FollowLens and tap Import to select the file. FollowLens reads the followers and following lists and creates a snapshot.

Tip: Choose JSON as the format and include Followers and following — that's what FollowLens reads. To track changes, request a fresh export from Instagram every so often and import each one; FollowLens does the comparison automatically.

Getting started

  • Request your export from Instagram using the steps above and download the ZIP to your iPhone.
  • Open FollowLens and tap Import, then pick the file you downloaded.
  • Your first import becomes your baseline snapshot — the lists (one-way follows, fans, mutuals) are available right away.
  • Import again later with a newer export to reveal your new followers, unfollowers, and count trends between the two.

FAQ

Can I see results before requesting an export?

Yes. Choose Load sample data to explore the dashboard with fictional accounts. Your own insights begin when you import your export.

Does FollowLens monitor in real time?

No. Insights update when you import a fresh export. A missing account means it was absent from a later file; FollowLens cannot tell whether that was an unfollow, a renamed or deleted account, or a difference in the export. Use All time and include both relationship lists for reliable comparisons.

Do I need to log in to Instagram?

No. FollowLens never asks for your Instagram password and never signs in to your account. It works purely from the official “Followers and following” file you download from Instagram yourself.

Why import a file instead of connecting live?

Because it keeps you safe and in control. Using Instagram's own official data export means there's no login to hand over, and no scraping by FollowLens. You decide when to pull a fresh export, and FollowLens simply compares each one to the last to show you what changed.

How do I see changes over time?

Request a new export from Instagram whenever you want an update and import it. Each import is a dated snapshot; FollowLens compares the newest one to the previous ones so your new followers, unfollowers, and count trends appear automatically. The more often you import, the finer your history.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select FollowLens, and tap Cancel. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep Pro access until the current period ends. You can restore a purchase on a new device from the app's paywall or settings with Restore purchases.

Why do I need a new baseline after updating?

Starting with version 1.4, complete exports are required. Older versions could save an import with only one relationship list. Those snapshots are kept in your library and backups, but we cannot verify whether an empty list meant zero accounts or a missing file. Import a complete Followers and following export again to establish a reliable baseline. A later complete export reveals changes from that baseline.

Pro is missing after I paid.

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that bought Pro. A pending payment or Ask to Buy request must complete before access activates. If you were charged but still cannot restore, contact us with the purchase date and product name; never send card details. You can also check your receipt or request help through Apple's purchase support.
