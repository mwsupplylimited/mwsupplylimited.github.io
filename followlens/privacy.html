FollowLens Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026 · MW Supply Limited

Your relationship data stays under your control. FollowLens reads the official Instagram export you choose on your device. It never asks for your Instagram password. Banner advertising and Apple purchases involve third-party services, as explained below.

1. Imported relationship data

FollowLens processes follower and following handles, profile links, timestamps, and snapshot history locally to compare imports. We do not upload your imported lists to our own server or to the advertising provider. There is no FollowLens account system, hosted relationship database, or first-party analytics service.

FollowLens does not connect to your Instagram account or monitor it automatically. Results reflect the exports you import and cannot establish why a particular account is absent from a later export.

2. Local storage, backups, and sharing

Your imported history and app preferences are stored on your device. FollowLens does not provide automatic iCloud sync. If your device backup settings include app data, Apple may include this local data in your device backups.

You can create and restore a FollowLens backup at no extra charge, or export CSV lists with Pro. These files are created on your device. When you choose Files, iCloud Drive, email, or another destination in the share sheet, the receiving service handles the file under its own terms. Backups and CSVs contain relationship handles; share them only with destinations you trust. We do not receive a copy.

3. Advertising and your choices

Version 1.4 and later, including 1.4 TestFlight builds, may show banner ads through Unity LevelPlay and ironSource. These versions integrate the ironSource advertising network and remove Google AdMob. Pro removes the app's advertising placements. If you purchase Pro after ads have already started in the current session, previously sent ad requests cannot be recalled.

Advertising services may process IP addresses, device and app information, identifiers, diagnostics, and ad views or interactions to deliver and measure advertising and prevent fraud. An IP address can indicate an approximate location. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is available only if you grant Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Imported follower lists are not provided to the advertising SDK.

In version 1.4 and later, the app asks you to choose optional ad personalization before starting ads. You can choose limited ads and review the choice in Settings. Declining optional personalization does not mean all advertising data processing stops. Tracking permission is a separate iOS choice, available in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can use FollowLens without allowing tracking.

For the provider's practices and available access, deletion, or opt-out requests, read Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Earlier releases (1.3 and earlier) included Google AdMob and Google's consent messaging. Those SDKs may process device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising under your consent and iOS tracking choices. See Google's advertising practices and privacy policy. Updating the app replaces that advertising integration.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes purchases through your Apple Account. We do not receive your card details. FollowLens uses StoreKit to verify purchase transactions and determine Pro access. Monthly and annual subscriptions may include an introductory offer for eligible Apple Accounts; the purchase sheet shows the applicable terms and price.

Starting with version 1.4, basic purchase troubleshooting counters and the last event time are stored on your device. They contain app/build information and counts of checkout, product-loading, or restore outcomes, without card details or imported relationship lists. You can choose to share a diagnostic report from Settings; it is not uploaded automatically.

Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Restoring purchases restores eligible Pro access, not locally imported relationship history. Use a FollowLens backup to transfer that history.

5. Reminders and support

Optional import reminders are scheduled locally through iOS notifications. They do not fetch Instagram data. You can turn them off in FollowLens Settings or manage permission in iOS Settings.

If you email support, we receive the address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and resolve your request. Do not send your Instagram password or an entire data export unless you choose to share specific information needed for troubleshooting.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete an account and its local snapshots in FollowLens Settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data from that device. Copies you exported or saved in Files, iCloud Drive, device backups, email, or other services must be deleted separately through those services. Deleting app data does not cancel an Apple subscription.

7. Children

FollowLens is not directed at children under 13. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request.

8. Independent app

FollowLens is developed by MW Supply Limited and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta. “Instagram” describes the official export the app reads.

9. Changes and contact

We update this policy when the app's data practices change and revise the date above. Privacy questions and support requests: mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
