FollowLens Privacy Policy

Effective 13 July 2026 · Last updated 13 July 2026

Summary: FollowLens works entirely on the data you import — the official “Followers and following” file from Instagram's own “Download your information” export. There is no login, no password, and no scraping. The usernames from your export stay on your device and, for Pro subscribers, in your own private iCloud. We run no server and never receive this data. The free tier shows a small banner ad through Google AdMob, which may use a device identifier to serve ads — only with the consent iOS and the app ask you for, and you can always say no.

1. Information we collect

FollowLens itself does not ask for, collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information. There is no account system, no login, no user profile, and no first-party analytics. MW Supply Limited operates no server or backend — the app has nowhere to send your data, and we never receive it. The only data processing beyond your own device is done by the advertising provider on the free tier, described in section 6.

2. How FollowLens gets your data

FollowLens does not connect to Instagram, log in to your account, ask for your Instagram password, or scrape any website. Instead, you request your own data from Instagram using its official “Download your information” tool and hand the resulting “Followers and following” file to FollowLens. The app reads that file entirely on your device and compares successive imports to show you what changed — new followers, unfollowers, accounts that don't follow you back, accounts you don't follow back, mutuals, and follower-count trends over time.

3. Your followers & following data

The usernames and lists contained in the file you import are processed and stored on your device so the app can build your snapshots and insight lists. This data is never transmitted to MW Supply Limited or any third party — because we have no server, there is nowhere for it to go.

If you subscribe to FollowLens Pro and enable iCloud backup & sync, your snapshots are also stored in your own private iCloud database so they sync across your Apple devices. That data lives in your own iCloud account under your Apple ID, is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and is not accessible to us. You can turn iCloud off for the app in iOS Settings.

4. On-device settings and snapshots

Your preferences and your import history (the snapshots the app diffs to detect changes) are stored locally on your device. On the free tier this stays on the device only; on Pro, if you enable it, snapshots also sync through your private iCloud as described above. None of this is sent to us.

5. Exporting your own data

FollowLens Pro lets you export your lists to CSV. Those files are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them using the iOS share sheet. FollowLens does not upload exported files anywhere.

6. Advertising (free tier only)

The free tier of FollowLens shows a small anchored-adaptive banner provided by Google AdMob. If you subscribe to FollowLens Pro, the app shows no ads at all and the AdMob SDK is not started.

  • What AdMob may process: a device advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow it via the App Tracking Transparency prompt — together with coarse usage and diagnostic information (such as approximate device model, OS version, and ad-interaction data), used to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Google AdMob is the third party that receives this.
  • Your consent: before any identifier is used for ad personalisation, the app shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you still get ads — just non-personalised ones.
  • Managing your choices: you can change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Google's practices are described in Google's advertising policy and privacy policy.

7. Purchases

The FollowLens Pro subscription (Monthly and Annual, the latter with a 3-day free trial) is processed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. Purchase status is validated on-device using Apple's StoreKit; the app only learns whether your device currently has an active Pro entitlement. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled any time in your Apple Account settings.

8. Data deletion

You are always in control of your data. You can delete individual snapshots or all imported data from within the app at any time. Deleting the app from your device removes all of its on-device data. If you enabled Pro iCloud sync, you can also remove the app's data from your iCloud storage in iOS Settings. Because we hold none of your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Children

FollowLens is not directed at children under 13. The free tier uses Google AdMob with age-appropriate ad settings where required, and the app collects no personal data itself, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

10. Not affiliated with Instagram or Meta

FollowLens is an independent app developed by MW Supply Limited. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc. “Instagram” is a trademark of its respective owner and is used here only to describe the export file the app reads.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how FollowLens handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy labels for the affected version of the app.
