PostStash

Save Instagram photos, carousels, reels, and stories to your iPhone, iPad, and iCloud Drive. Version 1.0

About

PostStash extracts public Instagram media from a pasted URL and saves it to your Photos library and (optionally) to iCloud Drive at full resolution. Photos and carousels work without an account. Signing in with a throwaway Instagram account additionally unlocks videos, reels, stories, and the "back up my own account" feature.

Getting started

  • Copy a public Instagram link (post, carousel, or reel) and tap Paste — PostStash auto-detects new clipboard links on launch.
  • Tap Fetch. Photos and carousels save in seconds.
  • Tap an individual item to save it, or use Save All for the whole post.
  • For reels, videos, and stories, tap the account icon (top-left) and sign in. Use a throwaway Instagram account — replaying an authenticated session outside the official app can get an account flagged.
  • Back up your own account with the "Back up my account" button while signed in. It paginates your posts and current stories and skips anything already downloaded.

iCloud sync

When iCloud is signed in and the toggle in Settings → iCloud is on, every saved item is mirrored into iCloud Drive › PostStash › Downloads. Settings and your local download history sync across iPhone and iPad via iCloud Key-Value Storage.

FAQ

Why do I need to sign in for videos?

Instagram only exposes video URLs to authenticated web sessions. Logged-out viewers can read the cover image of a reel but not the video stream.

Does PostStash store my Instagram password?

No. Sign-in happens inside Instagram’s own web login page rendered in a WebView. PostStash never sees your password — it only reads the session cookie that Instagram itself sets after you log in, and stores it in the system’s persistent web data store. You can sign out at any time from the account menu.

Where are my downloads?

By default, in your Photos library (camera roll). Turn on Save into "Instagram Downloads" album in Settings to file them into a named album (this requires full Photos access). With iCloud backup enabled, copies also land in iCloud Drive › PostStash › Downloads.

Why does the "Account" icon look gray briefly after launch?

The persistent web cookie store reads back empty for a moment after a cold launch — PostStash auto-retries within half a second and the icon flips to green once your session is detected.

Is this app affiliated with Instagram or Meta?

No. PostStash is independent and not affiliated with Instagram, Meta, or any other platform.

Can I download other people’s posts?

Technically yes for public posts, but downloading content posted by other users may violate Instagram’s Terms and the original creator’s copyright. Only download media you have the right to save.
