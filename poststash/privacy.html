PostStash Privacy Policy

Effective 29 May 2026 · Last updated 29 May 2026

Summary: PostStash collects no personal data, has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. All processing happens on your device. The optional Instagram sign-in uses Instagram’s own login page and only stores the resulting session cookie locally.

1. Information we collect

None. PostStash does not ask for, collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information on any server. We operate no backend.

2. Information stored locally on your device

PostStash stores the following data on your device only, using iOS’s standard storage mechanisms, so the app remembers it between launches:

  • Your settings (auto-save, haptics, iCloud backup on/off, Photos album mode) — in UserDefaults and (when iCloud is enabled) NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore.
  • A local list of the items you’ve already saved (CDN filename or Instagram media id, source URL, thumbnail URL, date) so "skip already-downloaded" works during account backup — in UserDefaults and (when iCloud is enabled) NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore.
  • Downloaded media files in your Photos library and, if you enable iCloud backup, in your iCloud Drive › PostStash › Downloads folder.
  • Instagram session cookies (if you sign in) — stored in iOS’s persistent web data store (WKWebsiteDataStore) the same way Safari stores cookies. PostStash never sees your Instagram password.
  • Your Instagram username (if you signed in) — cached so the account menu can show which account is active. Removed when you sign out.

This data never leaves your device except via the iCloud mechanisms above, which are end-to-end synced by Apple between your devices. It is automatically removed if you delete the app, and you can clear the in-app history at any time using Download history › Clear.

3. Network activity

PostStash makes network requests only to Instagram and its CDNs (instagram.com, cdninstagram.com, fbcdn.net) in order to:

  • Load the public Instagram pages you ask it to extract media from.
  • Download the resulting media files for saving.
  • If you sign in, replay your session cookies on the same domains to fetch authenticated content (videos, reels, stories, your own posts).

PostStash makes no requests to any server operated by MW Supply Limited or any third party.

4. Third-party services

PostStash contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics frameworks, no advertising networks, and no crash reporting other than Apple’s first-party crash reporting (which is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and only runs if you have opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

5. iCloud

If you enable iCloud backup, your settings, download history, and downloaded media files are uploaded to your personal iCloud account using Apple’s standard iCloud Drive and iCloud Key-Value Storage APIs. MW Supply Limited has no access to your iCloud data. iCloud is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

6. Permissions

PostStash requests the following iOS permissions only when they are needed:

  • Photos — Add Only (default): the minimum permission needed to save a downloaded file to your camera roll. PostStash never asks for full read access unless you opt in to the "album" mode in Settings.
  • Photos — Read/Write (opt-in): only if you turn on the "Save into 'Instagram Downloads' album" setting, which requires full access to manage a named album.

7. Children

PostStash is rated 17+ on the App Store because downloaded content can come from public Instagram posts, which Instagram itself does not moderate for child-safety. The app collects no data of any kind from any user.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how PostStash handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy nutrition labels for the affected version of the app.

9. Contact
