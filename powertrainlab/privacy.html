Powertrain Lab Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026 · Applies to version 1.1.0

SupportPrivacy policy

Powertrain Lab processes motion and generates sound on your iPhone. There is no app account, advertising or analytics SDK, and the app does not upload your motion or location data to a server.

Your sessions stay local. Optional local drive recording in Developer mode is off by default and leaves the device only when you export it. Apple handles optional subscription purchases.

Motion and optional location

Motion sensors provide acceleration, rotation and orientation for the simulated engine and gearbox. Motion is required for a live driving session. Parked previews and Drive Lab work without starting sensors.

Location speed is on by default and can be turned off with Use optional location speed under Drive preferences. When needed, the app requests While Using the App permission. Location updates run only during a session when location is enabled. Apple’s location services provide observations that can include coordinates; the app discards coordinates, altitude and course and uses only speed, speed accuracy and timestamps to help its estimate. Coordinates are never recorded. These inputs are processed on your iPhone and are not uploaded by the app.

Location is optional. Turn it off before a session or change permission in iPhone Settings. Without usable location, estimates can drift or become unavailable. Active audible sessions can continue in the background, including with the screen locked, using background audio and location; Stop session ends motion and location updates. If sound pauses in the background, the session waits up to a minute for motion updates, up to a minute for a disconnected audio output (only with location speed on) or up to 15 minutes for a phone call, then stops by itself so location and motion do not keep running.

Sound choices and saved setups

The app stores on your iPhone your sound, listening-position and character choices, custom gear ratios, transmission mode, engine volume, shift haptics, the optional-location choice and audio preferences. It also stores the reason and time a session last stopped by itself, and the information that paces Pro pages and Apple’s rating request: when a Pro page was last shown or closed, when the last session ended and whether it ended while moving, and how many qualifying stops and invitations there have been. Named setups include a name you enter, a local identifier and the selected sound, character, gearbox, mode and volume. Active and archived setups remain on your iPhone when Pro ends. There is no developer-hosted storage or synchronization.

Each session requires a fresh parked baseline. Separately, the app saves what it learned about the phone mount: its tilt, the car’s forward direction and a few correction values, with no location, time or route. This speeds up alignment when the same mount is used the same way; the app checks it again while driving. Device backup settings can affect whether local preferences and setups are backed up and restored.

Daily allowance and time checks

The shared daily allowance (five minutes a day across all cars), its last verified UTC day and accounting state are stored in a device-only Keychain item, without cloud synchronization. Keychain state may survive reinstalling on the same device; reinstalling does not guarantee another allowance.

To verify the current UTC day, the app makes an HTTPS HEAD request to its GitHub Pages support page. This uses an ephemeral session without stored cookies or credentials, and sends no request body, query identifier, motion, location, purchase records or unique app identifier. The app reads the response’s date rather than trusting the device’s date setting. This is the only request the app itself makes to a web server.

The host receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address and standard networking headers. GitHub’s privacy and logging practices apply; Powertrain Lab does not control host-log retention. Remaining time works offline. After reopening the app, a connection is needed to confirm a later allowance day. Parked previews, Drive Lab and verified Pro do not depend on this time check.

Apple Watch companion

The companion exchanges simulated RPM, gear, session availability and manual-shift requests with your paired iPhone through Apple’s WatchConnectivity service. Short-lived request identifiers prevent duplicate or stale shifts. These messages do not include location, motion recordings or purchase records, and are not sent to a developer server. The watch app collects no data of its own. It displays the iPhone simulation and never measures or controls the vehicle.

Music and audio

The app mixes its generated sound with other audio using iOS audio services. Optional Lower other audio while the engine plays asks iOS to play other apps quieter while the engine plays. The app does not read music files, library details or account information, and does not control other apps’ playback.

Car photos and image credits

The car photos are freely licensed Wikimedia Commons images included in the app; they are not downloaded while you use it. Settings & help > About > Image credits lists each photo’s author, licence and source. Opening a licence or source link there opens your browser, under that website’s own privacy practices.

Exporting setups

Export all setups creates a JSON file with your active and archived setup names and settings. It does not include calibration, motion, location or purchase information. Export remains available without Pro. You choose the destination using iOS; that destination’s privacy practices apply. The app does not automatically send the file to support or a server.

Subscriptions and Apple

Apple handles purchase, billing and subscription management through your Apple Account. Powertrain Lab receives StoreKit product information and signed transaction information, including product and transaction identifiers and subscription status, to check Pro access on your device. The app does not receive payment-card details or send purchase records to a developer server.

Restore purchases asks Apple to synchronize your purchases and can require Apple Account authentication. Rating requests use Apple’s own rating prompt; any rating you give goes to Apple. App Store, location, permission and backup services operate under Apple’s policies and your device settings. Turning off renewal preserves access for its remaining valid period.

Optional local drive recording (Developer mode)

Developer mode, the last section of Settings & help, offers optional local drive recording for diagnostics. Turn on Record next drive locally while stopped, before calibration. Recording is off by default. Your choice is remembered across app launches until you change it while stopped. A log can include raw motion, inferred speed, calibration, drivetrain state and device/build diagnostics. Logs contain no coordinates or microphone audio, are excluded from device backup and are never uploaded automatically. Each log is limited to 2 GB; storage errors or the size limit end recording, and incomplete files remain marked incomplete. Stop session finishes a log; reopen Developer mode to export a saved log through the iOS share sheet. Exit developer mode does not change an active recording. An export destination has its own privacy practices.

Removing data

Deleting the app removes local settings, saved setups, recorded logs and other app files from the device. Device-only Keychain allowance data may remain on the same device. Deleting the app does not delete a file you exported elsewhere, change your device backups, cancel a subscription or delete Apple’s transaction records. Manage or cancel your subscription with Manage subscription in Settings & help or in Apple’s subscription settings.

When you contact support

If you choose to email support, your email provider delivers your address, message and any attachments you include. That correspondence is used to respond to and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, precise routes or unrelated sensitive information. The app never automatically attaches motion, location or diagnostic logs.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can ask about or request deletion of correspondence at mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.

This website

These support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, cookies, remote fonts or external assets to these pages. Visiting them still sends connection information to GitHub. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visits without signing in. This website processing is separate from the app’s local motion and sound processing.

See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of connection and usage information. Email and any destination you choose for an exported file are separate services with their own practices. Apple’s Standard Terms of Use apply to the app.
