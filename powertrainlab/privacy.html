Powertrain Lab Privacy Policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

SupportPrivacy policy

Powertrain Lab processes motion and generates sound on your iPhone. There is no app account, advertising or analytics SDK, and the app does not upload your driving inputs to a developer server.

Your sessions stay local. The app does not create drive recordings or export driving data. Apple handles optional subscription purchases.

Motion and optional location

Motion sensors provide acceleration, rotation and orientation for the simulated engine and gearbox. Motion is required for a live driving session. Sound previews work without starting sensors.

Location speed is on by default. The first time you calibrate with it on, iOS asks for While Using permission; you can decline, or turn off Use optional location speed on the setup screen before calibrating. If you allow location, Apple's location services provide observations that can include coordinates. The app discards coordinates, altitude and heading and uses only speed, accuracy and timestamps to help its estimate. These inputs are processed locally and are not uploaded by the app.

Location is optional. Turn it off before a session or change permission in iPhone Settings. Without usable location, estimates can drift or become unavailable. Stop session ends motion and location updates. The app asks only for While Using permission. Location updates continue in the background only during a session you started: while its engine sound is playing, or for up to 15 minutes while a phone call or other audio interruption has paused it; iOS shows its location indicator. Setup, and a session paused for any other reason, stops when the app goes to the background.

Sound choices and saved setups

The app saves sound and character choices, transmission mode, engine volume, shift-haptics and optional-location preferences locally. So a later drive in the same mount can start from the direction it learned, it also saves what it learned about your phone's mount: the phone's tilt in the mount, the car's forward direction in the phone's own coordinates and a few correction values (how precisely it is known, how many stretches of driving it was learned from, a vibration offset and how far the phone sits ahead of the turning point). This contains no location, time or route. It is replaced only when a later drive learns a precise, confirmed direction; there is no separate control to clear it. Like your other preferences it can be included in device backups, and deleting the app removes it. Named setups include a name you enter, a local identifier and the selected sound, character, mode and volume. Active and archived setups remain on your iPhone when Pro ends. There is no developer-hosted storage or synchronization.

The parked calibration itself is per session; a remembered mount direction is reused only when the phone's parked tilt matches the saved tilt (within about 3°), and it is checked again as you drive. Internal timing supports simulation and stale-input checks; it is not sent to a server. Device backup settings can affect whether local preferences and setups are backed up and restored.

Exporting setups

Export all setups creates a JSON file with your active and archived setup names and settings. It does not include motion, location, calibration or purchase data. Export remains available without Pro. Choose the destination yourself using iOS; that destination's privacy practices apply. The app does not automatically send the file to support or a server.

Subscriptions and Apple

Apple handles purchase, billing and subscription management through your Apple Account. Powertrain Lab receives StoreKit product information and signed transaction information, including product and transaction identifiers and subscription status, to check Pro access on your device. The app does not receive payment-card details or send purchase records to a developer server.

Restore purchases asks Apple to synchronize your purchases. It can require Apple Account authentication. App Store, location, permission and backup services operate under Apple's policies and your device settings. Turning off renewal preserves access for its remaining valid period.

Removing data

Deleting the app removes local settings, saved setups and other app files from the device. It does not delete a file you exported elsewhere, change your device backups, cancel a subscription or delete Apple's transaction records. Manage or cancel your subscription using Manage subscription in the Pro screen or Apple's subscription settings.

When you contact support

If you choose to email support, your email provider delivers your address, message and any attachments you include. That correspondence is used to respond and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, precise routes or unrelated sensitive information. The app never automatically attaches driving data.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can ask about or request deletion of correspondence at mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.

This website

These support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, cookies, remote fonts or external assets to these pages. Visiting them still sends connection information to GitHub. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, including visits without signing in. This website processing is separate from the app's local motion and sound processing.

See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of connection and usage information. Email and any destination you choose for an exported file are separate services with their own practices.

Changes to app data handling will be reflected here.
