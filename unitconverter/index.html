About

Unit Converter Pro converts 150+ units across 20+ categories with real-time results. Enter a value once and see every conversion instantly — no more switching back and forth. The free version is ad-supported; Unit Converter Pro removes all ads.

Getting started

  • Choose a category by tapping the horizontal category pills at the top — Length, Weight, Temperature and more.
  • Type a value in the input field. All conversions update in real time as you type.
  • Tap the source unit to change which unit you are converting from. The highlighted card is the source.
  • Tap the swap button to quickly reverse the direction of conversion.
  • Search for any unit using the magnifying glass icon — search within the current category or across all categories.
  • Favorite a conversion by tapping the star icon on any result card. Your favorites appear in the Favorites tab.
  • Browse history in the History tab to revisit recent conversions. Searchable and grouped by date.

FAQ

Does it work offline?

Yes. All unit conversions (Length, Weight, Temperature, Volume, Speed, and every other category) work entirely on your device with no internet connection required. Only currency-related features (if added in the future) would require connectivity.

How many units are supported?

Unit Converter Pro supports 150+ units across 20+ categories. This includes common units (meter, kilogram, Fahrenheit, gallon, PSI, etc.) as well as specialized units like Nautical Mile, Troy Ounce, Mach, Rankine, and Gal.

Is my data backed up?

Your conversion history and favorites are stored on your device and survive relaunches and updates. With Unit Converter Pro, your favorites and history sync across your iPhone and iPad through your own private iCloud, and are restored when you set up a new device. The free tier keeps the most recent 7 days of history.

Where is my data sent?

We run no servers and never receive your conversions. Everything stays on your device; with Unit Converter Pro, your favorites and history sync through your own private iCloud (Apple’s CloudKit) — it lives in your personal iCloud account, visible only to you, and we never receive it. Separately, in the free version ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, which collects limited device and ad-interaction data to do so; iOS asks for your tracking permission first. Declining costs you no features. Unit Converter Pro removes all ads entirely. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
