Unit Converter Pro Privacy Policy

Effective 18 August 2026 · Last updated 18 August 2026

Summary: Your conversions are never sent to us. In the free version they stay on your device; with Unit Converter Pro, your history, favourites and settings also sync through your own private iCloud (Apple's CloudKit), staying in your personal iCloud account. The free version shows ads served through Unity LevelPlay, which collects limited device and ad-interaction data to do so. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask your permission first, in our own dialog, and declining costs you no features. Upgrading to Unit Converter Pro removes all ads. We have no account system and run no servers of our own.

1. Who we are

Unit Converter Pro is developed by MW Supply Limited (“we”). This policy explains what stays on your device and what is processed by the limited third-party services we use to show ads (free version) and to offer an optional Pro upgrade. The app has no account system and no backend — you can use every feature without signing in to us.

2. Information stored on your device

Unit Converter Pro stores the following on your device so the app remembers it between launches:

  • Your conversion history, stored with Apple's on-device SwiftData framework.
  • Your favourite conversions for quick access.
  • Your preferred decimal precision setting.

This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed entirely if you delete the app.

iCloud sync (Unit Converter Pro). If you subscribe to Pro, your conversion history, favourites and settings are also synced through your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so they stay in sync across your iPhone and iPad and are restored when you set up a new device. This data lives in your personal iCloud account under your Apple ID — it is visible only to you, handled under Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy, and we never receive it or have access to it. Nothing is synced to iCloud in the free version.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version of Unit Converter Pro is supported by ads served through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), a mediation service that runs an auction between advertising networks. Ads appear as a banner at the bottom of the screen and an occasional full-screen interstitial, shown only when you switch between categories — never while you are in the middle of a conversion, and at most a few times a day.

To serve these, our advertising partner may collect and process information including:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking when iOS asks (see below);
  • A non-personal device/usage identifier, device type, operating-system version and settings;
  • Your IP address and the approximate (coarse) region derived from it;
  • Information about how you interact with ads (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped).

This information is collected and used by Unity, not by us, under Unity's Player and App User Privacy Policy. MW Supply Limited only receives aggregate, non-identifying reports such as ad impressions and estimated revenue. Your conversions are never shared with any advertiser or used to target ads.

App Tracking Transparency. iOS asks whether Unit Converter Pro may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” the advertising identifier is not used. You can use the app fully either way.

Your consent choice (EEA, UK & Switzerland). Where the law requires your prior permission, Unit Converter Pro asks for it directly, in its own dialog, before any ad is requested. Both answers are presented with equal weight, nothing is pre-selected, and declining costs you nothing: you keep every feature and still see ads — they are simply generic rather than personalized. You can change your answer at any time in the app's Settings, as easily as you gave it. If you never answer, we treat that as “no.”

Removing ads. Upgrading to Unit Converter Pro removes all ad formats entirely. While you are a Pro member the app does not even start the advertising SDK, let alone request or display an ad.

4. Unit Converter Pro & payments

Unit Converter Pro removes all ads and unlocks the full conversion experience. It is available as an auto-renewable subscription, with monthly and annual options. Purchases, billing, renewals, and refunds are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit — we never see or store your name, card number, or Apple ID. To unlock Pro, the app checks your purchase entitlement directly with the App Store on your device; this check does not identify you to us. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

5. Network activity

Apart from the advertising and purchase services described above — and, for Pro subscribers, syncing your data to your own private iCloud as described in section 2 — Unit Converter Pro makes no network requests of its own and has no servers to talk to. Your conversions are never transmitted to us, and in the free version they never leave your device.

6. Third-party services

To show ads in the free version, Unit Converter Pro includes the Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation SDK and the Unity Ads adapter it auctions through. These are governed by Unity's Player and App User Privacy Policy. The consent prompt described in section 3 is our own — it is part of Unit Converter Pro, not a third-party SDK, so no additional company is involved in asking you.

Unit Converter Pro contains no analytics frameworks and no crash reporting other than Apple's own first-party reporting (governed by Apple's privacy policy, and only if you opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements). Pro members are not shown ads, and the app does not request ads for them.

7. Permissions and identifiers

Unit Converter Pro does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or health data. The advertising identifier is used only for ads in the free version, and only if you allow tracking as described in section 3.

8. Children

Unit Converter Pro is a general-audience utility rated 4+ on the App Store, and is declared to our advertising partner as not directed to children. Because the free version shows ads, we use the ad-quality controls our partner provides: served creatives are screened for adult, gambling, alcohol, drug and dating content, and flagged advertisers are blocked across our apps. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Upgrading to Pro removes all ads.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how Unit Converter Pro handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy nutrition labels for the affected version.

10. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
