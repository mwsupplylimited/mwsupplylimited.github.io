Windrose Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

In short: Windrose does not collect, store, or sell your personal information. There are no accounts and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Your location is used only to fetch weather for you and is never tied to your identity.

Information we do not collect

Windrose has no user accounts and no servers that store your data. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, device identifiers for tracking, browsing history, or any analytics about how you use the app. We do not use third-party advertising networks.

Location

If you grant location access, Windrose uses your device's location to show the weather, radar and conditions where you are. To retrieve a forecast for a point, your approximate coordinates (or the coordinates of a place you search for) are sent to our weather data providers (listed below) as part of an ordinary weather request. These coordinates are not stored by us, are not linked to your identity, and are never used for advertising or tracking. You can use the app without granting location access by searching for places by name. You can change or revoke location permission anytime in iOS Settings.

Data stored on your device

Your saved places, app settings, and the most recent forecast (cached so the app works briefly offline) are stored only on your device. Removing the app deletes this data.

Weather data providers

Windrose retrieves weather information from the following third-party services. Requests contain only the location and parameters needed to return weather data; they do not contain personal information about you. Each provider may log technical request metadata (such as IP address) under its own privacy policy:

  • Open-Meteo — forecast, marine and air-quality data.
  • RainViewer — precipitation radar and satellite imagery.
  • NOAA Aviation Weather Center — aviation reports (METAR/TAF/SIGMET).
  • U.S. National Weather Service (NOAA) — severe-weather alerts.

Subscriptions

Windrose Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple handles billing, renewals and receipts in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Windrose is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will be reflected in a future app update.
