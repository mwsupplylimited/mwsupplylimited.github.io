Windrose Privacy Policy

Windrose 1.4 · Last updated: 10 September 2026

Summary: This policy describes Windrose 1.4. Windrose has no app account system. It uses your location to fetch weather and stores settings and places on your device, with iCloud syncing when available. TelemetryDeck receives app-usage, subscription and advertising events for analytics. The free plan uses Unity Ads, which may collect device and ad-interaction data. The optional Windrose Pro subscription removes ad placements when the app recognizes the active subscription; advertising startup and previously transmitted data are explained below.

Information we do not collect

Windrose has no app account system, and MW Supply Limited does not operate a server to store your saved places or settings. We do not collect your name, email, contacts or browsing history through the app. Weather providers, Apple’s map, geocoding and iCloud services, the analytics service and the advertising provider receive data as described below.

Location

If you grant location access, Windrose uses your device's location to show the weather, radar and conditions where you are. To retrieve a forecast for a point, Windrose sends latitude and longitude to the weather data providers listed below. These coordinates can be precise when you grant Precise Location access; requests for a searched or saved place use that place’s coordinates. We do not operate a server that stores these weather requests, and the app does not send these coordinates to its advertising or analytics SDKs. Saved and recent places, including their coordinates, may sync through your Apple Account as described below. You can use the app without granting location access by searching for places by name. You can change or revoke location permission anytime in iOS Settings.

Data stored on your device and in iCloud

Windrose stores saved and recent places, Home and Work locations, app settings, earned perk status and cached weather data on your device. When iCloud is available, the app also uses Apple’s iCloud key-value storage to sync places, Home and Work locations, selected settings and earned perk status across devices using the same Apple Account. Place records include names and coordinates. The cached weather forecast is not included in this sync.

Deleting the app removes its local data, but does not by itself delete data already stored in iCloud or on your other devices. Synced data may return when you reinstall the app using the same Apple Account. Apple handles iCloud data under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Weather, map and place-name providers

Windrose retrieves weather information, maps and place names from the services below. Requests include the coordinates or place-search terms and parameters needed to return the requested data; Windrose does not attach an app account identifier. Each provider may log technical request metadata (such as IP address) under its own privacy policy. Map and radar requests describe the viewed area, which may be different from your device’s position:

  • Open-Meteo — forecast, marine, air-quality and ensemble data, winds and temperatures aloft, and place-name search.
  • Apple MapKit and geocoding services — base maps and conversion of coordinates into a place name. Reverse geocoding can send your coordinates to Apple’s network service; it is not guaranteed to happen only on your device. Map requests describe the area you view. Apple handles these services under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • RainViewer — precipitation radar imagery.
  • NASA GIBS — satellite cloud imagery (NOAA GOES and JMA Himawari).
  • NOAA Aviation Weather Center — aviation reports (METAR/TAF/SIGMET).
  • EUMETSAT — Meteosat satellite cloud imagery for Europe, Africa, the Middle East and western Asia.
  • MeteoAlarm (EUMETNET) — official severe-weather warnings issued by the European national meteorological services.
  • U.S. National Hurricane Center (NOAA) — Atlantic and Pacific tropical-cyclone tracks and advisories.
  • U.S. National Weather Service (NOAA) — severe-weather alerts.
  • Hong Kong Observatory — Hong Kong warnings and typhoon signals.
  • Japan Meteorological Agency and the U.S. Navy’s Joint Typhoon Warning Center — tropical-cyclone tracks and forecast cones.
  • GitHub Pages — the pressure map’s surface-pressure grid (derived from NOAA GFS) is published as a static file on GitHub’s hosting and downloaded once per session. GitHub may log the request under its own privacy statement.

Subscriptions

Windrose Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Windrose reads verified subscription transactions and renewal information to unlock Pro and display your plan. We do not receive your payment-card details. Subscription events and product details are sent to analytics as described below. Apple handles billing, renewals and receipts in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

Windrose 1.4 starts TelemetryDeck at app launch on both free and Pro plans, without a separate in-app analytics switch. The app sends events so we can understand feature use, reliability, subscriptions and advertising performance. These include:

  • App and dashboard opens, feature and paywall interactions, permission outcomes and forecast or map-loading outcomes.
  • Checkout attempts and outcomes, purchases, restores, trial starts and conversions, renewals, plan changes, billing problems, expiry and auto-renewal changes. Depending on the event, details include the product identifier, monthly or annual plan, listed product price and currency, trial eligibility or length, and a billing-period count category.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, earned rewards, tracking-permission outcomes and ad-traffic protection events. Depending on the event, details include the ad format, placement, network, reward type and interaction counts.

The TelemetryDeck SDK includes technical metadata such as app and operating-system versions, a session identifier and a hashed device-derived identifier for counting use across events. This is event-level data used to produce analytics reports, rather than only aggregate counts sent from your device. Windrose does not supply TelemetryDeck with your name, email, saved places, weather coordinates or advertising identifier. See the TelemetryDeck Privacy Policy and its event-data documentation.

Advertising (free plan) and App Tracking Transparency

On the free plan, Windrose shows ads using the Unity Ads SDK (Unity Technologies): a banner within the weather dashboard and an optional short video ad you can choose to watch to unlock extra features for a day. To select, deliver, measure and cap the frequency of these ads and to prevent fraud, the Unity SDK may collect device and usage information — including a device advertising identifier (IDFA, only with your consent), an IP address (and the coarse region derived from it), and information about your interaction with the ads.

When advertising is prepared, Windrose may request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission. iOS determines whether the prompt can appear, based on your existing choice and system restrictions:

  • If you allow tracking, the advertising identifier may be used to show you personalized ads, which can involve combining this data with data from other apps and websites for advertising — “tracking” as Apple defines it.
  • If you ask the app not to track (or take no action), you still see ads, but non-personalized ones, and the advertising identifier is not used to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Unity Technologies processes advertising data under the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Windrose also receives ad callbacks and forwards advertising interaction events to TelemetryDeck for our analytics, as described above. You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Windrose Pro removes ad placements when the app recognizes the active subscription. In version 1.4, an advertising startup already in progress can still request tracking permission or start the SDK while subscription status is being resolved. Becoming Pro does not undo an SDK startup or retract data already transmitted. Windrose does not pass weather coordinates to its advertising layer; any coarse, IP-derived region used in ad serving is handled by Unity as described above.

Children

Windrose is not directed at children. Ads on the free plan are served by Unity; when tracking permission has not been granted, the app explicitly requests non-personalized ads only.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will be reflected in a future app update.
