Current App Store release — Windrose 1.4

Windrose 1.4 · Last updated: 10 September 2026

Summary: This policy describes Windrose 1.4. Windrose has no app account system. It uses your location to fetch weather and stores settings and places on your device, with iCloud syncing when available. TelemetryDeck receives app-usage, subscription and advertising events for analytics. The free plan uses Unity Ads, which may collect device and ad-interaction data. The optional Windrose Pro subscription removes ad placements when the app recognizes the active subscription; advertising startup and previously transmitted data are explained below.

Information we do not collect

Windrose has no app account system, and MW Supply Limited does not operate a server to store your saved places or settings. We do not collect your name, email, contacts or browsing history through the app. Weather providers, Apple’s map, geocoding and iCloud services, the analytics service and the advertising provider receive data as described below.

Location

If you grant location access, Windrose uses your device's location to show the weather, radar and conditions where you are. To retrieve a forecast for a point, Windrose sends latitude and longitude to the weather data providers listed below. These coordinates can be precise when you grant Precise Location access; requests for a searched or saved place use that place’s coordinates. We do not operate a server that stores these weather requests, and the app does not send these coordinates to its advertising or analytics SDKs. Saved and recent places, including their coordinates, may sync through your Apple Account as described below. You can use the app without granting location access by searching for places by name. You can change or revoke location permission anytime in iOS Settings.

Data stored on your device and in iCloud

Windrose stores saved and recent places, Home and Work locations, app settings, earned perk status and cached weather data on your device. When iCloud is available, the app also uses Apple’s iCloud key-value storage to sync places, Home and Work locations, selected settings and earned perk status across devices using the same Apple Account. Place records include names and coordinates. The cached weather forecast is not included in this sync.

Deleting the app removes its local data, but does not by itself delete data already stored in iCloud or on your other devices. Synced data may return when you reinstall the app using the same Apple Account. Apple handles iCloud data under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Weather, map and place-name providers

Windrose retrieves weather information, maps and place names from the services below. Requests include the coordinates or place-search terms and parameters needed to return the requested data; Windrose does not attach an app account identifier. Each provider may log technical request metadata (such as IP address) under its own privacy policy. Map and radar requests describe the viewed area, which may be different from your device’s position:

  • Open-Meteo — forecast, marine, air-quality and ensemble data, winds and temperatures aloft, and place-name search.
  • Apple MapKit and geocoding services — base maps and conversion of coordinates into a place name. Reverse geocoding can send your coordinates to Apple’s network service; it is not guaranteed to happen only on your device. Map requests describe the area you view. Apple handles these services under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • RainViewer — precipitation radar imagery.
  • NASA GIBS — satellite cloud imagery (NOAA GOES and JMA Himawari).
  • NOAA Aviation Weather Center — aviation reports (METAR/TAF/SIGMET).
  • EUMETSAT — Meteosat satellite cloud imagery for Europe, Africa, the Middle East and western Asia.
  • MeteoAlarm (EUMETNET) — official severe-weather warnings issued by the European national meteorological services.
  • U.S. National Hurricane Center (NOAA) — Atlantic and Pacific tropical-cyclone tracks and advisories.
  • U.S. National Weather Service (NOAA) — severe-weather alerts.
  • Hong Kong Observatory — Hong Kong warnings and typhoon signals.
  • Japan Meteorological Agency and the U.S. Navy’s Joint Typhoon Warning Center — tropical-cyclone tracks and forecast cones.
  • GitHub Pages — the pressure map’s surface-pressure grid (derived from NOAA GFS) is published as a static file on GitHub’s hosting and downloaded once per session. GitHub may log the request under its own privacy statement.

Subscriptions

Windrose Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Windrose reads verified subscription transactions and renewal information to unlock Pro and display your plan. We do not receive your payment-card details. Subscription events and product details are sent to analytics as described below. Apple handles billing, renewals and receipts in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

Windrose 1.4 starts TelemetryDeck at app launch on both free and Pro plans, without a separate in-app analytics switch. The app sends events so we can understand feature use, reliability, subscriptions and advertising performance. These include:

  • App and dashboard opens, feature and paywall interactions, permission outcomes and forecast or map-loading outcomes.
  • Checkout attempts and outcomes, purchases, restores, trial starts and conversions, renewals, plan changes, billing problems, expiry and auto-renewal changes. Depending on the event, details include the product identifier, monthly or annual plan, listed product price and currency, trial eligibility or length, and a billing-period count category.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, earned rewards, tracking-permission outcomes and ad-traffic protection events. Depending on the event, details include the ad format, placement, network, reward type and interaction counts.

The TelemetryDeck SDK includes technical metadata such as app and operating-system versions, a session identifier and a hashed device-derived identifier for counting use across events. This is event-level data used to produce analytics reports, rather than only aggregate counts sent from your device. Windrose does not supply TelemetryDeck with your name, email, saved places, weather coordinates or advertising identifier. See the TelemetryDeck Privacy Policy and its event-data documentation.

Advertising (free plan) and App Tracking Transparency

On the free plan, Windrose shows ads using the Unity Ads SDK (Unity Technologies): a banner within the weather dashboard and an optional short video ad you can choose to watch to unlock extra features for a day. To select, deliver, measure and cap the frequency of these ads and to prevent fraud, the Unity SDK may collect device and usage information — including a device advertising identifier (IDFA, only with your consent), an IP address (and the coarse region derived from it), and information about your interaction with the ads.

When advertising is prepared, Windrose may request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission. iOS determines whether the prompt can appear, based on your existing choice and system restrictions:

  • If you allow tracking, the advertising identifier may be used to show you personalized ads, which can involve combining this data with data from other apps and websites for advertising — “tracking” as Apple defines it.
  • If you ask the app not to track (or take no action), you still see ads, but non-personalized ones, and the advertising identifier is not used to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Unity Technologies processes advertising data under the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Windrose also receives ad callbacks and forwards advertising interaction events to TelemetryDeck for our analytics, as described above. You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Windrose Pro removes ad placements when the app recognizes the active subscription. In version 1.4, an advertising startup already in progress can still request tracking permission or start the SDK while subscription status is being resolved. Becoming Pro does not undo an SDK startup or retract data already transmitted. Windrose does not pass weather coordinates to its advertising layer; any coarse, IP-derived region used in ad serving is handled by Unity as described above.

Children

Windrose is not directed at children. Ads on the free plan are served by Unity; when tracking permission has not been granted, the app explicitly requests non-personalized ads only.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will be reflected in a future app update.

Internal TestFlight beta — Windrose 1.5

Beta 1.5.0 · Last updated: 10 September 2026

These disclosures apply to the 1.5 beta only. Version 1.4 continues to use the storage, analytics and advertising integrations described in its own policy above.

Windrose has no app account system. Weather requests go to the services needed to return your chosen forecast or map. Places and preferences are stored locally, with private iCloud syncing when available. This beta does not request real ads. Its optional advertising choices both start off; the advertising section explains the candidate integration and distribution limits.

Location and weather requests

When you grant location permission, Windrose uses your coordinates to request nearby weather. Coordinates can be precise when Precise Location is enabled. If you search for or save a place, requests use that place’s coordinates. You can use manual place search without granting device-location access. Optional background following requires separate permission; you can change it in iOS Settings.

Requests can include latitude and longitude, place-search text, an airport or region, map tiles, time and model parameters. Providers receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and may retain request logs. Open-Meteo describes IP and URL logging, potentially including coordinates, and its retention practices in its terms and privacy information. Windrose does not attach an advertising identifier to weather requests and does not send saved places or weather coordinates to its advertising service.

Your device, iCloud and backups

Windrose stores saved and recent places, Home and Work labels, preferences, permission and advertising choices, earned perks and weather caches on your device. The widget uses a local shared snapshot. Windrose does not operate a developer server that stores your place collection.

When iCloud is available, saved places and Home/Work use encrypted place fields in a private CloudKit database belonging to your Apple Account. These records include names, coordinates, ordering, revisions and deletion state. They remain associated with the Apple Account used for syncing; encryption does not make them anonymous. Selected display, unit, forecast-model and radar preferences sync separately through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. Current permission choices, advertising consent, recent places and earned perk status are not newly synced by version 1.5. Pro access is verified through the App Store.

Switching iCloud accounts pauses place uploads until you choose whether to keep the collection locally or import it into the current account. Earlier versions stored places and additional settings in iCloud key-value storage; version 1.5 preserves recovery information while migrating this legacy data. Older installations and other devices can still retain their copies.

Settings → Backup & Sync offers export, import and available local recovery controls. Exports contain place names, exact coordinates, Home/Work and selected preferences. Windrose does not password-encrypt exported JSON files. Store and share them carefully.

Deleting a place removes it from the active collection and syncs that deletion when synchronization succeeds. Protected local recovery snapshots and sync deletion records can retain previous information. Removing the app does not erase every iCloud copy, other-device copy, provider log or file you exported. Synced places can return after reinstalling. Apple handles its services under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Services used for weather and maps

  • Open-Meteo: forecasts, marine and air-quality models, ensemble rain estimates, upper-air fields and place search.
  • RainViewer: precipitation radar imagery.
  • NASA GIBS and EUMETSAT: infrared satellite imagery.
  • NOAA Aviation Weather Center: aviation reports and advisories.
  • U.S. National Weather Service, Hong Kong Observatory and MeteoAlarm / EUMETNET: supported official warnings.
  • NOAA National Hurricane Center, Japan Meteorological Agency and the Joint Typhoon Warning Center: supported tropical-cyclone information.
  • Apple MapKit and geocoding services: base maps and conversion of coordinates into a place name. Reverse geocoding can send your coordinates to Apple’s network service; it is not guaranteed to happen only on your device. Map requests describe the area you view. Apple handles these services under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub-hosted public files: the NOAA GFS-derived pressure grid and airport catalogue. Hosting requests are subject to GitHub’s privacy statement.

A viewed map area does not necessarily indicate your device’s location. A provider may process request metadata under its own policy; an app display or cache does not make those requests on-device processing.

Optional advertising and tracking

Internal TestFlight builds do not initialize an advertising SDK or request real ads. Any test ad layout is a local, noninteractive placeholder. The optional LevelPlay integration and choices described below are implemented in the 1.5 candidate for eligible App Store distribution; they do not mean that this beta has served production ads or transmitted advertising data.

The free plan supports an inline weather-dashboard banner and optional video rewards using Unity LevelPlay with ironSource advertising and its included Ad Quality service. To deliver and measure ads, protect against fraud and diagnose service performance, these services may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, ad information and diagnostics.

The provider may infer an approximate city, state or country from IP. Its privacy policy describes geolocation sharing with ad networks for cross-context advertising. This is separate from Windrose’s precise weather-coordinate requests. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for its processing and privacy-rights controls.

In Settings → Ad Privacy, Allow device data for advertising and Allow personalized advertising both start off. The first is permission for advertising device-data access; the second is a separate choice about using and sharing advertising data to select ads based on interests. Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission is also required before Windrose starts advertising. If it is denied, restricted or undecided, Windrose requests no ads. Weather and navigation remain usable.

You can change the app choices at any time. If advertising is eligible to start and personalization is off, Windrose sends the provider a no-consent and do-not-sell-or-share signal. Turning off device data stops new ad requests and removes displayed banners. Change Apple’s tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Windrose Pro removes ads. When Pro is verified, Windrose stops requesting and showing ads. If an advertising service was already started before a purchase or consent change, stopping new ads cannot retract data previously transmitted or guarantee that every in-progress vendor diagnostic operation is recalled. Use the provider’s privacy controls for requests about data it already holds.

Analytics and diagnostics

Version 1.5 removes the standalone TelemetryDeck app-usage analytics integration and its outgoing event path. It deletes the known local pending-event cache when accessible. It does not send a replacement stream of app-usage or purchase events to a developer analytics server.

Advertising measurement and Ad Quality diagnostics remain part of the optional advertising service described above. Weather providers also receive technical request data. Version 1.4 used TelemetryDeck on free and Pro plans for app-usage, subscription-lifecycle and advertising-interaction events, with technical metadata, session identifiers and a hashed device-derived identifier. It also used standalone Unity Ads; an advertising startup already in progress could continue while Pro status was being resolved. Data already transmitted by earlier versions remains subject to the providers’ policies, including TelemetryDeck’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes Windrose Pro purchases. Windrose reads product prices, verified subscription transactions and renewal information to show your plan and unlock features. It does not receive your payment-card details, Apple Account password or Apple Account email. Version 1.5 does not send purchase transaction identifiers to a developer analytics server.

Manage billing and cancellation through the App Store or Settings → Windrose Pro → Manage/Cancel Subscription. Purchases are governed by the standard Apple terms of use.

Support and sharing

If you email support, we receive the sender address and whatever content you choose to include, and use it to handle your request. An ad report is optional and includes basic app/ad context, not weather coordinates or purchase credentials. System sharing sends the chosen forecast image or backup to the recipient or service you select; it is not an automatic upload to us.

Windrose is a general-audience weather app and is not designed specifically for children. Please contact us if you believe a child supplied personal information through a support request.

For data processed by Apple or an advertising or weather provider, its linked policy explains available rights and contact methods.
