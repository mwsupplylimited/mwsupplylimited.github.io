Where the data comes from

No proprietary model, no invented precision — Windrose is honest about its sources.

  • Forecasts, marine and air-quality data: Open-Meteo
  • Precipitation radar and satellite imagery: RainViewer
  • Aviation reports (METAR/TAF/SIGMET): NOAA Aviation Weather Center
  • Warnings: the Hong Kong Observatory and the US National Weather Service
  • Tropical-cyclone tracks: the Japan Meteorological Agency and the Joint Typhoon Warning Center

Important: Windrose is an informational aid and a supplement to — not a replacement for — official sources. Do not use it as the primary means of weather decision-making for flight, marine navigation, or any safety-critical activity. Always consult certified briefings and official warnings.

Help & frequently asked questions

How do I use the radar?

Open the Radar tab and press play to animate the loop. Drag the timeline to scrub to any moment, switch colour schemes in the settings menu, and pinch to zoom right in on the rain. With Pro, the timeline continues past “now” into the 3-hour rain forecast.

Is the radar available everywhere?

The precipitation radar is a global composite and covers most populated areas. Coverage and resolution vary by region; when you zoom in very close the radar is scaled up, so it looks blockier the further you zoom.

Why does Windrose ask for my location?

Your location is used to show the weather, radar and conditions where you are. Your coordinates are sent to the weather data providers above only to retrieve the forecast for that spot — they are never stored on our servers, linked to your identity, or used for advertising. You can also just search for places by name.

Is marine data available inland?

No — marine data (waves, swell, sea state, currents) is only available for coastal and offshore locations. Inland, Windrose will tell you so. Windrose does not currently include a tide model.

How accurate is the aviation data?

METARs, TAFs and SIGMETs come directly from the NOAA Aviation Weather Center; winds aloft are computed from numerical weather models. Windrose is for situational awareness only and is not a certified source for flight planning. Always obtain an official weather briefing.

Which tropical cyclones does it track?

Storm tracks, forecast cones and development areas come from the Japan Meteorological Agency and the Joint Typhoon Warning Center, which cover the West Pacific basin. Storms in other basins are not currently tracked.

Are there ads?

On the free plan, yes — one banner on the weather screen, and an optional short video you can choose to watch to unlock extra features for a day. There are never ads on the radar, and never a full-screen ad when you open the app. Windrose Pro removes all of them. Ads are served by Unity Ads; see the Privacy Policy for what they collect.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes — Windrose is a universal app for iPhone and iPad.

I subscribed but Pro isn't showing up.

Tap Restore Purchases on the Membership screen or the Pro screen — your subscription is tied to your Apple Account, so it follows you to a new device or a reinstall.

How do I cancel Windrose Pro?

Manage or cancel anytime on your device under Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Your Pro features stay active until the end of the period you have already paid for.
