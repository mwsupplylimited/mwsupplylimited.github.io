Windrose

Live precipitation radar and complete weather for everyone — on land, at sea, and in the air. One app, three modes: General, Marine and Aviation. Version 1.0

What it does

Windrose shows you the weather the way you need to see it. Watch an animated precipitation radar loop to see exactly where the rain is and where it's heading. Get a clear, colour-coded safety summary for wherever you are. And switch between three modes built for three very different people:

  • General — current conditions, a friendly “will the rain reach you?” nowcast, hourly and multi-day forecasts, UV, air quality and pollen, sunrise & sunset.
  • Marine — wave height, swell, sea state, sea-surface temperature, currents and wind for boating and sailing.
  • Aviation — decoded METARs and TAFs, colour-coded flight categories for nearby airports, winds and temperatures aloft, SIGMET hazards and a density-altitude calculator. Built for general-aviation pilots who don't have weather radar in the cockpit.

Important: Windrose is an informational aid and a supplement to — not a replacement for — official sources. Do not use it as the primary means of weather decision-making for flight, marine navigation, or any safety-critical activity. Always consult certified briefings and official warnings.

How to use it

  • Pick your mode. Tap General, Marine or Aviation at the top to reshape the dashboard for what you're doing.
  • Watch the radar. Open the Radar tab and press play to animate the precipitation loop. Scrub the timeline, switch colour schemes, and zoom right in on the rain. The radar is free for everyone.
  • Add places. Search any city or place and switch between your saved locations. Your current location is detected automatically.
  • Read the safety line. The colour-coded summary at the top of each mode tells you, in one sentence, whether conditions are good, need caution, or are dangerous.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the radar and weather data come from?

Forecasts, marine and air-quality data come from Open-Meteo; the live precipitation radar and satellite imagery from RainViewer; aviation reports (METAR/TAF/SIGMET) from the NOAA Aviation Weather Center; and U.S. severe-weather alerts from the National Weather Service.

Why does it ask for my location?

Your location is used to show the weather, radar and conditions where you are. Your coordinates are sent to the weather data providers above only to retrieve the forecast for that spot — they are never stored on our servers, linked to your identity, or used for advertising. You can also just search for places by name.

Is the radar available everywhere?

The precipitation radar is a global composite and covers most populated areas, and it's free for everyone. Coverage and resolution vary by region; when you zoom in very close the radar is scaled up, so it looks blockier the further you zoom.

Is Marine data available inland?

No — marine data (waves, swell, sea state) is only available for coastal and offshore locations. Inland, Windrose will let you know.

How accurate is the aviation data?

METARs, TAFs and SIGMETs come directly from the NOAA Aviation Weather Center; winds aloft are computed from numerical weather models. Windrose is for situational awareness only and is not a certified source for flight planning. Always obtain an official weather briefing.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes — Windrose works on iPhone and iPad.

How do I cancel Windrose Pro?

Manage or cancel anytime on your device under Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Your Pro features stay active until the end of the period you've already paid for.
