PDF Scanner

Scan, OCR, sign and edit PDFs on iPhone & iPad. Version 1.0.9

About

PDF Scanner turns your camera into a fast, accurate document scanner and gives you a full PDF editor in the same app. Scans are automatically OCR’d so you can search every word — even printouts and your own notes — and the editor lets you enhance pages, reorder them, mark them up, add text, redact, drop in a signature, and fill PDF form fields. Documents stay on your device and in your own iCloud Drive. PDF Scanner is free to use; an optional Pro upgrade removes ads and the export watermark, unlocks every page and opens up the full editor. The free tier shows ads — served by Google AdMob up to version 1.0.8, and by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) from version 1.0.9 (see the Privacy Policy).

Getting started

  • Tap Scan Document at the bottom of the Documents screen to open the camera scanner. Capture as many pages as you like, then tap Save. PDF Scanner auto-runs OCR so the resulting PDF is fully searchable in the app, in Files, and in Preview.
  • Tap Import (top toolbar) to bring in any existing PDF from Files or iCloud Drive.
  • Open a document and use the bottom bar to switch between View, Enhance, Pages, Markup, Sign, and Forms.
  • Search inside a document by tapping the magnifying-glass in the top toolbar while in View mode. Matches are highlighted; use the chevrons to step through them.
  • Search and sort the document list from the Documents screen using the search field at the top and the Sort menu.

FAQ

Does scanning work on iPad?

Yes. PDF Scanner is a universal app and uses Apple’s built-in document camera, which gives you the same edge-detection, perspective-correction and filters on both iPhone and iPad.

How accurate is the OCR?

PDF Scanner uses Apple’s on-device Vision text recognition at its highest accuracy setting, with language correction enabled. Recognition runs entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded.

What’s included free, and what does Pro add?

You can scan, import, view, organize and search documents for free, in as many folders as you like. On the free tier a document opens to its first 3 pages — nothing you scan is ever thrown away, the remaining pages simply unlock with Pro — and exported files carry a subtle “PDF Scanner” mark across each page together with a small footer line. PDF Scanner Pro removes the watermark, unlocks every page, and opens up the full editor: text recognition (OCR) and in-document search, markup, signatures, form filling, add text, redaction, App Lock, merge/split/extract/password-protect, and all enhance filters. Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. The free tier also shows ads — a banner, an occasional full-screen ad at a natural break, and an optional rewarded video — and going Pro removes all of them.

How do I manage, cancel, or restore my subscription?

Open the crown / membership button in the app to see your plan, renewal date, and price, and to manage or cancel. Subscriptions are billed by Apple and can also be managed any time in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. New device or reinstalled the app? Tap Restore Purchases on the membership screen.

Can I remove the watermark without going Pro?

Yes, for one export at a time. On a free export you can export with the watermark, or go Pro to remove it for good — and whenever an ad is available there is a third choice: watch a short ad to drop the watermark from that single export. If no ad is available at that moment the watch-an-ad button simply isn’t shown, and the other two choices still work. Going Pro is the only way to remove it permanently — and it removes the ads too.

Can I erase markup or a signature after I’ve applied it?

Yes. From version 1.0.6, markup and signatures are saved as real PDF annotations rather than being flattened into the page, so they stay editable: reopen Markup to rub strokes out with the eraser, or use Erase Marks in the … menu to tap a whole mark away. The page keeps its searchable text either way. If you want the marks made permanent, Flatten (Make Permanent) in the … menu bakes them in — that step can’t be undone, and it does replace the marked pages with images. Marks applied by older versions were already flattened and can’t be recovered.

How do I get a scan into iCloud Drive?

PDF Scanner stores documents directly in the app’s own iCloud Drive folder, so they appear in the Files app under iCloud Drive → PDF Scanner and sync across your devices. You can also share a document and choose Save to Files → iCloud Drive.

Will my signature be reused on the next document?

Yes. Signatures are kept in a local signature library, so you draw (or scan) one once and then just drag and resize it onto each page. Tap Sign to pick a saved signature, Draw New to add another, or Manage to change a signature’s ink color, edit its strokes, or delete one you no longer need. From version 1.0.8 you can also Scan a signature you have written on paper — photograph it and the app lifts the ink onto a transparent background. The library never leaves your device.

What data does the app collect?

Your documents and the text recognized from them stay on your device or in your own iCloud Drive — we never see them, and we run no servers. The free version shows ads, and the advertising company collects limited device and ad-interaction data to serve them: Google AdMob in versions up to 1.0.8, and Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), serving Unity Ads and ironSource Ads, from version 1.0.9. iOS asks whether the app may track you; you can decline and still use everything, and you will simply see non-personalized ads. Pro members see no ads and are not asked. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
