PDF Scanner Privacy Policy

Effective 31 May 2026 · Last updated 24 July 2026

Summary: Your documents stay on your device and — when iCloud Drive is enabled — in your own private iCloud account. Scanning and text recognition run entirely on your device; we never see your files and we run no servers. Advertising depends on which version you are running: in versions up to and including 1.0.8 the free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects limited device and ad-interaction data to serve them; from version 1.0.9 onwards PDF Scanner shows no ads at all and the advertising SDK is never started (see section 5). Upgrading to PDF Scanner Pro removes the export watermark and unlocks the full app — and, on versions up to 1.0.8, removes all ads.

1. Who we are

PDF Scanner is developed by MW Supply Limited (“we”). This policy explains what data the app handles, what stays on your device, and what is processed by the limited third-party services we use to offer an optional upgrade and — in versions up to and including 1.0.8 — to show ads. We have no account system and operate no backend — you can use every feature without signing in to us.

This policy covers every version of PDF Scanner that is in use, and those versions do not all behave the same way. Where behaviour differs, the section says which versions it applies to. You can check your version in the app under … → Settings.

2. Your documents

The documents you scan or import — the page images, the resulting PDFs, the searchable text recognized from them, any markup, signatures or form entries you add, and the signatures you save to your signature library for reuse — are stored on your device. When iCloud Drive is enabled on your Apple ID, they are also kept in the app’s iCloud Drive folder (iCloud Drive → PDF Scanner) so they are backed up and available across your devices (see section 3). If iCloud Drive is unavailable, everything stays in the app’s local storage instead. This content never leaves your device or your iCloud account except via a Share action you initiate, and it is removed if you delete the app (local copies) or delete the documents. We have no access to it.

3. iCloud Drive

PDF Scanner stores its documents in your personal iCloud Drive using Apple’s iCloud document storage. This keeps your scans in sync across your iPhone and iPad and lets them return after you reinstall the app. The data is held in your own iCloud account — MW Supply Limited has no access to it, and iCloud is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. If you are not signed in to iCloud, your documents simply stay on the device.

4. Camera and text recognition

The Scan feature uses your device camera, through Apple’s VisionKit document camera, to capture document pages, with edge detection and perspective correction. Optical character recognition (OCR), which makes your scans searchable, runs entirely on your device using Apple’s Vision framework. No image, page, or recognized text is ever sent off your device by PDF Scanner.

5. Advertising

What this section says depends on your app version. Advertising was part of the free version through version 1.0.8. From version 1.0.9 onwards, PDF Scanner shows no ads at all, on any tier. Both are described below, because both versions are in use.

Versions 1.0.9 and later — no advertising

PDF Scanner requests, loads and displays no ads of any kind: no banner ads, no full-screen (interstitial) ads, no app-open ads and no rewarded video. The Google Mobile Ads SDK is still included in the app, but it is never started: no ad request leaves your device, no advertising identifier is read in order to show you an ad, and the ad-consent SDK is never initialized either. As a result iOS no longer shows you the App Tracking Transparency prompt for this app, and no ad-consent form appears. If an earlier version asked you for ad consent, that stored consent choice is cleared once, automatically and locally, when you update — it is not used for anything and there is no longer a screen on which to review it. Because there is no rewarded video any more, the watermark on a free export is removed only by upgrading to PDF Scanner Pro.

Why the app still mentions tracking. Version 1.0.9 deliberately keeps the advertising SDK and the app’s existing privacy declarations in place, because advertising may return in a future version. That means the app’s bundled privacy manifest still declares the app as capable of tracking and still names Google’s ad domain, and the App Store privacy label may still list the advertising data described below. We would rather over-declare than under-declare. In practice, while no ads are shown, that advertising code never runs and collects nothing. If advertising does return — with Google or another provider — we will update this page, the App Store privacy label and the in-app disclosures before that version ships.

Versions up to and including 1.0.8 — Google AdMob

In these versions the free version of PDF Scanner is supported by ads. It shows banner ads, occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads, and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to remove the watermark from a single export. These ads are served by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve them, Google may collect and process information including:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking when iOS asks (see below);
  • A non-personal device/usage identifier, device type, operating-system version and settings;
  • Your IP address and the approximate (coarse) region derived from it;
  • Information about how you interact with ads (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped).

This information is collected and used by Google, not by us. It is handled under Google’s Privacy Policy and as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. MW Supply Limited only receives aggregate, non-identifying reports such as the number of ad impressions and estimated revenue. Your documents and their contents are never shared with Google or used to target ads.

App Tracking Transparency (versions up to 1.0.8). iOS asks whether PDF Scanner may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” the advertising identifier is not used and you will see only non-personalized ads. You can use the app fully either way. Versions 1.0.9 and later do not show this prompt, because they request no ads.

Consent (EEA, UK & other regions; versions up to 1.0.8). Where required by law, PDF Scanner uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and respects your choice. Versions 1.0.9 and later never present this form and clear any consent choice already stored on your device.

Removing ads. On versions up to 1.0.8, upgrading to PDF Scanner Pro removes all banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads; while you are a Pro member the app does not request or display ads. On versions 1.0.9 and later there are no ads to remove, for free and Pro users alike.

6. PDF Scanner Pro

PDF Scanner Pro is an optional upgrade that removes the export watermark and unlocks the full app (unlimited pages per document, all enhancement filters, markup, signing, form filling, text recognition and in-document search, and the PDF tools). On versions up to and including 1.0.8 it also removes all ads. It is available as an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases, billing, renewals, and refunds are handled entirely by Apple through your App Store account — we never see or store your name, your payment-card details, or your Apple ID. To unlock Pro features, the app checks your purchase entitlement directly with the App Store on your device; this check does not identify you to us. You can view, change, or cancel a subscription at any time in the App Store under Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

7. Network activity

PDF Scanner makes no network requests of its own and has no servers to talk to. On versions up to and including 1.0.8, the advertising and consent services in section 5 make network requests to Google. On versions 1.0.9 and later they make none, because they are never started — the only remaining network activity is the App Store purchase check described in section 6 and Apple’s own iCloud synchronization, which happens at the operating-system level when iCloud Drive is enabled.

8. Sharing and exporting

You can share or export documents using the standard iOS Share sheet (for example, to Files, Mail, Messages, or AirDrop). Where a file goes from there is controlled entirely by the destination you pick. PDF Scanner does not transmit your documents anywhere on its own.

9. Third-party services

PDF Scanner includes two SDKs, both from Google:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) — serves the banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.
  • Google User Messaging Platform — manages the ad-consent prompt where it is legally required.

On versions up to and including 1.0.8, both are active in the free version and are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. On versions 1.0.9 and later both are still shipped inside the app but neither is ever started, so neither receives, sends or processes any data — they are retained only so that advertising can be re-enabled in a future version, which would be disclosed here first. PDF Scanner contains no analytics frameworks and no crash reporting other than Apple’s first-party crash reporting (which is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and only runs if you opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements). PDF Scanner Pro members are not shown ads on any version, and the app does not request ads for them.

10. Permissions and identifiers

PDF Scanner does not access your location, microphone or health data. On versions up to and including 1.0.8, the advertising identifier is used only for ads in the free version, and only if you allow tracking as described in section 5; on versions 1.0.9 and later the app does not use the advertising identifier at all and iOS no longer shows the tracking prompt. The other system features the app uses are optional, are used only when you invoke them, and stay on your device:

  • Camera. Used only when you scan a document, scan a signature, or scan a QR/barcode; capture and text recognition happen on your device and the results stay there.
  • Files. Importing a PDF or saving an export uses the system document picker, which grants access only to the specific file you choose.
  • Photos. Used only if you import an image or a photographed signature, through the system picker, which grants access only to what you select.
  • Contacts. Requested only if you choose to save a contact card that you scanned from a QR code; the contact is written by the system contact sheet, on your device.
  • Face ID / passcode. Used only if you turn on App Lock, and only to unlock the app. The check is performed by iOS; we never receive your biometric data.

11. Children

PDF Scanner is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. On versions up to and including 1.0.8, because the free version shows ads, we instruct Google to serve only family-appropriate (maximum content rating G) ads, and upgrading to PDF Scanner Pro removes all ads. Versions 1.0.9 and later show no ads at all. Either way, the app is not designed for use by children under the age of digital consent without a parent or guardian.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how PDF Scanner handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy nutrition labels for the affected version of the app.
