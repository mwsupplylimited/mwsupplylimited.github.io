About

SciCalc is a clean, fast scientific calculator with real expression parsing, a scrolling tape of past calculations, persistent history, memory, degree/radian trig, and a dedicated landscape keypad. No ads, no accounts, no data collection.

Getting started

  • Type a whole expression and press =. Operator precedence and parentheses are handled correctly. Try 5sin(30) + π².
  • 2nd key reveals inverse trig (sin⁻¹, etc.), 10ˣ, eˣ, x!, τ, and cube root.
  • Ans inserts the last result. After pressing =, an operator (+, ×, etc.) continues from Ans automatically.
  • Tap any past calculation on the tape to insert its expression, its result, or copy the result to the clipboard.
  • Rotate to landscape for a wide 9×5 keypad that puts every function on one page.
  • DEG / RAD button at the top-left toggles angle mode.
  • Tap anywhere on the input line to place the cursor exactly where you want it.

SciCalc Pro

The full calculator is free forever, with no ads and no data collection. SciCalc Pro adds:

  • Graphing — plot any function of x (e.g. sin(x), x²−3, 1/x) on an interactive graph with pinch‑zoom, pan, trace, and multiple functions at once.
  • iCloud history sync — your calculation tape, kept in sync across all your devices through your own private iCloud.
  • Unlimited history — keep every calculation (the free tier keeps the most recent 50).

Pro is an auto‑renewable subscription: $1.99/month or $9.99/year (with a 7‑day free trial on the annual plan). Prices may vary by region. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Tap the crown icon in the app to subscribe or manage your membership.

FAQ

Why does -2² equal 4 instead of -4?

Unary minus binds tighter than the power operator in SciCalc, matching the iOS built-in Calculator. If you want -(2²) = -4, type -(2^2) or 0 - 2^2.

Does 3.5! work?

Yes. Non-integer factorials use the gamma function: 3.5! ≈ 11.6317.

Is my history backed up?

Your history is stored on your device and survives relaunches and updates. With SciCalc Pro, it also syncs across your devices through your own private iCloud. Free history is kept on-device and limited to the most recent 50 calculations.

Where is my data sent?

We run no servers and collect nothing. The only network activity is with Apple's own services: iCloud/CloudKit (for optional Pro history sync, stored in your private iCloud that only you can access) and the App Store (for subscriptions). See the Privacy Policy for full details.
