SciCalc Privacy Policy

Effective 28 May 2026 · Last updated 16 August 2026

Summary: Your calculations are never sent to us. In the free version they stay on your device; with SciCalc Pro, your history also syncs through your own private iCloud (Apple's CloudKit), staying in your personal iCloud account. The free version shows ads served through Unity LevelPlay, which collects limited device and ad-interaction data to do so. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask your permission first, in our own dialog, and declining costs you no features. Upgrading to SciCalc Pro removes all ads. We have no account system and run no servers of our own.

1. Who we are

SciCalc is developed by MW Supply Limited (“we”). This policy explains what stays on your device and what is processed by the limited third-party services we use to show ads (free version) and to offer an optional Pro upgrade. The app has no account system and no backend — you can use every feature without signing in to us.

2. Information stored on your device

SciCalc stores the following on your device so the app remembers it between launches:

  • Your calculation history (the scrolling tape), stored with Apple's on-device SwiftData framework.
  • The current memory register value (M+/M−/MR) and your preferred angle mode (degrees or radians).

This stays on your device, is never sent to us, can be cleared at any time with the Clear History button or the MC key, and is removed entirely if you delete the app.

iCloud sync (SciCalc Pro). If you subscribe to Pro, your calculation history is also synced through your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so it stays in sync across your iPhone and iPad and is restored when you set up a new device. This data lives in your personal iCloud account under your Apple ID — it is visible only to you, handled under Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy, and we never receive it or have access to it. History is not synced to iCloud in the free version.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version of SciCalc is supported by ads served through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), a mediation service that runs an auction between advertising networks. There are three places an ad can appear:

  • a banner across the top of the calculator, above the app's own controls;
  • an occasional full-screen ad, shown only when you close the graph or history screen, or when you return to the app after being away a while — never while you are part-way through a calculation, never in the first minute and a half after opening the app, and at most three times a day;
  • an optional video you choose to watch in exchange for temporarily unlocking multi-function graphing. This one never appears unless you tap it.

To serve these, our advertising partner may collect and process information including:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking when iOS asks (see below);
  • A non-personal device/usage identifier, device type, operating-system version and settings;
  • Your IP address and the approximate (coarse) region derived from it;
  • Information about how you interact with ads (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped).

This information is collected and used by Unity, not by us, under Unity's Player and App User Privacy Policy. MW Supply Limited only receives aggregate, non-identifying reports such as ad impressions and estimated revenue. Your calculations are never shared with any advertiser or used to target ads.

App Tracking Transparency. iOS asks whether SciCalc may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” the advertising identifier is not used. You can use the app fully either way.

Your consent choice (EEA, UK & Switzerland). Where the law requires your prior permission, SciCalc asks for it directly, in its own dialog, before any ad is requested. Both answers are presented with equal weight, nothing is pre-selected, and declining costs you nothing: you keep every feature and still see ads — they are simply generic rather than personalized. You can change your answer at any time in the app's Settings, as easily as you gave it. If you never answer, we treat that as “no.”

Removing ads. Upgrading to SciCalc Pro removes all three ad formats entirely. While you are a Pro member the app does not even start the advertising SDK, let alone request or display an ad.

4. SciCalc Pro & payments

SciCalc Pro removes all ads, unlocks multi-function graphing (up to six functions at once), keeps unlimited calculation history and syncs it through your own iCloud. It is available either as an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or annual) or as a one-time lifetime purchase that never renews. Purchases, billing, renewals, and refunds are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit — we never see or store your name, card number, or Apple ID. To unlock Pro, the app checks your purchase entitlement directly with the App Store on your device; this check does not identify you to us. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

5. Network activity

Apart from the advertising and purchase services described above — and, for Pro subscribers, syncing your history to your own private iCloud as described in section 2 — SciCalc makes no network requests of its own and has no servers to talk to. Your calculations are never transmitted to us, and in the free version they never leave your device.

6. Third-party services

To show ads in the free version, SciCalc includes the Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation SDK and the Unity Ads adapter it auctions through. These are governed by Unity's Player and App User Privacy Policy. The consent prompt described in section 3 is our own — it is part of SciCalc, not a third-party SDK, so no additional company is involved in asking you.

SciCalc contains no analytics frameworks and no crash reporting other than Apple's own first-party reporting (governed by Apple's privacy policy, and only if you opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements). SciCalc Pro members are not shown ads, and the app does not request ads for them.

7. Permissions and identifiers

SciCalc does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or health data. The advertising identifier is used only for ads in the free version, and only if you allow tracking as described in section 3.

8. Children

SciCalc is a general-audience calculator rated 4+ on the App Store, and is declared to our advertising partner as not directed to children. Because the free version shows ads, we use the ad-quality controls our partner provides: served creatives are screened for adult, gambling, alcohol, drug and dating content, and flagged advertisers are blocked across our apps. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Upgrading to SciCalc Pro removes all ads.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how SciCalc handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy nutrition labels for the affected version.
