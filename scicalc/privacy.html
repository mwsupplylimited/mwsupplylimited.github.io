SciCalc Privacy Policy

Effective 28 May 2026 · Last updated 16 June 2026

Summary: SciCalc collects no personal data, shows no ads, and uses no analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs. Your calculations stay on your device. With SciCalc Pro, your history can optionally sync through your own private iCloud — which only you can access; we cannot.

1. Information we collect

None. SciCalc does not ask for, collect, transmit to us, or store on our servers any personally identifiable information. The app has no account system, no login, and no user profile, and we operate no servers that receive your data.

2. Information stored locally on your device

SciCalc stores the following on your device so the app remembers it between launches:

  • Your calculation history (the scrolling tape), stored with Apple's on-device SwiftData framework.
  • The current memory register value (M+/M−/MR) and your preferred angle mode (degrees or radians).

You can clear this at any time with the Clear History button in the History sheet or the MC button on the keypad, and it is removed entirely if you delete the app.

3. iCloud sync (SciCalc Pro)

If you subscribe to SciCalc Pro and are signed in to iCloud, your calculation history can sync across your devices using Apple's CloudKit private database. This data is stored in your own iCloud account, is end-to-end controlled by Apple's iCloud, and is not accessible to MW Supply Limited — we never see it. It is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn this off by not subscribing, or remove the data by clearing your history or signing out of iCloud. Free users' history stays local to the device only.

4. Subscriptions & payments

SciCalc Pro is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or annual). All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never receive or store your name, card number, or any payment details — Apple handles payment entirely and shares only anonymous, aggregate sales data with us. Manage or cancel your subscription anytime in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

5. Network activity

SciCalc contacts no servers owned by MW Supply Limited. The only network activity is with Apple's own first-party services: CloudKit (for optional Pro iCloud history sync) and StoreKit (for subscription purchases and restoration). There are no other network requests.

6. Third-party services

SciCalc contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics frameworks, no advertising networks, and no tracking. The only crash reporting is Apple's own first-party reporting, which runs only if you have opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

7. Children

SciCalc is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+ on the App Store. Because we collect no personal data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how SciCalc handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy labels for the affected version.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
