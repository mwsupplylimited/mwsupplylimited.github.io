Metron — Unit Converter

A unit converter for people who are paid to be right about numbers. Over 1,200 units across more than 130 categories — every one of them free, forever, because paywalling a base SI unit is indefensible. Enter a value once and every compatible unit updates live, with the right number of digits rather than a screenful of floating-point noise. Each unit shows its defining relation and its source, and says whether it is exact by definition or measured. It never ships a bare “gallon”, “ounce”, “ton” or “calorie”: US liquid, US dry and imperial; avoirdupois and troy; short, long and metric; thermochemical and IT — all separate, all named. Everything runs on your device and the app makes no network request of its own. For iPhone & iPad. Free with ads; Metron Pro adds the expression solver with compound units and dimensional checking (2.5e-3 mol/L × 180 g/mol), significant figures and uncertainty propagation, the CODATA 2022 constants, molar mass from a chemical formula, unlimited history, export to CSV, LaTeX and Markdown, widgets, iCloud sync and no ads.

Dishroll — What to Eat Near Me

Random restaurant roulette that actually decides. Set how far you’ll go, which cuisines and which kinds of place, then tap Draw — a wheel spins over a live map and lands on one restaurant. Every candidate comes from Apple’s own food categories, so it never sends you to a salon or a vape shop, and it tells you its real odds: “pure random” is exactly uniform, “popular first” still guarantees every venue a quarter of an equal chance. A no-repeat window skips where it just sent you rather than merely making it less likely. Nudge a result Closer, to Different food, Somewhere new or Sit-down; search any city for a trip; keep favourites, a never-again list and a food passport; or run a Group Pick over the local network where everyone vetoes first. For iPhone & iPad. Free with unlimited draws and one banner; Dishroll Pro adds iCloud sync, Collections, unlimited Group Picks and full history.

Voltway — Battery & Power for Mac

See exactly where your Mac’s watts go, and control the last 20% Apple won’t. Voltway draws a live power-flow diagram — watts out of the wall, watts into the Mac, and whether the battery is charging or supplying, with flow thickness and particle speed tracking real wattage — then opens up the battery data macOS keeps to itself: learned capacity per cell, cell voltages, a live per-cell impedance reading — impedance growth warns of ageing earlier than capacity fade — cycle count, and how long the pack has sat at a high state of charge. It is drawn honestly: each flow is measured independently so the figures are not expected to add up exactly, and it never shows your adapter’s label rating as if it were live draw. With an optional, separately-downloaded notarized helper it also controls charging — limits below 80%, separate stop and resume levels, and running from the adapter without charging — and it fails safe by design, restoring normal charging if the app stops talking to it, if the battery gets low or hot, on quit, and even if it crashes. The whole read-only side is free forever; Voltway Pro adds the charge-control tools.

Search about 630 EPA-registered repellents — or just pick an active ingredient and concentration off the label — and see how long it has actually been measured to protect you, cited to the study behind the number: DEET 23.8% is about 5 hours, a citronella wristband is about 15 seconds.
