Apps

Helmward — Remote Desktop

See and control your Mac at home from your iPhone, iPad, or another Mac while you travel. A private, Apple-native remote desktop with end-to-end encryption, simple ID + password pairing, and no false “commercial use” lockouts or session time limits. Free to start; Helmward Pro adds multi-monitor, file transfer, Wake-on-LAN and an ad-free app. Coming soon.

Corynx — System Monitor

A beautiful, native system monitor for your Mac. Watch CPU (with per-core detail), GPU, memory, disk, network, battery and thermals — live in the menu bar and in a clean dashboard. Private by design: everything stays on your Mac, no accounts, no ads. Free, with Corynx Pro adding history & trend graphs, the detailed GPU module, full menu-bar customization and CSV export.

AdMob Monitor — Earnings & Stats

Check your Google AdMob earnings without opening a browser. Sign in with Google to see today's, yesterday's and monthly estimated revenue, per-app and per-ad-unit breakdowns, custom reports with CSV export, and progress toward your payment threshold — organised like the AdMob console so everything is where you expect. Read-only via the official AdMob API; not affiliated with Google. For iPhone & iPad. Free with ads — Pro removes ads and unlocks full history, custom reports and multiple accounts.

Vivace — Hi-Res Music Player

A high-resolution, lossless music player for audiophiles. Play the files you own — FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, DSD and more — with the true source format shown on every track, gapless playback, a 10-band equalizer, synced lyrics and CUE-sheet albums. Browse by album, artist, genre, folder and playlist. For iPhone & iPad. Free, with Vivace Pro removing ads and unlocking DSD playback, automatic online lyrics, ReplayGain, every theme and unlimited playlists.

Duplikit — Multiple App Instances

Run a second copy of the same Mac app — Claude, Slack, Chrome and more — each signed into a different account, side by side. Duplikit launches your app with its own private profile; nothing leaves your Mac. For macOS. 7-day free trial, then a simple subscription.

Windrose — Weather Radar

Live precipitation radar plus complete weather for everyone — on land, at sea, and in the air. Watch an animated radar loop to see where the rain is heading, get a colour-coded safety summary, and switch between General, Marine and Aviation modes (decoded METARs/TAFs, winds aloft, SIGMETs, waves & swell). For iPhone & iPad. Free, with Windrose Pro unlocking the aviation & marine modes, full radar nowcast and the 16-day forecast.

Star Maps — Sky & Satellite Tracker

Hold your phone up to the sky to see stars, constellations, planets, the Sun, the Moon and deep-sky objects overlaid on the real sky in augmented reality — tap anything to identify it. Track the ISS, Starlink and hundreds of bright satellites in real time and see when they fly over. For iPhone & iPad. Free with ads — Star Maps Plus unlocks fainter stars, the full satellite catalogue and flyover alerts.

Money Splitter — Split Bills with Friends

Split trip and group bills fairly. Create a trip, share a code, and everyone adds what they paid — Money Splitter works out who owes who and the fewest payments to settle up. Split equally, by exact amounts, percentages, or shares. For iPhone & iPad with iCloud sync. Free with ads.

NutriSnap — Calorie & Food Tracker

Snap a photo of your meal to log its calories and nutrients, track the calories you burn with Apple Health, and follow a personal plan for losing weight, maintaining, gaining, or building muscle. For iPhone & iPad, with private iCloud sync. No ads, no accounts, no data collection.

Pourfect — Ball Sort Puzzle

A relaxing color-sorting puzzle for iPhone & iPad. Pour balls between tubes until each holds a single color. No timer, endless always-solvable levels, unlockable themes, and helpful undo/hint/add-tube. Free with ads — a one-time purchase removes them.

Time to Wake Up — Arrival Reminder

A location alarm for the bus, train, or coach. Set your stop on a map, pick a wake-up zone, and rest — it sounds a loud alarm (or a gentle notification) the moment you're arriving. Works with the screen locked. No ads, no accounts; your location never leaves your device.

Block Bloom: Puzzle

A relaxing block puzzle for iPhone & iPad. Drag pieces onto a 9×9 grid and clear rows, columns, and 3×3 boxes for satisfying combos and streaks. Earn coins to unlock colorful themes. Free with ads — a one-time purchase removes them.

Blackout Camera

A camera for the dark. Shoot photos and video while the screen stays completely black — tap Screen Off, or let it black out the moment recording starts. Red night-vision mode and full manual controls (ISO, shutter, focus, white balance) for astrophotography and low-light work. No ads, no accounts, no data collection.

Beat jet lag before it beats you. Tell it your route and dates and it works out whether to shift your body clock at all, then builds a day-by-day plan of when to seek light, sleep, and take melatonin — grounded in circadian science. Optionally reads Apple Health to personalise. No ads, no accounts, no data collection.

Money Flow: Budget & Expenses

A worldwide taxi-fare meter for iPhone. Uses GPS to measure your trip and estimate the running fare from real, official metered tariffs — flag-fall, distance, waiting time and day/night rates — for 81+ cities and countries across every continent. Free for your home country with ads — Ride Meter Pro unlocks all 81+ regions, removes ads, and adds exportable history and iCloud backup.
