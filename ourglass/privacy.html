Ourglass

Privacy Policy Updated 5 September 2026

There are no accounts and there is no company server. Everything you put into Ourglass stays on your device and in your own iCloud. MW Supply Limited never receives it and could not read it if we wanted to.

What Ourglass stores

All of it on your device, and in your own iCloud so it reaches your other devices and the partner you shared a calendar with:

  • The memories you write: the day, a title, your words, and the photos you add.
  • Drawings you make, kept as your own layer on each photo or page.
  • Places you add: the name, the coordinate, and whether you have been or plan to go.
  • Milestones and their reminder times.
  • Time capsules: their title, their letter, and the day they open.
  • The display name you chose for yourself in each calendar, and the time zone your device is in.

Where it goes

Each calendar is stored in the iCloud account of whoever created it, in a private area of that account, and shared with the one partner they invited. Apple's iCloud carries it between your devices. This is the same mechanism Apple's own apps use, and it is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Coordinates and the text of your memories are marked for encryption in iCloud, which means they are unreadable without your own keys. A list of everywhere a couple has been is exactly the kind of thing that should need them.

What Ourglass does not do

  • No account, no sign-up, no email address, no password.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting to us, no usage tracking, no advertising identifier.
  • Nothing is ever sent to a server belonging to MW Supply Limited, because there is not one.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anybody. There is nobody to sell it to.

Permissions, and exactly when they are used

Photos

Ourglass uses the system photo picker, which runs outside the app. The app receives only the specific pictures you choose and never gets access to your photo library. That is why Ourglass never asks for photo library permission.

Location

Asked for only at the moment you tap “use my current location” while adding a place, and read exactly once, in the foreground, to place that pin. There is no background location and no tracking of where you go. If you say no, the other ways of adding a place all still work.

Notifications

Used only for the reminders you set yourself on your own milestones. They are scheduled on your device by your device; nothing is pushed from anywhere.

Purchases

Ourglass Pro is sold through Apple. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether the purchase is currently valid. We never see your name, your card, or your address.

Deleting things

A memory you delete goes to a bin for thirty days and can be restored, together with its photos and drawings; after that it is gone. Leaving a shared calendar removes it from your devices. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on that device, and you can remove the iCloud copy from Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Children

Ourglass is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date above changes and the new version appears here before the version of the app it applies to is released.
