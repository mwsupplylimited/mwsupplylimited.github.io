Ourglass

Privacy Policy Updated 1 October 2026

There is no separate Ourglass account. Calendar data is stored on your devices and in the creator's iCloud, shared with accepted participants. The app does not send that data to a server operated by MW Supply Limited.

What Ourglass stores

Ourglass stores the following on your devices and, while shared access is available, in the calendar creator's iCloud:

  • The memories you write: the day, a title, your words, and the photos you add.
  • Drawings you make, kept as your own layer on each photo or page.
  • Places you add: the name, the coordinate, and whether you have been or plan to go.
  • Milestones and their reminder times.
  • Existing capsule records, if present: their title, letter and scheduled opening day.
  • The display name you chose for yourself in each calendar, and the time zone your device is in.

Where it goes

Each calendar is stored in the iCloud account of whoever created it and shared with the people who have accepted its invitation. Send invitations only to your intended partner. New invitations use a private invitation link; unused invitations can be withdrawn without removing someone who already joined through a private invitation. Older public links may remain open after someone joins; Ourglass leaves them unchanged to preserve that person's access. Apple's iCloud carries calendar data between devices and is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

The app writes memory text and coordinates using CloudKit's encrypted-field support. It does not provide a separate password or encryption-key service.

What Ourglass does not do

  • No separate Ourglass sign-up, email address or password. iCloud sharing uses your Apple Account.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting to us, no usage tracking, no advertising identifier.
  • The app does not send calendar data to a server operated by MW Supply Limited.
  • MW Supply Limited does not sell your calendar data. Calendar data is shared through iCloud with people who have access to that calendar.

Permissions, and exactly when they are used

Photos

Ourglass uses the system photo picker, which runs outside the app. The app receives only the specific pictures you choose and never gets access to your photo library. That is why Ourglass never asks for photo library permission.

Location

Asked for only at the moment you tap “use my current location” while adding a place, and read exactly once, in the foreground, to place that pin. There is no background location and no tracking of where you go. If you say no, the other ways of adding a place all still work.

Notifications

Milestone reminders are scheduled locally on your device. iCloud also sends silent notifications so Ourglass can fetch calendar changes; these do not display an alert or message.

Purchases

When purchases become available, Apple will handle payment and provide the app with purchase and subscription status, including plan and transaction dates. The app does not receive your payment-card details or billing address.

Deleting things

A deleted memory can be restored from the bin for thirty days, together with its photos and drawings. After that it becomes eligible for permanent deletion during a later successful synchronization.

Leaving someone else's shared calendar ends your account's shared access once iCloud confirms the operation. On the device where you leave, its downloaded copy goes to Deleted calendars for thirty days and can be kept as a read-only copy. Your other devices may retain their downloaded memories as a read-only copy. Leaving does not erase the creator's calendar.

The creator can delete a calendar and restore it during a thirty-day window. It can then be deleted from iCloud after synchronization. Copies already downloaded on another person's devices may remain read-only, and independent copies that person saved are not recalled.

If Apple reports that the affected iCloud data was explicitly purged or its encrypted data was reset, Ourglass removes the affected downloaded content, including local photos, without a thirty-day bin. The app does not upload that removed copy again.

Deleting the app removes the data stored by that app installation; it does not by itself delete the calendar from iCloud. You can manage Ourglass iCloud storage through Apple's iCloud settings. Device backups and independent copies are managed separately.

Children

Ourglass is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date above changes and the new version appears here before the version of the app it applies to is released.
