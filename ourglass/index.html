Ourglass

⌛  A calendar for the two of you. In development

Ourglass is in development and is not yet available on the App Store. This page describes the current development build.

What it does

Ourglass is a shared calendar for a relationship. You keep the days that mattered — a photo, a few words, a drawing on top of the photo — and both of you see the same calendar, on supported iPhones and iPads with Ourglass and iCloud enabled, even though you sign in with different Apple Accounts.

There is no public feed. Calendar content is available to the people who have access to that calendar.

The four screens

Calendar

The month, with a mark on every day you kept something. Open a day to add photos, write, or draw. A memory belongs to the calendar day you chose rather than to a timestamp, so a night out in Hong Kong and the same night read in London sit on the same square instead of drifting a day apart.

Places

Everywhere you have been together, as a globe you can spin or a flat map, with the journey drawn between them in order. Add a place by searching for it, by dropping it where you are, or from a photo that already knows where it was taken. Places you have not been to yet can be marked as planned.

Milestones

Count-downs to what is coming and count-ups from what has already happened, with reminders. Ourglass writes the obvious ones for you — 100 days, a year, 1000 days — so they appear without anybody having to think of them.

Us

The two of you, each with the local time where they are, so you can see at a glance whether it is a reasonable hour to call. This uses the time zone each device publishes, never a location it measured.

One calendar per partner

Each relationship is its own calendar with its own memories, its own map and its own colour. Each calendar has a separate iCloud share; access to one does not grant access to your other calendars.

How sharing works

There is no separate Ourglass account. On-device calendars stay separate from iCloud calendars. A calendar connected to iCloud lives in the creator's own iCloud, and the partner is invited to that specific calendar through Apple's own sharing. Your Apple Account provides access on your devices. Calendar data is not sent to a server operated by MW Supply Limited.

Send the invitation only to your intended partner. New invitations use a private invitation link. You can withdraw an unused invitation from the invite screen; a partner who already joined through a private invitation keeps their access. Older public links may remain open after someone joins. Ourglass refuses to close those links because doing so would remove that person's access.

Things worth knowing

Both people need iCloud switched on

Sharing requires an Apple Account signed in to iCloud. Calendar changes can synchronize when iCloud and a network connection are available.

Deleting is reversible for thirty days

A memory you delete goes to a bin, together with its photos and drawings. Restoring it brings those items back. After thirty days it becomes eligible for permanent deletion during a later successful synchronization; deletion is not guaranteed to finish at the exact end of the window.

If Apple reports that the calendar's iCloud data was explicitly purged or its encrypted data was reset, Ourglass removes the affected downloaded content, including photos. That removal does not use the thirty-day bin.

Drawings belong to whoever drew them

Each of you draws on your own layer of the same photo. You can erase your own ink and you cannot erase theirs, so two people drawing on the same picture at the same time cannot destroy each other's work.

Your location is only ever read when you ask for it

The only time Ourglass reads your location is the moment you tap “use my current location” while adding a place. There is no background location, and if a fix does not arrive within a few seconds it gives up and offers you the search field instead.

Common questions

Can my partner and I use different Apple Accounts?

Yes — that is the whole point. You do not need to share an Apple Account, a password, or anything else.

Does it work on my iPad too?

Yes. Your calendars can synchronize between supported iPhones and iPads running Ourglass, signed in to your Apple Account with iCloud enabled.

Can I keep more than one calendar?

You can create one calendar for free, or more with Pro. Calendars you join do not count toward that creation limit. Each calendar has its own separate share.

What happens if we break up?

If someone else created the calendar, you can leave it. Once iCloud confirms the leave, your account loses shared access. On the device where you leave, the downloaded copy goes to Deleted calendars for thirty days, where you can choose to keep it read-only. Your other devices may keep their downloaded memories as a read-only copy. Leaving does not erase the creator's calendar.

If you created the calendar, you can delete it. It moves to Deleted calendars, and only you can restore it during the thirty-day window. It can then be deleted from iCloud after synchronization. Copies already on another person's devices may remain read-only; deleting a calendar does not recall independent copies they saved.

Why does Ourglass say “Sync paused”?

Open Details to read the reason and recovery steps. For a failed download or saved sync progress, close Ourglass completely from the app switcher, then reopen it to retry. Some unreadable changes require an app update. If the message returns, contact support; reopening is a retry, not confirmation that the missing changes have arrived.

How do I preserve calendars from an earlier version?

If Ourglass finds an older calendar database, choose Recover older data from its notice. If the current account cannot open, choose On-device recovery on that screen. Prepare a recovery package, inspect its saved copy where available, and choose Save recovery package to copy it to Files. The app reports a verified save only after reading back the destination.

This preserves app files; it does not import or restore calendars. The originals remain unchanged. Unknown ownership, incomplete files or cloud deletion restrictions can prevent an action, and the screen explains the failure. Recovery does not require Pro. Keep exported packages private: they can contain photos, journal text and earlier sharing details.

Does anyone at MW Supply Limited see our photos?

The app does not send your photos to a server operated by MW Supply Limited. See the privacy policy.
