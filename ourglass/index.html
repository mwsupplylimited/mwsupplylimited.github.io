Ourglass

⌛  A calendar for the two of you. Version 1.0

What it does

Ourglass is a shared calendar for a relationship. You keep the days that mattered — a photo, a few words, a drawing on top of the photo — and both of you see the same calendar, on every device each of you owns, even though you sign in with different Apple Accounts.

It is not a chat app and it is not a social network. Nobody else can see any of it, there is no feed, and there is nothing to post.

The four screens

Calendar

The month, with a mark on every day you kept something. Open a day to add photos, write, or draw. A memory belongs to the calendar day you chose rather than to a timestamp, so a night out in Hong Kong and the same night read in London sit on the same square instead of drifting a day apart.

Places

Everywhere you have been together, as a globe you can spin or a flat map, with the journey drawn between them in order. Add a place by searching for it, by dropping it where you are, or from a photo that already knows where it was taken. Places you have not been to yet can be marked as planned.

Milestones

Count-downs to what is coming and count-ups from what has already happened, with reminders. Ourglass writes the obvious ones for you — 100 days, a year, 1000 days — so they appear without anybody having to think of them.

Us

The two of you, each with the local time where they are, so you can see at a glance whether it is a reasonable hour to call. This uses the time zone each device publishes, never a location it measured.

This is also where time capsules live: a letter you write today that neither of you can read again until a day you choose. Once it is sealed it cannot be edited, and once it is opened it cannot be rewritten.

One calendar per partner

Each relationship is its own calendar with its own memories, its own map and its own colour. They do not mix, and the person in one has no idea the others exist. This is a data-model fact rather than a setting: each one is a separate private store shared with exactly one person.

How sharing works

Ourglass has no accounts and no company servers. Each calendar lives in the creator's own iCloud, and the partner is invited to that specific calendar through Apple's own sharing. Your own devices reach it because it is yours; your partner's devices reach it because you shared it with them. MW Supply Limited is not in the middle and cannot read any of it.

An invite expires after seven days, and once your partner has accepted, the link stops working for anybody else. A shared calendar link that stays live forever is how the wrong person ends up in a couple's private calendar.

Things worth knowing

Both people need iCloud switched on

Sharing runs entirely on Apple's iCloud. If either of you is signed out of iCloud, or has iCloud Drive off, that device keeps working on its own but nothing moves between you until it is signed back in.

Deleting is reversible for thirty days

A memory you delete goes to a bin rather than disappearing, and takes its photos and both people's drawings with it. Restoring it brings all of that back. After thirty days it is removed for good.

Drawings belong to whoever drew them

Each of you draws on your own layer of the same photo. You can erase your own ink and you cannot erase theirs, so two people drawing on the same picture at the same time cannot destroy each other's work.

Your location is only ever read when you ask for it

The only time Ourglass reads your location is the moment you tap “use my current location” while adding a place. There is no background location, and if a fix does not arrive within a few seconds it gives up and offers you the search field instead.

Common questions

Can my partner and I use different Apple Accounts?

Yes — that is the whole point. You do not need to share an Apple Account, a password, or anything else.

Does it work on my iPad too?

Yes. Every device signed into your own Apple Account sees your calendars.

Can I keep more than one calendar?

Yes, with Pro. Each is completely separate from the others.

What happens if we break up?

You can leave a shared calendar, which removes it from your devices, or the person who created it can stop sharing it. Either way nothing is silently deleted from the other person.

Does anyone at MW Supply Limited see our photos?

No. There is no server to see them with. See the privacy policy.
