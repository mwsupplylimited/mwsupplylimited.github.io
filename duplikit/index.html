How it works

Duplikit never copies or modifies the original app. It opens your installed app with its own private profile — so a second copy can sign in to a completely different account.

Frequently asked questions

Does Duplikit copy or change the original app?

No. It opens your existing app with its own private data folder. The original is untouched and keeps updating normally.

Will the copies stay logged in?

Yes. Each copy keeps its own login and data between launches, just like the original.

How are the copies shown in the Dock?

Because Duplikit runs the genuine app, every copy shares the original app’s Dock icon and name. You manage and launch your named copies from inside Duplikit.

Is my data private?

Completely. Everything stays on your Mac. Duplikit has no servers and collects no personal data. See the Privacy Policy.

Why isn’t Duplikit on the Mac App Store?

The App Store sandbox prevents an app from giving another app a separate profile, so a sandboxed version literally can’t do what Duplikit does. Distributing directly (notarized by Apple) is the only way it works — and it’s just as safe.

Do I need the other app installed?

Yes — Duplikit launches apps you already have. Install Claude, Slack, Chrome, etc. the normal way first, then add them in Duplikit.

Can I use one license on more than one Mac?

Yes — your license works on your Macs. Just paste the key on each one.
