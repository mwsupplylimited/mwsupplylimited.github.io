Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 June 2026

In short: Duplikit runs entirely on your Mac. It has no servers, no analytics, and collects no personal information. Everything you set up stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Duplikit does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. We do not operate analytics or tracking servers, and the app contains no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.

Information stored on your Mac

To do its job, Duplikit stores the following locally on your Mac only, inside its own app container:

  • The list of app copies you create — their names, which app each points to, and your separation settings.
  • The private data folder each copy uses, so your copies stay signed in between launches.
  • The launchers Duplikit generates for your copies.

This information never leaves your Mac. The accounts and data inside the apps you duplicate belong to those apps and are handled under their own privacy policies — Duplikit only provides each copy with a separate folder to store its data in.

App access

When you choose an app to duplicate, Duplikit reads that app (which you select yourself) so it can create a matching launcher with the right name and icon. It does not modify, copy, or upload the app.

Subscriptions & purchases

Duplikit offers an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) with a free trial. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment-card details. To unlock your subscription and restore purchases, the app checks your subscription status with Apple via StoreKit; Apple provides only the anonymized transaction and entitlement information needed to enable your benefits. You can view, change, or cancel your subscription anytime in your Apple Account settings, and Apple’s handling of payment data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or sell your personal information.
  • We do not run analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location, messages, or the contents of the accounts inside the apps you duplicate.
  • We do not transmit your data off your Mac.

Data retention and deletion

Everything Duplikit stores stays on your Mac until you remove it. Deleting an instance in the app removes its launcher and its private data folder. Removing the Duplikit app removes its data with it.

Children

Duplikit is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
