Duara

Understand your cycle and your patterns — and, if you choose, share a private space with your partner. Grounded in science, private by design. Version 1.0

Duara is for general wellness and education only. It is not a medical device, does not provide a diagnosis, and is not a method of birth control. Predictions are estimates and can be wrong. Always talk to a qualified healthcare provider about your health, and call your local emergency number in an emergency.

What it does

Duara helps you log and understand your menstrual cycle. Record your period, symptoms, mood, energy, and more, and Duara learns your rhythm to predict your next period and estimate your fertile window — always with an honest sense of how certain (or uncertain) each estimate is. It explains what each phase of your cycle means, helps you make sense of changes like a late period, and points you toward a clinician when something is worth checking.

How to use it

  • Set up your cycle. Tell Duara your goal, when your last period started, and your typical cycle length. You can change all of this later in Me → Your cycle.
  • Log your day. Tap + or Log today to record flow, symptoms, mood, energy, cervical fluid, intimacy, and notes. Your period is worked out automatically from the days you log bleeding.
  • See your Today screen. The cycle ring shows your current day and phase, your next predicted period (with a likely range), and your estimated fertile window.
  • Browse the Calendar. See logged and predicted periods, your fertile window, and the days you've logged.
  • Open Insights. View your cycle-length history, how predictable your cycle is, and what each phase tends to involve.
  • Ask about a concern. Use I have a concern to get general, non-diagnostic information about things like a late period or heavy bleeding, with clear guidance on when to seek care.

Connecting with a partner

Duara has an optional private space for two. In the Us tab you can connect with one partner and then choose, item by item, what to share — nothing is shared just by connecting.

  • Connect. One person taps Invite my partner and sends the private invite link; the other opens it on their device and accepts. Both people confirm on their own Apple ID. You can unpair instantly at any time.
  • Cycle-aware support. If you share your cycle, your partner sees a gentle, non-clinical view — your phase and a "how to show up today" card. You choose the level (phase only, dates only, or more), and your fertile window is never shared.
  • Mood, on their Lock Screen. Your partner can see your mood of the day as a Home/Lock Screen widget and, for an hour at a time, as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
  • Location. Off by default. When you turn it on, you choose how much (last-known on request, on arrival, for a set time, or live) and you can stop instantly.
  • Chat & dates. A private 1-to-1 chat and a shared calendar with anniversary and birthday countdowns.

Every kind of sharing is something you turn on yourself and can revoke with one tap. See the Privacy Policy for how partner data travels (Apple's CloudKit sharing — never our servers).

FAQ

How accurate are the predictions?

Predictions are based on the cycles you log. Every cycle naturally varies, so Duara always shows a likely range and a confidence level rather than a single guaranteed date — and it gets sharper the more you log. Ovulation and the fertile window are estimates and can be off by several days.

Can I use Duara to avoid pregnancy?

No. Duara is not a contraceptive and has not been cleared by the FDA to prevent pregnancy. You can become pregnant on any day of your cycle. To avoid pregnancy, use a proven method of contraception and talk to a healthcare provider.

Why is my period late?

A late period is very common and is usually a delayed one — stress, illness, travel, disrupted sleep, intense training, weight change, or starting/stopping birth control can all push ovulation (and your period) later. If your period might be late and pregnancy is possible, a home test is the simplest way to check. Open I have a concern in the app for general information, and see a clinician if a period is more than a week late without explanation or you have no period for 90+ days.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes — Duara works on iPhone and iPad.

How do I delete my data?

Open Me → Delete all data to permanently remove everything from your device (and your iCloud, if sync is on). Deleting the app also removes its on-device data.
