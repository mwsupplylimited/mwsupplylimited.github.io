Vivace

A high-resolution, lossless music player for audiophiles. Play the files you own — FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, DSD and more — the way they were meant to sound. For iPhone, iPad & Mac.

About

Vivace is a local music player built for people who care about sound quality. It plays your own high-resolution and lossless files with a clean signal path, shows the true source format and bit depth of every track (“FLAC 24-bit/96kHz”, “DSD64”), and organises your collection by album, artist, genre, folder and playlist. No streaming subscription or Vivace login is required.

Supported formats

Vivace plays FLAC, Apple Lossless (ALAC), WAV, AIFF, CAF, AAC, MP3, Apple M4A and Opus natively, and DSD (.dsf and uncompressed .dff, DSD64) by decoding it to high-rate PCM for playback. DRM-protected tracks (for example Apple Music streams) can’t be opened — you need a file you own.

Getting started

  • Add your music. Choose + in the Library, or File › Add Files / Add Folder on Mac. Select audio files or a folder through Apple’s file picker. You can copy music into Vivace or use supported original folders without a second audio copy. Linked folders need their drive or file provider to remain available.
  • Open in Vivace. From any other app or the Files app, choose Share › Vivace (or “Open in Vivace”) to import audio. You can also drop files into Vivace over a cable using Finder’s Files tab.
  • Browse and play. Choose Songs, Albums, Artists, Genres, Folders or Playlists using the Library buttons or the sidebar on Mac and wider iPad layouts. Select a track to play.

Now Playing

  • A large artwork view, a precise scrubber, and full transport (shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat).
  • A format badge showing the true source resolution of the track.
  • An output picker to send audio to AirPlay, Bluetooth, headphones or a connected USB DAC.
  • Up Next queue and Lyrics, available from the player.
  • Background playback and system playback controls. On Mac, closing the window keeps playback running; use Window › Vivace to reopen it.

Gapless & last-position memory

Tracks within an album of the same format play back-to-back with no silence. Vivace remembers where you stopped in each track and offers to resume it next time — great for long mixes, live sets and audiobooks.

Equalizer

A 10-band graphic equalizer with a preamp and a set of presets (Rock, Jazz, Classical, Bass Boost, Vocal and more) is included free for everyone.

CUE sheets

Drop a single-file album (one big .flac/.wav plus a .cue) into a folder and import it — Vivace reads the cue sheet and splits it into proper, individually-playable tracks, with the titles and timings from the cue. This is included free.

Lyrics

Vivace shows lyrics embedded in your files, including time-synced lyrics that highlight line-by-line as the song plays. If a track has no embedded lyrics, Vivace Pro can look them up online from LRCLIB, a free community lyrics database. The lookup sends the track title, artist, album and duration. LRCLIB also receives normal network connection information; see the Privacy Policy.

DSD (Pro)

Vivace Pro plays 1-bit DSD files by decoding them to high-resolution PCM on-device. The Now Playing badge always shows the original DSD rate. Vivace outputs the decoded PCM to built-in outputs and external DACs. It does not send native 1-bit DSD or DSD over PCM (DoP).

FAQ

Does Vivace stream from Spotify or Apple Music?

No. Streaming catalogues are DRM-protected and can’t be opened by third-party players. Vivace plays files you own — purchased downloads, your own rips, DAW exports, and so on.

Why decode DSD to PCM?

Vivace decodes DSD to high-rate PCM on your device so it can play through supported outputs. The source badge still identifies the original DSD file. Connecting a USB DAC does not change this into native DSD or DoP output.

Where are my files stored?

Copied imports live in Vivace’s storage. Linked music stays in the original folder you chose; removing that link from Vivace leaves the originals intact. Optional iCloud Backup copies eligible music to your private iCloud and uses your storage quota. Your music is not uploaded to us. See the Privacy Policy.

It says “DSD requires Pro” / a feature is locked.

DSD playback, automatic online lyrics, ReplayGain, every theme and accent colour, and unlimited playlists are part of Vivace Pro — which also removes ads. EQ presets and tone controls, CUE-sheet albums and embedded & synced lyrics are free. Custom and per-output EQ are part of Pro.
