Vivace Privacy Policy

Effective 8 June 2026 · Last updated 13 August 2026

Summary: Vivace has no accounts, and we (the developer) never receive your music or personal information. Your audio files stay on your device unless you switch on iCloud Backup (a Vivace Pro feature, off by default), which copies them to your own iCloud account. Vivace uses a privacy-first analytics service (TelemetryDeck) that measures anonymous, aggregate usage — never personal data or listening history — and you can switch it off in Settings. If you are signed in to iCloud, the songs you like or dislike sync through your own iCloud account so your taste follows you between devices — that goes to Apple, never to us. The free version shows ads served by Unity Ads, which uses a device advertising identifier. The optional online-lyrics feature sends only a track’s title, artist, album and length to a lyrics database. Vivace Pro removes the ads.

1. Who we are

Vivace is published by MW Supply Limited (“we”, “us”). We operate no servers of our own and run no account system. There is no login.

2. Information we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Vivace has no servers, no database, and no account system of our own. We cannot see your songs or your library. We do collect anonymous, aggregate usage analytics through TelemetryDeck (section 8) — counts and feature-usage events that are never tied to your identity and contain none of your content — and you can turn this off. Apart from that, data leaves your device only to the third-party services described below (advertising, online lyrics, and payments), each of which you can avoid, and to your own iCloud account (section 5), which only you and Apple can see.

3. Audio files you add

When you add music, Vivace uses Apple’s Files picker or accepts files shared to it from another app. The files you choose are copied into the app’s private storage on your device and read locally for playback. They are never sent to us or to any third party.

4. On-device processing

All audio decoding — including DSD-to-PCM conversion, the equalizer, and metadata, artwork and lyric extraction — happens locally on your device. No part of your audio is sent off the device for processing.

5. iCloud

Vivace uses your own iCloud account — never a server of ours — to carry a small amount of data between your devices. It is stored in your personal iCloud under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to it and no way to read it.

  • Likes and ratings. If you are signed in to iCloud, the songs you mark liked or disliked sync automatically so your taste follows you between devices. What syncs is a short list of track title, artist, album and your like/dislike — no audio, no device identifiers, nothing about how or when you listen. If you are not signed in to iCloud, nothing syncs and the app works exactly as before.
  • iCloud Backup & Sync (Vivace Pro, off by default). If you turn it on, Vivace copies your library to your own private iCloud storage so you can restore it on a new device: the audio files themselves, plus their details (title, artist, album, artwork, format, play count, rating, last position) and your playlists. It goes to your personal iCloud account under Apple’s Privacy Policy and counts against your iCloud storage. We never receive it and cannot read it. It is off unless you switch it on, it can be limited to Wi-Fi, and you can turn it off or remove the iCloud copy at any time from Settings › iCloud Backup.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Everything Vivace stores lives either on your device or in your own iCloud account:

  • On your device. Deleting the app removes the music you imported into it, along with its settings, artwork and caches.
  • In your iCloud. Your likes/ratings and (if you enabled it) your iCloud Backup live in your personal iCloud account. Settings › iCloud Backup › Remove Backup from iCloud deletes the backed-up copy. Signing out of iCloud stops all syncing.
  • Third parties. Ad data held by Unity and purchase records held by Apple are governed by their own policies, linked in this policy.

7. Advertising (free version)

The free version of Vivace shows a banner on the library/browse screens, and occasionally a full-screen ad when you reopen the app. Ads are never shown over the player or during playback. Ads are served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and handled under Unity’s Privacy Policy. To serve ads, Unity may access a device advertising identifier and limited technical/usage data, including an approximate location inferred from your IP address. Vivace also includes the ironSource Ad Quality SDK, which Unity uses to detect invalid and fraudulent ad traffic.

  • App Tracking Transparency. On first launch Vivace asks for your permission before any tracking. If you decline (or haven’t granted it), ads are served in a non-personalised mode.
  • Non-personalised by default. Ads are served in a non-personalised mode unless you have authorised tracking through App Tracking Transparency.
  • Removing ads. Upgrading to Vivace Pro removes all ads, and no ad requests are made for Pro users.

8. Analytics (anonymous)

Vivace uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service, to understand how the app is used in aggregate so we can improve it. TelemetryDeck is designed to collect no personal data: it does not use the device advertising identifier, does not build a cross-app profile of you, and needs no tracking permission. Vivace sends only anonymous, non-identifying events — for example that the app launched, that an import finished (as a rounded count range, never file names or titles), that a track played (with only its audio format, e.g. “FLAC”), or that a paywall or a Pro feature was shown or used. We never send your songs, titles, artists, albums, playlists, file paths, email address, or listening history. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Share Anonymous Usage Data; when it is off, nothing is initialised or sent.

9. Online lyrics (Pro, optional)

If you enable online lyrics (a Vivace Pro feature, controlled by a setting), Vivace looks up lyrics from LRCLIB, a free community lyrics database. To find a match, Vivace sends only the track title, artist, album and duration of the song you are playing. No account, device identifier, location, or personal information is sent. Fetched lyrics are cached on your device. Leave the setting off (or stay on the free tier) and no lyric look-ups are made.

10. Payments

Vivace Pro is sold through the App Store as an auto-renewing subscription or a one-time purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. Apple may share aggregate, non-identifying sales information with us.

11. Permissions

Vivace does not request access to your microphone, contacts, photos, or location, and never reads your device’s location services. (The ad network can still infer an approximate, city-level location from your IP address, as described in section 7 — that is why the App Store privacy label lists coarse location.) It asks for tracking permission only for the purpose of personalised advertising in the free version (App Tracking Transparency); you may decline. The Files picker shown when you import music is run by iOS itself and only returns the items you choose.

12. Children

Vivace is not directed at children. The free version serves ads in a non-personalised mode unless you grant tracking permission.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how Vivace handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy labels for the affected version.

14. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
