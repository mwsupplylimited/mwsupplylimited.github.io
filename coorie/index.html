Coorie

Baby monitor for the devices you already own. Version 1.0

About

Coorie turns a spare iPhone or iPad into a baby monitor — no extra hardware to buy. One device sits by your baby and streams live video and audio; another device, signed in to the same Apple ID, watches the feed. There's no pairing, no codes, and no approval step — because both devices are already yours, they simply connect. Over your home Wi‑Fi it's free. Coorie Pro adds cry and motion alerts, two-way talk-back, soothing lullabies, recording, multiple cameras, Picture-in-Picture, and removes ads.

The big idea: Coorie connects your own devices directly, peer-to-peer, through your private iCloud — so your baby's video and audio stay between your devices and are end-to-end encrypted. There's no separate account to make and no stranger's server in the middle.

Getting started

  • Open Coorie on the device by your baby and choose “Baby Station.” Prop it up with a clear view of the crib and keep it plugged in.
  • On your own device, open Coorie and choose “Parent Viewer.”
  • Make sure both devices are signed in to the SAME Apple ID / iCloud account. That's what links them.
  • The feed connects automatically — no codes, no pairing, no approval needed. You'll see and hear your baby within moments.

What you can do

On the free tier over home Wi‑Fi, the Parent Viewer shows your baby's live video and audio in real time. Coorie Pro unlocks more:

  • Cry & motion alerts — get notified on your device when Coorie detects crying or movement.
  • Two-way talk-back — speak to your baby through the Baby Station to soothe them.
  • Lullabies — play gentle sounds from the Baby Station, right from the Parent Viewer.
  • Recording — capture and save clips from the feed.
  • Multiple cameras — set up more than one Baby Station and switch between rooms.
  • Ad-free — a completely ad-free app.

FAQ

How does it connect without a server?

Coorie uses your private iCloud to introduce your two devices to each other, then streams the video and audio peer-to-peer directly between them. The media stream is end-to-end encrypted, so it stays between your own devices. There's no Coorie account and no Coorie server that sees your feed.

Does it work in the background?

iOS only lets the camera run while the app is open, so keep the baby device awake and plugged in. You can dim the screen so it doesn't light up the room while the camera keeps streaming. Audio continues briefly in the background, but for a continuous feed, keep the Baby Station on screen and on power.

Is my baby's video private?

Yes. The feed stays within your own iCloud account and is end-to-end encrypted (DTLS-SRTP). Coorie has no servers that see your video — it never passes through MW Supply Limited. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What do I need?

Two Apple devices (iPhone or iPad) signed in to the same Apple ID, both running iOS 17 or later, and a shared Wi‑Fi network.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Coorie, and cancel. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro access until the end of the current period.
