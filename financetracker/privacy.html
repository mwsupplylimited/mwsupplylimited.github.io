Money Flow Privacy Policy

Effective 29 May 2026 · Last updated 29 May 2026

Summary: Money Flow collects no personal data, has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. Everything you enter stays on your device, and — only if you choose — syncs through your own private iCloud account. We never see your financial data.

1. Information we collect

None. Money Flow does not ask for, collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information on any server. We operate no backend and have no account system — you can use every feature without signing in.

2. Information stored locally on your device

Money Flow stores the data you enter on your device only, using Apple’s standard on-device database (SwiftData/SQLite), so the app remembers it between launches:

  • Your transactions (amount, date, note, expense or income, and the category and account you assign).
  • Your categories and accounts, including their names, icons, colors, and starting balances.
  • Your settings (currency, default account, appearance, and the iCloud sync toggle).

The database lives in a private app container shared with the Money Flow widget so the widget can display your totals. This data never leaves your device except via the iCloud mechanism described below, and it is automatically removed if you delete the app.

3. iCloud sync

If iCloud is signed in on your device and the sync toggle in Settings is on, your transactions, categories, accounts, and settings are mirrored to your personal iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit private database. This keeps your data in sync across your iPhone and iPad and lets it return after you reinstall the app. The data is stored in your own iCloud account — MW Supply Limited has no access to it, and iCloud is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. If you turn iCloud sync off or are not signed in to iCloud, all data simply stays on the device.

4. Network activity

Money Flow makes no network requests of its own. It has no servers to talk to. The only network activity is Apple’s own iCloud synchronization, which happens at the operating-system level when you enable iCloud sync.

5. Importing and exporting data

Money Flow can import transactions from a CSV file you choose, and export your data as CSV or Excel files. Imported files are read locally; exported files are saved or shared only to the destination you pick through the standard iOS document and share sheets. These files are under your control and are never sent to us.

6. Third-party services

Money Flow contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics frameworks, no advertising networks, and no crash reporting other than Apple’s first-party crash reporting (which is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and only runs if you have opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

7. Permissions

Money Flow requests no special privacy permissions. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or health data. Choosing files for import or export uses the system document picker, which grants the app access only to the specific file you select.

8. Children

Money Flow is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. It collects no data of any kind from any user, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how Money Flow handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy nutrition labels for the affected version of the app.

10. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
