Money Flow Privacy Policy

Effective 29 May 2026 · Last updated 22 August 2026

Summary: Your financial data stays on your device and — only if you choose — syncs through your own private iCloud account. We never see it, we hold no copy of it, and we run no servers. On iPhone and iPad the free version shows ads, which collect limited device and ad-interaction data; ads are non-personalised by default. On Mac there are no ads at all and no advertising SDK is built into the app. Upgrading to Money Flow Pro removes all ads on every device.

Which ad network? It depends on your version. Version 1.10 and earlier use Google AdMob, with Google’s consent form where required. Version 1.11 and later remove Google’s SDKs entirely and use Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) with Unity Ads. Section 4 describes each one separately.

1. Who we are

Money Flow is developed by MW Supply Limited (“we”), which trades as Matthew Wong on the App Store — that is the seller name you will see on the store listing and on your receipt, and it refers to the same developer as “MW Supply Limited” throughout this policy. MW Supply Limited is the data controller for the limited processing described here, and can be reached at mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.

This policy explains what data the app handles, what stays on your device, and what is processed by the limited third-party services we use to show ads and offer an optional subscription. It covers Money Flow on iPhone, iPad and Mac. We have no account system and operate no backend — you can use every feature without signing in to us, and we hold no copy of your financial data on any server of ours.

2. Information stored locally on your device

Money Flow stores the data you enter on your device only, using Apple’s standard on-device database (SwiftData/SQLite), so the app remembers it between launches:

  • Your transactions (amount, date, note, type — expense, income, or transfer — and the category and accounts you assign), and any receipt photo you choose to attach to a transaction.
  • Your categories and accounts, including their names, icons, colors, starting balances, and per-category budgets.
  • Your savings goals and recurring transaction rules.
  • Your settings (currency, monthly budget, default account, the app-lock and daily-reminder preferences, and the iCloud sync toggle).

The database lives in a private app container shared with the Money Flow widget so the widget can display your totals. This data never leaves your device except via the iCloud mechanism described below, and it is automatically removed if you delete the app. We have no access to it.

3. iCloud sync

If iCloud is signed in on your device and the sync toggle in Settings is on, your transactions (including any receipt photos), categories, accounts, budgets, savings goals, recurring rules, and settings are mirrored to your personal iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit private database. This keeps your data in sync across your iPhone, iPad and Mac and lets it return after you reinstall the app. The data is stored in your own iCloud account — MW Supply Limited has no access to it, and iCloud is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. If you turn iCloud sync off or are not signed in to iCloud, all data simply stays on the device.

4. Advertising

What this section says depends on which version you are running, and on which device. Advertising in Money Flow changed between versions, and this page is the App Store privacy policy for all of them, so each state is described separately below. You can check your version in the app under Settings → About, or on the App Store listing. Subscribers to Money Flow Pro see no ads at all in every version, on every device: while you are a Pro subscriber the app does not request or display ads, and the ad SDK is not started.

Money Flow for Mac shows no ads at any tier. The macOS version of Money Flow contains no advertising SDK of any kind — neither Google’s nor Unity’s is built into the Mac app, so nothing on your Mac requests an ad, no advertising identifier is read, and no ad network receives anything from a Mac install. Free or Pro, the Mac app is ad-free. Everything in this section that follows applies to iPhone and iPad only.

Version 1.10 and earlier (iPhone and iPad) — Google AdMob

In these versions the free tier shows banner ads and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads provided by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

  • What Google may process: your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), approximate device information (model, operating-system version, language and settings), your IP address and the coarse region derived from it, and ad-interaction data (which ads were requested, shown or tapped) — used to select and measure ads and to detect invalid traffic and fraud.
  • Your consent: iOS first asks for App Tracking Transparency permission, and the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking. In addition, these versions include Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), which presents an in-app consent form where one is required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) and records your choice. If you decline, you still see ads — just non-personalised ones.
  • Managing your choices: tracking permission can be changed at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Google’s policies: Google’s advertising policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Version 1.11 and later (iPhone and iPad) — Unity LevelPlay (ironSource)

From version 1.11, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform are removed from the app entirely — they are no longer built into Money Flow, and no Google advertising service receives anything from a 1.11 or later install. The free tier’s banner and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads are instead served through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation, with Unity Ads as the demand network. To serve these ads, Unity may collect and process:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • A non-personal device/usage identifier, device type, operating-system version and settings;
  • Your IP address and the approximate (coarse) region derived from it;
  • Information about how you interact with ads (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped).

This information is collected and used by Unity Technologies as an independent data controller, not by us. It is handled under Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. MW Supply Limited only receives aggregate, non-identifying reports such as the number of ad impressions and estimated revenue. Your financial data is never shared with Unity or used to target ads, and we never volunteer a user identifier to the ad SDK.

No in-app consent dialog. Unlike the Google stack it replaced, LevelPlay ships no consent-form equivalent, so from version 1.11 Money Flow presents no in-app consent dialog. Instead the app takes the privacy-protective position by default: it reports no consent to personalised advertising, and asserts the California “do not sell / do not share” signal, for everyone, from the first launch.

Personalised ads are opt-in and default to OFF. Ads are non-personalised for everyone unless you affirmatively opt in using the Personalized Ads switch under Settings → Ad Privacy. That switch is off until you turn it on, you can turn it back off at any time, and your choice is sent to the ad network immediately. This is separate from the App Tracking Transparency prompt — allowing tracking does not by itself turn personalised ads on.

App Tracking Transparency. In both versions, iOS asks whether Money Flow may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” the advertising identifier is not used and you will see only non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Removing ads. Subscribing to Money Flow Pro removes all banner and interstitial ads in every version. While you are a Pro subscriber, the app does not request or display ads.

5. Money Flow Pro subscription

Money Flow Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription (available monthly or yearly, each with a 14-day free trial) that removes all ads and unlocks premium features such as advanced insights, custom accent themes, and alternate app icons, and that lifts the 10-transaction limit of the free Mac preview. It is a Universal Purchase, so one subscription covers your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Purchases, billing, renewals, and refunds are handled entirely by Apple through your App Store account — we never see or store your name, your payment-card details, or your Apple ID. To unlock Pro features, the app checks your purchase entitlement directly with the App Store on your device; this check does not identify you to us. You can view, change, or cancel your subscription at any time in the App Store under Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

6. Network activity

Money Flow has no servers of its own. Besides the advertising and subscription services described above and Apple’s own iCloud synchronization, the app makes one kind of request of its own: to fetch currency exchange rates (see below). It sends no personal or financial data with that request.

6a. Exchange rates (multi-currency)

If you record transactions in more than one currency, Money Flow converts them to your chosen display currency. To do this it downloads daily exchange rates from a third-party provider, open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API’s free, key-less endpoint). The request contains no account, identifier, or transaction data — only a standard web request for the public rate table, which necessarily includes your device’s IP address as with any internet request. The rates are cached on your device so the feature works offline, and the app ships with a built-in fallback table. We do not send your financial data anywhere, and no personal data is shared with the rate provider.

7. Importing and exporting data

Money Flow can import transactions from a CSV file you choose, and export your data as CSV or Excel files. Imported files are read locally; exported files are saved or shared only to the destination you pick through the standard iOS document and share sheets. These files are under your control and are never sent to us.

8. Third-party services

Which advertising SDK is built into Money Flow depends on your version, and on whether you are running the iPhone/iPad app or the Mac app:

  • Money Flow for Mac (all versions) — no advertising SDK at all. Neither Google’s nor Unity’s libraries are built into the Mac app.
  • iPhone and iPad, version 1.10 and earlier — the Google Mobile Ads SDK together with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), governed by Google’s advertising policy and Google’s privacy policy.
  • iPhone and iPad, version 1.11 and later — the Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation SDK with the Unity Ads adapter, and nothing else. It is governed by Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. In this version the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform are removed from the app entirely.

No other advertising or mediation network is built into any version of Money Flow. Money Flow contains no analytics frameworks and no crash reporting other than Apple’s first-party crash reporting (which is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and only runs if you opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements). Money Flow Pro subscribers are not shown ads, and the app does not request ads for them.

9. Permissions and identifiers

Money Flow does not access your location, microphone, contacts, or health data. The camera is used only when you choose to scan a receipt, and all processing happens on your device (see below). The advertising identifier is used only for ads in the free version on iPhone and iPad, and only if you allow tracking as described in section 4 — the Mac app never reads it, because it links no advertising SDK. The other system features the app can use are optional and stay on your device:

  • Camera. If you scan a receipt, the camera captures the photo and all text recognition runs on your device using Apple’s Vision framework. The receipt image and the details read from it stay on your device — they are never sent to us or any third party.
  • Face ID / Touch ID. If you turn on app lock, authentication is performed by iOS. The app only receives a yes/no result and never sees your passcode or biometric data.
  • Photos. If you attach or scan a receipt from your library, you pick the image through Apple’s system photo picker, which runs separately from the app and shares only the single photo you select — the app is not granted access to your photo library.
  • Notifications. If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.
  • Files. Importing or exporting uses the system document picker, which grants access only to the specific file you choose.

10. Legal basis for processing (UK/EU GDPR)

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, the UK GDPR and EU GDPR require us to tell you the legal basis on which each kind of processing is carried out. Because almost everything Money Flow does happens on your own device, the list is short:

  • The financial data you enter (Article 6(1)(b) — performance of a contract). Storing your transactions, categories, accounts, budgets, goals and settings on your device is what the app is for; it is necessary to provide the service you asked for. We are not a recipient of this data — it stays on your device and, at your option, in your own iCloud.
  • iCloud sync (Article 6(1)(a) — consent). Sync is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings. The data is stored in your own Apple iCloud account under your agreement with Apple.
  • Non-personalised advertising in the free tier (Article 6(1)(f) — legitimate interests). Showing a limited number of non-personalised ads is how the free version is funded. We have balanced this against your rights: no advertising identifier is used unless you allow tracking, no financial data is ever shared with an ad network, and Money Flow Pro removes ads entirely. You may object to this processing at any time (see section 12).
  • Personalised advertising and use of the advertising identifier (Article 6(1)(a) — consent). This happens only where you have affirmatively opted in — through the App Tracking Transparency prompt, through Google’s consent form in version 1.10 and earlier, and through the Personalized Ads switch in version 1.11 and later. Consent can be withdrawn at any time, with no effect on the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Purchases and subscription entitlement (Article 6(1)(b) — performance of a contract). Handled by Apple; we receive no payment or identity data.

11. How long data is kept

We hold no copy of your data, so in almost every case the retention period is set by you, not by us:

  • Your financial data (on device). Kept until you delete it or delete the app. Deleting the app removes the on-device database and any receipt photos with it. Transactions you delete inside the app go to Recently Deleted first; you can delete them permanently from there straight away, and anything left is purged automatically after 30 days.
  • Your iCloud copy. Kept in your own iCloud account until you delete the data, turn sync off and remove it, or delete the app’s iCloud data through iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. It is subject to Apple’s retention practices, not ours.
  • Cached exchange rates. Overwritten on each refresh and removed when the app is deleted; they contain no personal data.
  • Advertising data. Held by the ad provider (Google for version 1.10 and earlier, Unity for 1.11 and later) under its own retention schedule, described in its privacy policy linked in section 4. We do not control it and never receive it.
  • Aggregate ad reports we do see (impression counts and estimated revenue) contain no personal data and are retained by the ad platform for its standard reporting window.
  • Support email. If you email us, we keep that correspondence only as long as needed to deal with your question, and no longer than 24 months.

12. Your privacy rights

Under the UK/EU GDPR and comparable laws you have the rights below. Because we operate no servers and hold no copy of your data, most of them are exercised directly in the app rather than by asking us — which is faster, and does not require you to identify yourself to anyone:

  • Access. All of your data is visible in the app at any time. Settings → Import / Export also produces a complete copy.
  • Rectification. Edit or correct any transaction, category, account, budget or goal directly in the app.
  • Erasure. Delete individual records in the app, clear Recently Deleted, or delete the app to remove everything on the device. To erase the iCloud copy, delete the app’s data through iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
  • Portability. Settings → Import / Export exports your data as CSV or Excel, and the backup feature writes a complete machine-readable backup to Files or iCloud Drive — both are open formats you can take anywhere.
  • Restriction. Turning iCloud sync off, and subscribing to Money Flow Pro (or simply using the Mac app), stop the corresponding processing.
  • Objection to processing for advertising. You can object to ad-funded processing at any time by subscribing to Money Flow Pro, which stops all ad requests; you can also switch personalised ads off and deny tracking permission, so that no advertising identifier is used.
  • Withdrawing consent. Turn off the Personalized Ads switch in Settings → Ad Privacy (version 1.11 and later), and change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Withdrawal takes effect immediately and does not affect processing carried out before it.
  • No automated decision-making. Money Flow performs no profiling or automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. Insights, forecasts and subscription detection are computed on your device for your own use only.

If you would like help exercising any of these rights, email mwsupplylimited@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. Please note that we cannot access, retrieve or delete your data for you — we do not have it — so our help will take the form of instructions for doing it on your device. Requests about data held by an advertising provider should be directed to that provider using the policy links in section 4.

Right to complain. If you believe your data has been handled unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO); in the EEA, the supervisory authority of the country where you live or work (a list is published by the European Data Protection Board). We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first, but you are not required to contact us before complaining.

13. International transfers

MW Supply Limited transfers no personal data internationally, because we receive none: your financial data never reaches us, and never leaves your device except to your own iCloud account. Two of the third parties described above do operate internationally:

  • Apple (iCloud sync, App Store purchases) stores and processes data in its own global infrastructure under Apple’s privacy policy, using its own transfer safeguards.
  • The advertising provider for your version — Google (version 1.10 and earlier) or Unity Technologies (version 1.11 and later) — may process the limited ad data described in section 4 outside the UK/EEA, including in the United States, relying on Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU-US and UK-US Data Privacy Framework, or equivalent safeguards as set out in their respective privacy policies. Each acts as an independent controller for that processing.
  • The exchange-rate provider receives no personal data beyond the IP address inherent in any web request, as described in section 6a.

14. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA gives you specific rights. MW Supply Limited does not collect, receive, store, sell or share your personal information: the app has no account system, no analytics and no backend, and your financial data never reaches us.

  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and we never have. In version 1.11 and later, Money Flow additionally asserts the “do not sell / do not share” signal to the ad network by default, for every user, without you having to ask — and it reports no consent to personalised advertising unless you turn the Personalized Ads switch on yourself. Turning that switch off again reinstates the signal immediately.
  • Right to know / access. The categories of personal information involved are identifiers (an advertising identifier, only where you have allowed tracking), internet or network activity (ad interactions), and coarse geolocation inferred from an IP address — all of it collected by the advertising provider as an independent business, not by us. Your own financial records are not disclosed to anyone and remain visible and exportable in the app at any time.
  • Right to delete. Exercised directly on the device: delete records in the app, clear Recently Deleted, or delete the app. We hold nothing to delete.
  • Right to correct. Edit any record directly in the app.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. Money Flow does not collect sensitive personal information for us or disclose it to any third party. Your financial records stay on your device and are never used for advertising.
  • Right to opt out of targeted advertising. Already the default. You may additionally deny App Tracking Transparency permission, leave Personalized Ads off, subscribe to Money Flow Pro to remove ads entirely, or use the Mac app, which contains no advertising SDK at all.
  • Non-discrimination. Exercising any of these rights does not change the price of Money Flow Pro or restrict any feature.

To make a California privacy request, email mwsupplylimited@gmail.com. You may use an authorised agent. We will respond within 45 days.

15. Children (COPPA)

Money Flow is a general-audience budgeting app. It is not directed to children under 13 within the meaning of the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and it is not directed to children under the applicable age of digital consent in the UK or EEA (13–16 depending on the country). It is rated 4+ only because it contains no objectionable content, not because it is designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Consistent with this, the app declares itself to its advertising partner as a general-audience, not child-directed app, so no child-directed treatment or mixed-audience ad handling is requested on its behalf. No age or date of birth is ever collected, because there is no account system.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to an advertising provider through Money Flow, contact us at mwsupplylimited@gmail.com and we will help you direct the request to that provider. Note that a child’s entries in the app itself stay on the device and are removed by deleting the app. Subscribing to Money Flow Pro removes all ads, and the Mac app shows none at any tier.
