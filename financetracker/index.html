Money Flow: Budget & Expenses

Scan receipts, track spending, and stay on top of your budget — fast and private, on your device and your own iCloud. Version 1.5

About

Money Flow is a fast, private personal-finance tracker for iPhone and iPad. Log expenses, income, and transfers in seconds, organize them with customizable categories and accounts, set budgets and savings goals, and understand your habits through dashboards and insights built with Swift Charts. There are no accounts to create, and your financial data stays on your device and your own private iCloud — we never see it. The free version is supported by ads; upgrading to Money Flow Pro removes all ads and unlocks advanced insights, custom themes, and alternate app icons.

Getting started

  • Add a transaction. Tap the + button, enter an amount, pick expense or income, and choose a category and account. Add an optional note and date.
  • Customize categories and accounts. The app starts with sensible defaults (Groceries, Dining, Rent, Salary, Checking, Savings, and more). Edit names, icons, and colors, or add your own in Settings.
  • See your insights. The Dashboard and Insights tabs break down spending by category and over time so you can spot trends at a glance.
  • Add the widget. Long-press your Home or Lock Screen, tap +, and add the Money Flow widget to see your balance and recent activity without opening the app.
  • Quick add with Siri & Shortcuts. Money Flow supports "Add Expense" and "Add Income" actions you can run from Shortcuts.

iCloud sync

When you are signed in to iCloud and the toggle in Settings → iCloud is on, your transactions, categories, accounts, budgets, savings goals, recurring items, and settings sync automatically across your iPhone and iPad through your own private iCloud account. Because the data lives in your iCloud, it also comes back if you reinstall the app on a new or reset device. If you prefer to keep everything on a single device, just leave iCloud sync off — the app works fully offline.

Importing & exporting

You can bring existing records in from a .csv file and export your data as CSV or Excel at any time from Settings. Exports go wherever you choose through the standard iOS share sheet — Files, Mail, AirDrop, and so on.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in Apple’s standard on-device database. If you enable iCloud sync, a copy is mirrored to your personal iCloud account so it stays in sync across your devices. We never receive or store your financial data.

Will my data survive if I delete and reinstall the app?

If iCloud sync was enabled, yes — your data is restored from your iCloud account when you reinstall and sign in to the same Apple Account. If iCloud sync was off, the data is stored only on the device and is removed when you delete the app, so export a backup first if you plan to delete it.

Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes. With iCloud sync enabled on both devices and the same Apple Account signed in, changes flow between them automatically.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. Money Flow has no sign-in and works completely offline. iCloud sync is the only feature that uses the network, and it is optional.

What file formats can I import and export?

Import accepts CSV files. Export produces CSV and Excel (.xlsx) files.

How do recurring transactions work?

In Settings → Recurring Transactions, add an item with an amount, category, account, and how often it repeats (daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly). Money Flow posts each one automatically when it’s due — including any that came due while the app was closed — and you can pause, edit, or delete a rule at any time.

How do transfers and account balances work?

Choose Transfer when adding a transaction to move money from one account to another. Transfers don’t count as income or expense; they simply adjust each account’s balance. Current balances appear on the Dashboard and in Settings → Manage Accounts, calculated from each account’s starting balance plus its activity.

How do I lock the app?

Turn on Settings → Security to require Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode each time you open Money Flow. Authentication is handled entirely by iOS; the app never sees your passcode or biometric data.

Where are receipt photos stored?

Receipt photos you attach are stored on your device alongside the transaction, and — if iCloud sync is on — in your own private iCloud. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
