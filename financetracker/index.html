About

Money Flow is a fast, private personal-finance tracker for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Log expenses, income, and transfers in seconds, organize them with customizable categories and accounts, set budgets and savings goals, and understand your habits through dashboards and spending charts.

There is no bank linking. Money Flow never asks for a bank login and uses no aggregator such as Plaid — you enter transactions yourself or import a CSV your bank already gives you. There are no accounts to create, and your financial data stays on your device and your own private iCloud — we never see it.

Getting started

  • Add a transaction. Tap the + button, enter an amount, pick expense or income, and choose a category and account. Add an optional note and date.
  • Customize categories and accounts. The app starts with sensible defaults (Groceries, Dining, Rent, Salary, Checking, Savings, and more). Edit names, icons, and colors, or add your own in Settings.
  • See your insights. The Dashboard and Insights tabs break down spending by category and over time so you can spot trends at a glance.
  • Add a widget (iPhone & iPad). Long-press your Home or Lock Screen, tap +, and add a Money Flow widget — your balance and recent activity, Safe to Spend on the Lock Screen and in StandBy, or the interactive Templates widget to queue a saved entry. Open Money Flow to save queued entries and refresh totals.
  • On the Mac, the same app runs in its own window with full keyboard and menu support. The free preview lets you keep up to 10 active user-entered transactions. Pro unlocks unlimited entry and premium tools.
  • Quick add with Siri & Shortcuts. Money Flow supports "Add Expense" and "Add Income" actions you can run from Shortcuts.

iCloud sync

When you are signed in to iCloud and the toggle in Settings → iCloud is on, your transactions, categories, accounts, budgets, savings goals, recurring items, and settings sync automatically across your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own private iCloud account — along with your merchants, tags, quick-add templates, saved filters and net-worth history. Records that successfully sync can return after reinstalling with the same iCloud account. Sync is enabled by default when iCloud is available. To keep new changes on one device, turn sync off and quit and reopen Money Flow. This does not delete an existing iCloud copy. Save a backup before deleting the app.

Importing & exporting

You can import records from a .csv file with column mapping and a preview, and export your data as CSV or Excel from Settings. CSV import requires Pro on Mac; CSV and Excel export are available free on every platform. Exports go wherever you choose through the standard system share sheet — Files, Mail, AirDrop, and so on — and the Mac app saves them straight to a folder you pick.

FAQ

Is Money Flow free? What is the limit on the Mac?

On iPhone and iPad the app is free with no transaction limit — core tracking is included. The upcoming 1.12 build 61 supports advertising with your consent; Pro shows no ads. On the Mac the app is completely ad-free, and the free download is instead a preview limited to 10 active user-entered transactions. You can browse and edit existing records, manage accounts and export your data. Pro is required for unlimited entry, CSV import, backup restore, new recurring rules and premium tools. Money Flow Pro lifts that limit and unlocks premium planning tools across your devices. Recurring bills the app posts for you and anything in the Bin do not count toward the ten.

Does Money Flow connect to my bank?

No — deliberately. Money Flow has no bank linking and uses no aggregator such as Plaid. It never asks for a bank login, so there is no credential to leak. You add transactions yourself, scan a receipt, or import a CSV your bank already gives you.

How much is Money Flow Pro, and is there a trial?

Money Flow Pro offers monthly and annual plans, with a 14-day introductory trial for eligible subscribers. One subscription covers your iPhone, iPad and Mac using the same App Store account. Your local price, billing period and trial eligibility are shown before purchase. You can cancel any time in the App Store under Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Does the app show ads?

The upcoming iPhone/iPad version 1.12 build 61 supports ads on the free tier after you choose Allow ads. Personalized ads are off by default and require a separate choice and Apple tracking permission. Declining tracking still allows non-personalized ads if you allow advertising. Choosing Continue without ads keeps advertising off and you can keep using the free app. Change these choices in Settings → Privacy. Pro shows no ads, and the Mac app is ad-free at every tier.

Build 61 is a planned replacement and is not yet available on the App Store. The earlier iOS 1.12 build 59 had no advertising SDKs and was not released; Mac 1.12 build 60 is available. Version 1.11 included advertising SDKs, but inclusion alone does not establish ad serving. See the Privacy Policy for version-specific behavior and provider data processing.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in Apple’s standard on-device database. Sync is enabled by default when iCloud is available, and mirrors records to your private iCloud account. To disable it, turn off Settings → iCloud, then quit and reopen the app. We never receive or store your financial data.

Will my data survive if I delete and reinstall the app?

Records that successfully synced can return when you reinstall and sign in to the same iCloud account. Unsynced changes may be lost when you delete the app. Let sync finish and save a separate backup before deleting Money Flow. If sync was off, a backup is needed to restore local-only records.

Does it sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes. With iCloud sync enabled on each device and the same Apple Account signed in, changes flow between them automatically.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No Money Flow account is required. You can record and browse your finances offline. iCloud sync, new exchange rates, subscription purchases and restores use the network. Advertising, when allowed and available in an advertising build, also uses the network. Cached exchange rates may be older while offline.

What file formats can I import and export?

Import accepts CSV files with column mapping and a preview. Export produces CSV and Excel (.xlsx) files; Pro also includes PDF reports. Full backups preserve app data for restore. Import and backup restore require Pro on Mac.

How do recurring transactions work?

In Settings → Planning → Subscriptions & Bills, add an item with an amount, category, account, and how often it repeats (daily, weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month on the 1st & 15th, monthly, or yearly). When Money Flow opens, it creates due entries, including occurrences missed while the app was closed. You can pause, edit or delete a rule; creating new recurring rules requires Pro on Mac.

How do transfers and account balances work?

Choose Transfer when adding a transaction to move money from one account to another. Transfers don’t count as income or expense; they simply adjust each account’s balance. Current balances appear on the Dashboard and in Settings → Manage Accounts, calculated from each account’s starting balance plus its activity.

How do I lock the app?

Turn on App Lock under Settings → Security. On iPhone and iPad, authentication uses Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode. On Mac, it uses Touch ID or your login password. Apple handles authentication; Money Flow does not receive your passcode, password or biometric data.

Where are receipt photos stored?

Receipt photos you attach are stored on your device alongside the transaction, and — if iCloud sync is on — in your own private iCloud. They are not sent to us. They are also included in backups you choose to save or share.
