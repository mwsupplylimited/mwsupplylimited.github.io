Ride Meter: Taxi Fare Meter

A worldwide taxi-fare meter for your pocket — estimate the running fare from real, official metered tariffs for 81+ cities and countries. Version 1.0

Please note: Ride Meter is an independent fare estimator. It is not an official or certified taximeter, and the actual metered fare charged by a taxi may differ. Use it to understand and sanity-check fares, not as a legal record of what you owe.

About

Ride Meter turns your iPhone into a worldwide taxi-fare meter. Using your device’s GPS, it measures your trip’s distance, speed, and waiting time, then computes a running fare from the real, official metered tariff for the region you select — flag-fall, per-distance and per-minute waiting charges, day/night/holiday rates, and known surcharges. It covers 81+ cities and countries across every continent, shows a live meter as you ride, produces an itemised receipt for every trip, keeps a trip history, and lets you see a full, transparent breakdown of each region’s rates — including the source and the year the tariff was last verified. The free version covers your home country and is supported by ads; Ride Meter Pro unlocks every region worldwide and removes all ads.

Features

  • Live meter. Watch the fare tick up in real time as you travel, driven by GPS distance, speed, and waiting time.
  • 81+ regions worldwide. Real, official metered tariffs for 81+ cities and countries across every continent.
  • Itemised receipts. Every trip ends with a clear, line-by-line receipt: flag-fall, distance, waiting time, applicable day/night/holiday rate, and surcharges.
  • Trip history. Look back over your past trips and their receipts.
  • Transparent rates. See exactly how each region’s fare is built up, along with the source and the year the tariff was last verified.
  • Day, night & holiday tariffs. The meter applies the correct rate band automatically where a region has them.

How to use

  • Pick your region. Choose the city or country whose tariff you want to use. Your home country’s regions are included free; alternate countries require Ride Meter Pro.
  • Allow location. Ride Meter needs location access to measure distance and speed. Grant the permission when iOS asks, or turn it on later in Settings → Ride Meter → Location.
  • Tap Start. Start the meter as your trip begins.
  • Drive. The live meter updates as you move and wait, applying the region’s flag-fall, per-distance, per-minute waiting, and time-of-day rates.
  • Tap Stop. End the trip to see and save an itemised receipt. It’s added to your trip history.

FAQ

Is the fare accurate?

Ride Meter gives an estimate built from each region’s official, published metered tariff — flag-fall, per-distance and per-minute waiting rates, time-of-day bands, and known surcharges. It is not a certified taximeter, and the fare a taxi actually charges can differ because of route, rounding, local surcharges, tolls, traffic, or tariff changes. Treat the number as a well-informed guide, not an exact bill. Each region also shows the source and the year its tariff was last verified.

Why does Ride Meter need my location?

It uses your location to measure how far and how fast you travel and how long you wait, which is what the meter needs to compute the fare. Your location is used on your device for this purpose only — it is not sold or sent to us. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get the other countries?

The free version covers your home country’s regions. Subscribe to Ride Meter Pro to unlock all 81+ regions worldwide, remove ads, keep unlimited exportable trip history, and back up to iCloud.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device, select Ride Meter, and turn off renewal. Subscriptions are managed entirely through your Apple Account; we cannot start, change, or cancel them for you.
