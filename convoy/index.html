Convoy

Drive road trips together — a live convoy for a group in separate cars. Version 1.0

About

Convoy keeps a group of friends driving in separate cars together on a road trip. Everyone sees everyone on one live map — each car’s position, heading and live speed — so nobody gets left behind. The group leader sets the route and every car gets turn-by-turn to follow it; you can chat, start a one-tap group audio call, drop shared hazard and police alerts, and race friendly checkpoints that feed a leaderboard. It’s built to make a real road trip feel like driving in a convoy in a racing game.

Convoy is free with ads. The optional Convoy Pro subscription removes all ads and unlocks larger convoys, automatic checkpoint generation, full leaderboard history, custom car skins, and a saved-route library.

Getting started

  • Start a convoy to become the leader, or Join a convoy with the 6-character code a friend shares (codes avoid look-alike characters so they’re easy to read aloud in the car). You can also tap a shared invite link to join.
  • Set your car. Pick your display name and car color so the others can spot you on the map at a glance.
  • Grant location “While Using” when asked — Convoy needs it to place you on the shared map and show your live speed. While a convoy is active, location continues in the background (with the iOS blue indicator) so the group still sees you when your screen is off.
  • Leader sets the route. Search a destination or drop waypoints; the route appears on every member’s map and each car gets guidance to follow it. Any member can also open the route in Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze for full voice navigation.
  • Checkpoints & leaderboard. The leader can drop checkpoints or auto-generate a set along the route; as members reach each one, the leaderboard updates with arrival order and times.

Staying in touch while driving

Convoy is designed to keep your eyes on the road. Send quick preset messages (“Pit stop”, “Slow down”, “Pulling over”) with one tap, or start a group audio call for the convoy using Apple’s Group FaceTime Audio. Tap a hazard button to drop a shared Police / Accident / Hazard / Slow / Camera alert that appears on everyone’s map with a spoken heads-up as they approach.

FAQ

Who can see my location?

Only the members of a convoy you are currently in, and only while you are in it. Your live position, heading and speed are shared with your convoy through our backend so they appear on the shared map. When you leave the convoy (or it ends), that live data stops and is cleared. Convoy is not a background “always tracking” app — nothing is shared when you’re not in an active convoy. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is shared and how.

Does the group call cost anything?

No. The group call uses Apple’s Group FaceTime Audio, which is free over your internet connection. Everyone in the call needs an Apple device.

Do the hazard/police alerts come from Waze?

No. Convoy’s alerts are reported by the members of your own convoy and shared only within it. You can optionally open the Waze app to report to Waze’s wider community, but Convoy does not read from or write to Waze.

Will it drain my battery?

While a convoy is active, Convoy uses GPS continuously to keep your position live for the group, which is energy-intensive — keep your phone on a car charger for long drives. When you’re not in a convoy, Convoy does nothing in the background.

Why am I seeing ads, and what is the tracking prompt?

On the free plan Convoy shows a small banner ad through Google AdMob that never covers the map controls. On first launch iOS asks whether Convoy may track you: choosing Allow permits personalized ads, while Ask App Not to Track still shows ads but non-personalized ones. Subscribe to Convoy Pro to remove ads entirely. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is my data backed up?

Your own trip history, saved routes, car profile and settings are stored in your personal iCloud account (if you’re signed in to iCloud), so they follow you across your devices. The live convoy data itself is temporary and is not part of that backup.
