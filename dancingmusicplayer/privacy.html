Dancing Music Player Privacy Policy

Effective 31 May 2026 · Last updated 31 May 2026

Summary: Dancing Music Player does not collect personal data, contains no advertising, and uses no analytics or third-party SDKs. The audio files you open are read on your device for playback and never uploaded or shared with anyone.

1. Information we collect

None. Dancing Music Player does not ask for, collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information. The app has no account system, no login, and no user profile.

2. Audio files you open

When you tap "Open a song" the app uses Apple's standard Files picker. The file you pick is read on your device for playback — it is never uploaded to any server, transmitted off the device, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app keeps a temporary working copy in its own sandbox while it is playing so that playback is reliable; this copy is deleted automatically.

3. On-device processing

All audio processing — decoding, time-stretching, pitch-shifting, and the speed knob — happens locally on your device using Apple's audio frameworks. No part of your audio is sent off the device for processing.

4. Network activity

The app makes no network requests of any kind. It does not contact servers operated by MW Supply Limited or by any third party.

5. Third-party services

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics frameworks, no advertising networks, and no crash reporting services other than Apple's own first-party crash reporting (which only runs if you have opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

6. Permissions

The app does not request access to your microphone, contacts, photos, location, or any other protected resource. The Files picker shown when you import a song is run by iOS itself, not by this app, and only returns the single file you choose.

7. Children

Dancing Music Player collects no data of any kind, so it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how Dancing Music Player handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy nutrition labels for the affected version of the app.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
