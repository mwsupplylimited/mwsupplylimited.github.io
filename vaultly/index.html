Vaultly

A private, encrypted album — separate from Apple Photos. Version 1.0

About

Vaultly is a private photo and video vault for iPhone and iPad. Everything you add is encrypted on your device with AES-256 and kept completely separate from Apple Photos, so your personal media isn't sitting in the one album a thief goes straight for. The whole app is locked behind Face ID, and with Vaultly Pro your vault syncs privately across your own devices through iCloud — where even Apple only ever stores ciphertext.

Vaultly protects your media two ways: encryption keeps the files unreadable on disk and off your device, and Face ID keeps the app itself locked. It is not a cloud service you upload to us — there is no account, and we never see your photos or videos.

Getting started

  • Open Vaultly and unlock with Face ID (or your device passcode).
  • Tap + and choose From Photos or From Files to bring media in. Each item is encrypted as it's imported.
  • Tap any item to view or play it — pinch to zoom photos, and edit with filters, crop, rotate, or trim.
  • Use Share or Save to Photos to export a copy, or delete items you no longer want.

FAQ

How is my media protected?

Every photo and video is encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM before it is written to storage, with a key held in the iOS Keychain. The files are unreadable without your key, and Vaultly keeps them out of unencrypted device backups. On top of that, the app is gated by Face ID and hides your content in the app switcher.

Is it really separate from Apple Photos?

Yes. Imported media lives only inside Vaultly's encrypted vault and never appears in the Photos app unless you choose to export a copy back to Photos. If your phone is lost or stolen, your Vaultly media isn't in the place most people look first.

What does Face ID protect versus encryption?

Face ID locks the app so someone with your unlocked phone still can't open your vault. Encryption protects the files themselves — on disk and when synced — so they're unreadable even if someone bypasses the app. Vaultly is honest about this: we don't claim to be “unhackable,” we give you a strong second lock and real at-rest encryption.

How does iCloud sync work?

With Pro, Vaultly syncs your vault across your own devices through your private iCloud. Media is encrypted before it leaves your device, and the encryption key rides Apple's end-to-end-encrypted iCloud Keychain, so even Apple only stores ciphertext. You can turn sync off any time in the app's Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. Vaultly has no account and no sign-up. Your media stays on your device (and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud).
