SciCalc Privacy Policy

Effective 28 May 2026 · Last updated 28 May 2026

Summary: SciCalc collects no personal data, makes no network requests, contains no advertising, and uses no analytics or third-party SDKs. Everything you type stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

None. SciCalc does not ask for, collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information. The app has no account system, no login, no user profile.

2. Information stored locally on your device

SciCalc stores the following data on your device only, using iOS's standard UserDefaults mechanism, so that the app remembers it between launches:

  • Your calculation history (the scrolling tape, capped at the most recent 100 entries).
  • The current memory register value (M+/M−/MR).
  • Your preferred angle mode (degrees or radians).

This data never leaves your device. It is automatically removed if you delete the app, and you can clear it at any time using the Clear History button in the History sheet or the MC button on the keypad.

3. Network activity

SciCalc makes no network requests of any kind. The app does not contact any server owned by MW Supply Limited or any third party.

4. Third-party services

SciCalc contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics frameworks, no advertising networks, and no crash reporting services other than Apple's own first-party crash reporting (which is governed entirely by Apple's privacy policy and only runs if you have opted in to Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

5. Children

SciCalc is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+ on the App Store. Because the app collects no data of any kind, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

6. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how SciCalc handles data (for example, adding an optional iCloud sync feature in a future version), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy nutrition labels for the affected version of the app.
