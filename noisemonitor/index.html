Choose the storage approach you need.

Continuous full recordings stay until you delete their originals and do not use the rolling-history cap. For a chosen temporary budget, use Events only with optional rolling history. After stopping, review retained history and save a missed moment as an excerpt of up to 10 minutes.

History is off by default. Old temporary sections expire as the cap fills; saved events and their matching video remain separate. Continuous audio uses about 350 MB/hour at 48 kHz, plus optional video. Check power and free storage. Missing sound or footage cannot be recovered. See retention, storage and selection details.

See what sensitivity includes.

Open a saved clip to view Sound level (RMS) and the regions your settings would include. Drag the white playback cursor and listen with Play/Pause directly below the chart. Brief peaks are optional. Adjust sensitivity for your next session while keeping the original audio unchanged.

This on-device preview is an estimate and stays available without Pro. Steady-background learning can let a newly started fan settle after about 15 seconds of stable sound, followed by the selected quiet countdown. It cannot identify the source; a steady unwanted hum can also become background. Continuous mode or optional rolling history in Events only retains all captured sound. How background learning works.

See the measurements behind the moment.

Open a saved sound’s Measured levels & evidence details to review RMS, sample peaks, estimated clip background and samples at the digital limit. Event exports include original-audio measurements, sample positions and checksums, even when you choose logs only.

These are unweighted digital dBFS calculations, not calibrated room dB(A). Background is estimated from this clip, not measured independently before the event. Decimal precision does not establish acoustic accuracy, origin, time or a legal breach. Understand the measurements and their limits.

Labels organize your journal and do not train AI. See browsing and Favorites.
