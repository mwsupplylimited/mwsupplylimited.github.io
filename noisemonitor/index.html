Match the sound with the moment.

Optionally record camera video alongside audio, then toggle matching video on in a saved sound to watch inline. A separate export screen creates a time-aligned movie. Your originals stay unchanged. Video runs continuously and uses about 0.9 GB/hour. On iPhone and iPad, keep the app visible: locking or leaving it stops video while audio continues.

Uncovered periods are black and contain no camera evidence. Audio evidence ZIPs exclude video; camera segments are managed separately on the device.

Go back for sounds detection missed.

Enable optional rolling history before Pro monitoring to keep all captured audio and optional video within a chosen temporary storage budget. After stopping, review RMS levels and a timeline showing automatic events, manual events and your selection. Save a missed moment as a lossless excerpt of up to 10 minutes.

History is off by default. Oldest temporary sections expire as the cap fills; saved events and their matching video are kept separately outside it. Audio or footage never captured cannot be recovered. See retention, storage and selection details.

See what sensitivity includes.

Open a saved clip to inspect RMS volume, brief impact peaks and the changing background threshold. Adjust sensitivity and preview the sections event detection would keep before your next session. The original audio stays unchanged.

Find the day. Keep track of the important sounds.

Browse saved recordings by month and day. Flag events for follow-up and filter by your own custom labels. Rename a category across your journal without changing the original sound.

Calendar, flags and custom labels stay free. Dates use your current local time zone; recordings spanning midnight appear on both days. Labels are your own annotations, not AI training or verified source findings. See calendar and label details.

Review a short excerpt, explore broad AI sound patterns and add your own labels for furniture scraping, hard impacts, repeated thuds and other sounds.
