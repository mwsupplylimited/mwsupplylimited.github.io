A useful recording starts with a short test.

Check what your microphone can hear before leaving Noise Monitor running. The free sound check and saved-recording playback, RMS tuning, sound learning, notes and exports are available without Pro.

Make your first recording

  1. Place your device in the room you want to record. Keep the microphone uncovered and away from fans, speakers and surfaces that rattle.
  2. Choose Try a 30-second sound check and allow microphone access.
  3. Make a small test sound, then stop or let the check finish. Wait for saving to complete.
  4. Open the saved recording, listen back and add a note or tag. Use Export recording to keep an original copy and its log.

Common questions

Can AI distinguish household sounds from likely external noise?

Open a saved recording and choose Identify sound & teach on-device AI. Select a 2–15 second excerpt containing one main sound, listen to it and choose Analyze selected sound. Apple's built-in model provides broad sound patterns. Your confirmed examples teach a separate local comparison for sound type, such as chair or furniture scraping, a dropped object or repeated thuds, and for likely source.

Source suggestions mean resemblance to examples you personally witnessed inside your home or examples you suspect came from outside. They cannot confirm a neighbour, a particular flat, a jump or an object's weight. Unfamiliar or conflicting sounds remain Uncertain. Analysis uses the unboosted original, happens on-device and does not change the audio. No recording or learning data is uploaded.

How do I label examples and improve suggestions?

After analysis, choose your own sound-type and source labels. Confirm that you reviewed the excerpt. For an inside-home label, confirm that you personally witnessed the sound; for an outside-home label, acknowledge that its exact source is unverified. Choose Uncertain when you do not know. Suggestions are never automatically adopted as labels.

Collect several independent recordings from the same room and keep microphone placement similar. Source suggestions require at least three distinct recordings in each source group. Sound-type suggestions require at least three distinct recordings for each of two or more types. Repeated excerpts or duplicate originals count once, and the current recording is held out of its own prediction. You therefore need additional recordings to test what the app has learned.

Use Review under Labels for this recording to correct an example. Manage or remove examples in the learning screen, or choose Clear all learning examples and confirm to erase all labels and features while keeping original recordings. Room changes or missing recordings make their old examples inactive. Labels remain local to each device without automatic sync; more examples do not guarantee better accuracy.

What does the local learning check measure?

Run local learning check compares each labelled original with the other examples while holding that original out. It reports label agreement, disagreements and uncertain results separately for sound type and source. These are checks against your own assessments, which may be mistaken, especially for suspected external sounds. They are not a calibrated probability, independent validation or a guarantee of future accuracy. Review disagreements and uncertain examples before collecting more.

How can I tune sensitivity using a recording?

Open a saved recording and choose Visualize RMS & tune sensitivity, or open Visualize RMS & fine-tune in capture settings. Select a reference clip, inspect its RMS (root mean square) level, peaks and adaptive background threshold, then adjust the rise above background. A lower rise is more sensitive. Shaded sections estimate the audio Events only mode would keep, including lead-in and tail.

Sharp impacts can trigger even below the RMS line; the preview shows actual detector decisions separately. Apply the value for your next event session. Continuous mode saves all audio regardless of sensitivity. This tool is free, works locally on original audio and never changes the recording. Earlier background history and microphone processing can change future results. Digital dBFS levels are not calibrated dB/dBA.

Why is my sound check silent?

Use the app directly on your unlocked iPhone or iPad for the initial test. End iPhone Mirroring or remote phone control first: Apple says iPhone Mirroring cannot access the iPhone microphone. Check microphone permission, make a small tapping sound and watch for the live input meter to move.

The app stops and reports an error if the input is system-muted or delivers three seconds of exact digital silence. A very quiet room is different from an unavailable microphone. For playback, check your media volume and the displayed output route, including connected headphones. Make another sound check before relying on extended monitoring.

Which recording mode should I use?

Events only saves context around detected changes or sounds you mark manually, with up to five seconds of buffered audio before a trigger and a quiet tail afterward. Longer activity is split into files. Detection can miss quiet thuds or sounds outside your microphone's range.

Continuous saves all microphone audio in approximately five-minute files. It is a useful starting point for a first overnight test or quiet, unpredictable sounds. Both extended monitoring modes require Pro access. The free sound check is a short continuous recording.

Can recording continue with the screen locked?

On iPhone and iPad, active recording is configured to continue when the screen locks. Calls, audio-input changes, power loss, force-quitting and system restrictions can stop it. Test a locked-screen recording on your own device, keep it powered and check the app's status when you return.

On Mac, keep the lid open. Noise Monitor requests prevention of idle system sleep while monitoring, but deliberate sleep and lid closure can stop capture. Closing the window leaves monitoring running; quitting stops and finishes recording.

Why is there no recording, or why did monitoring stop?

Confirm microphone access in your device's privacy settings, check the input and free storage, then make another sound check. Events mode saves only detected or manually marked sounds. Try Continuous mode when detection misses quiet sounds.

The app requires space to start and stops when storage becomes low. After an interruption, read the status message and explicitly start a new session. A readable unfinished file may be recovered after restart, but recovery is not guaranteed.

How do I export or back up recordings?

Open a saved recording and choose Export recording, or use Export in the library for saved recordings. The ZIP contains original WAV audio, an incident log, a monitoring-session log and a manifest with file checksums. AI suggestions, learning labels and sound features are excluded. Choose where to save or share it through the system controls. On Mac, the export sheet also offers Save ZIP.

Export and verify a separate copy before deleting originals or removing the app. The current iPhone/iPad library is excluded from automatic device backups. Previous backups, Mac backups and copies you export remain under your control. A prepared ZIP and your local library are separate copies. Recordings and learning data do not automatically sync between devices.

What is free, and what needs Pro?

The 30-second sound check is free. Playing saved recordings, RMS tuning, on-device sound learning, notes, tags and exports stay available without Pro, including after a subscription expires. Automatic event monitoring and continuous monitoring require an active monthly or yearly subscription, an eligible active trial, or the lifetime unlock.

Which plans have a trial?

Monthly has no introductory trial. A 3-day free trial may be offered with the yearly plan only when the App Store returns the configured offer and confirms that your account is eligible. Check the purchase screen and Apple confirmation for the exact offer, localized price and renewal terms. Lifetime is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

How do I restore, cancel or request a refund?

Open Plans and choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Connect to the internet if products or restore status do not load. One Pro purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac using the same Apple Account. Restore it on another supported device; recordings remain local without automatic sync.

Manage cancellation in your Apple Account subscriptions. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple advises canceling a trial at least 24 hours before it ends if you do not want it to renew. See Apple's cancellation instructions. To ask Apple about an eligible refund, follow Apple's refund instructions.

Are the levels a calibrated noise measurement?

No. The displayed levels are dBFS, relative to the device's digital input maximum. Microphone pickup, placement and sensitivity affect results. The app does not measure calibrated environmental dB/dBA, establish who caused a sound, or decide whether noise is unlawful. Notes and tags are your observations.

How do I delete a recording?

Open the recording and choose the delete control. This removes that local original, its notes and its associated learning examples. If learning cleanup fails, a warning explains that those examples are excluded from predictions; use Clear all learning examples to erase remaining labels. Copies in exports, cloud destinations or backups must be removed separately. You can keep reviewing and exporting existing recordings without a Pro subscription.
