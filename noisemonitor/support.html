Make a clear first recording.

Take 30 seconds to check what your microphone can hear, then listen back before a longer session. The free sound check and saved-recording playback, RMS tuning, sound learning, notes and exports are available without Pro.

Make your first recording

  1. Place your device in the room you want to record. Keep the microphone uncovered and away from fans, speakers and surfaces that rattle.
  2. Open Monitor, choose Try a 30-second sound check and allow microphone access.
  3. Make a small test sound, then stop or let the check finish. Wait for saving to complete.
  4. Open the saved recording, listen back and add a note or tag. Use Export recording to keep an audio-only ZIP with an unchanged audio copy and its log.

Common questions

Where are Events, Full recordings and Favorites?

Use the five pages: Monitor, Events, Full recordings, Favorites and Calendar. Wide windows use a sidebar; compact layouts use tabs. The Events page contains included sounds, while Full recordings contains retained source sessions. You do not need to scroll past one library to reach the other.

In Events, Full recordings and Favorites, choose Year, then an available Month. Clear the date filter to show all dates. Items are grouped once by their start date using the Gregorian calendar and displayed time zone. A recording spanning New Year stays in its starting year. This changes browsing only, not the recording's timestamps.

A heart adds or removes a favorite. Existing flagged events are already favorites; previously flagged originals remain separately accessible as individual originals. A full-recording favorite is a separate choice. Favorites can show all items, events, or full recordings and retained originals. Removing a heart never deletes audio or changes event inclusion. Deselected favorites remain visible but stay out of included-event lists, Calendar and all-events export. Browsing and Favorites remain free after Pro ends.

How do I review a whole overnight recording and choose events?

Choose Continuous in Capture settings before monitoring. It retains all captured microphone audio and optional camera video in saved storage while also saving automatically detected and manually marked event excerpts. After stopping, open Full recordings. One timeline shows the retained session across its original sections. Playback advances across adjoining sections and stops at gaps; missing sound is not manufactured.

Each recording shows its date, Started at clock time and a duration in hours, minutes and seconds. End times include the date and recorded time zone, so overnight sessions are clear. Playback position is elapsed time out of the saved timeline; Recorded at is the original clock time. An active recording shows only finished sections saved so far. If an exact stop was not recorded, Saved through identifies the last saved audio without claiming that it is the actual stop time. A timeline containing gaps is labelled separately.

Separate lanes show coverage, automatic events, manual events, deselected events and your selection. Seek to a sound, set its start and end, then choose Save selected event & add labels. On iPhone/iPad, Done above the keyboard dismisses it and keeps your entered text; use the separate save action to save the event. A full-session selection may cross adjoining sections and exceed 10 minutes if it fits the WAV size limit and available storage. Gaps, incompatible sources and changed files are refused. Exact duplicate saved ranges are refused, including deselected events: include the existing event again instead.

Use Include event to deselect or include an event again without deleting its audio, notes or labels. Deselected events are omitted from the calendar and Export all events, and remain available from the session or Deselected events. Continuous sources are also omitted from all-events export; open an original to export it explicitly. Full-session review and selection remain free, including after Pro ends.

Build 19 and older Continuous recordings retain their existing event markers; the app does not automatically rerun detection over previous nights. Earlier source sections are grouped only where existing session metadata is unambiguous. You can manually select additional events from retained audio. Never-recorded intervals cannot be recovered, and an estimated session ending does not establish full coverage.

Can AI distinguish household sounds from likely external noise?

Open a saved recording and choose Identify & label a sound. The Identify & label a sound page has three steps: Listen to the sound, See a suggestion and Add your labels. Open Choose a different moment for a 2–15 second excerpt containing one main sound, listen, then tap Identify sound. Apple's built-in model provides broad sound patterns. Your confirmed examples teach a separate local comparison for sound type, such as chair or furniture scraping, a dropped object or repeated thuds, and for likely source.

Source suggestions mean resemblance to examples you personally witnessed inside your home or examples you suspect came from outside. They cannot confirm a neighbour, a particular flat, a jump or an object's weight. Unfamiliar or conflicting sounds remain Uncertain. Analysis uses the unboosted original, happens on-device and does not change the audio. No recording or learning data is uploaded.

How do I label examples and improve suggestions?

After analysis, choose your own sound-type and source labels. Confirm that you reviewed the excerpt. For an inside-home label, confirm that you personally witnessed the sound; for an outside-home label, acknowledge that its exact source is unverified. Choose Uncertain when you do not know. Confirm I listened and chose these labels myself, then tap Save my labels or Update my labels. Labels saved on this device confirms completion. If saving is unavailable, the page explains the remaining step; after changing the excerpt, identify the new excerpt first. Suggestions are never automatically adopted as labels.

Collect separate recordings from the same room and keep microphone placement similar. Source suggestions require at least three independent recording groups for each source label. Sound-type suggestions require three groups for each of two or more types. Overlapping selections, shared history audio, duplicate originals and linked event parts count together, even when saved as different files. The entire current group is held out of its own prediction. You therefore need additional separate recordings to test what the app has learned. Manual history excerpts can still be labelled, including steady background/fan noise.

Use Edit labels under Saved labels for this recording to correct an example, or Remove to remove it. Open Learning progress & more options for counts, checks and global examples. Clear all learning examples asks for confirmation before erasing labels and features while keeping original recordings. Room changes or missing recordings make their old examples inactive. Labels remain local to each device without automatic sync; more examples do not guarantee better accuracy.

What does the local learning check measure?

Under Learning progress & more options, Check my saved examples compares each labelled recording group with the other groups while holding all of its overlapping selections, copies and linked parts out. It reports label agreement, disagreements and uncertain results separately for sound type and source. These are checks against your own assessments, which may be mistaken, especially for suspected external sounds. They are not a calibrated probability, independent validation or a guarantee of future accuracy. Review disagreements and uncertain examples before collecting more.

How can I tune sensitivity using a recording?

Open a saved recording and choose Visualize RMS & tune sensitivity, or open Visualize RMS & fine-tune in capture settings. Select a reference clip. Sound level (RMS) shows average signal strength over a short moment; the orange dashed line is the detection level above the changing background. A lower rise above background is more sensitive. Shaded sections estimate included event audio, including lead-in and tail. Continuous full-source audio is retained separately from its automatic event markers.

Play/Pause and the playback-position slider are directly below the chart. The white line shows your playback position: drag anywhere on the chart to move it, then listen from there. Stop monitoring before listening or applying a new sensitivity. For longer recordings, use Zoom to 30 seconds to inspect a smaller section. Open Brief peaks & detailed levels and enable Show brief peaks if you want the extra line. A brief peak is the loudest instant, such as a tap or bang; showing it only changes the chart, not detection.

Sharp impacts can trigger even below the RMS line; the preview shows actual detector decisions separately. Apply the value for your next monitoring session. Continuous mode saves all audio regardless of sensitivity. This tool is free, works locally on original audio and never changes the recording. Earlier background history and microphone processing can change future results. Digital dBFS levels are not calibrated dB/dBA.

Why does a fan initially trigger an event, then become background?

A fan already running when monitoring starts can be learned as background. If a louder steady sound starts later, the app first saves the change. After about 15 seconds of stable RMS level and peak behavior without detected impacts, the background estimate starts adapting. When detections stop, your selected quiet countdown ends the event. Impacts or larger variations restart the stability check; repeated banging is not stopped just because an event has become long.

This is on-device signal analysis, not a fan or source identification. A similarly steady unwanted hum can become background, and an irregular fan can still trigger events. Use Continuous or enable rolling history in Events only before monitoring when you want to keep all captured sound. Higher sensitivity can retain quieter impacts but may also save more ordinary background peaks. Original audio is never filtered by background learning.

How do I highlight and label a sound inside one saved recording?

For selections spanning a full session, use Full recordings. The following is the separate single-recording tool. Stop monitoring, open the recording and choose Select & save an event at the top. Review the RMS envelope and event timeline. Pause or move the playhead, choose Set start here and Set end here, then refine the range with sliders or exact seconds. Choose Save selected event & add labels. The saved event appears below with favorite and category controls; Open saved event opens its playback, notes and export tools.

Select a shorter section than the whole source, up to 10 minutes. For the whole recording, use Labels & favorites instead. Exact duplicate time ranges in the same room are refused. Automatic and Manual timeline lanes show other saved events at that time, not proof of the sound’s cause. This tool is free and does not require rolling history.

The app copies complete audio samples without re-encoding and records the source ID, checksum and sample range. The source recording stays unchanged. This is a derived excerpt, not a new capture. Labels are local organizational annotations, separate from AI training. Matching video relies on separately managed camera files that remain available; selecting audio does not create new camera footage.

A recovered source may be incomplete. Its recovery information is retained; inherited recovery details are identified as inherited. If an excerpt combines multiple source recordings and any has recovery details, choose an individual source before highlighting again. Exporting only the selected event does not automatically include its source recording or interrupted original. Export those separately when you need the fuller context.

How do I move between linked event parts?

Open a saved part of a long event. Where its group and monitoring session can be matched safely, use Previous saved part or Next saved part. Missing numbered parts, timing gaps, overlaps and estimated session endings are disclosed. Each file keeps its own audio, timestamps, notes and labels; navigation does not merge files or prove continuous coverage. If links cannot be resolved safely, open the files separately from saved recordings. Unsaved tag or note edits prompt Save, Discard or Keep editing before changing parts.

How do Calendar, Favorites and my own labels work?

Open Calendar, move between months, choose a day and open a sound. Dates use the device’s current local time zone, shown below the calendar. An event crossing midnight appears on each day it overlaps. Counts include only included events, following Favorites only and Custom label filters. Clear filters shows all included events again. Continuous originals and deselected events remain available elsewhere. Browsing does not change capture timestamps or their recorded time-zone information.

In a saved sound, use Labels & favorites to add a heart, create a category or assign existing categories. Manage all custom labels lets you rename or delete them. A rename updates assignments; deleting a category removes only that label and preserves recordings and favorites. Custom labels can contain up to 60 characters; the catalog holds up to 256 labels. Names must be distinct and cannot contain line breaks or control characters.

Favorites and custom labels are local organization, separate from AI examples, and do not establish who made a sound. Event favorites retain the existing flag field in exports; full-recording favorites are not exported. Metadata for missing recordings is retained so restored recordings can recover their organization. If either organization file cannot be read safely, the app preserves it and offers Retry; editing and organization export remain blocked until it loads safely. Calendar, Favorites and labels stay free and do not sync between devices.

Can I record and export video alongside sound?

Enable Record camera video alongside audio, then use the free Try a 30-second video sound check or start Pro monitoring. Allow Camera and Microphone. Camera video records continuously, using approximately 0.9 GB/hour in addition to audio. Continuous keeps captured video in saved storage regardless of the temporary-history preference. In Events only without history, microphone sound is saved only around events, so other footage may have no matching audio. In Events only with history, captured audio/video enter capped temporary history while saved events keep separate matching video copies.

The screen stays awake during video capture. Keep the app visible on iPhone and iPad: locking or leaving stops video without auto-resume while audio may continue. Dark screen is an app cover, not a device lock. Keep Mac awake with its lid open. After stopping, review saved sound and matching video inline or use the separate sound-and-video export screen. A viewing movie combines original audio with overlapping camera frames; gaps are black and contain no camera evidence. Export creates a compressed viewing copy and preserves originals. Export all events can include matching videos with sound for included events, as files in a chosen folder or in one ZIP. Use Manage saved camera video to remove video separately, since segments may overlap several sounds.

Can I show or hide video automatically when reviewing?

Show video by default, under Events → Review options, is on by default. It controls only reviewing existing matching footage. Turn it off for audio-first review, or use Show matching video on one recording. It does not turn on the camera or autoplay a recording. Audio-only sections remain playable when footage is absent. Camera recording remains a separate opt-in on the monitoring screen.

Why is my sound check silent?

Use the app directly on your unlocked iPhone or iPad for the initial test. Use the explicit Stop control to end screen sharing or iPhone Mirroring first; closing the window may leave it active: Apple says iPhone Mirroring cannot access the iPhone microphone. Check microphone permission, make a small tapping sound and watch for the live input meter to move.

The app stops and reports an error if the input is system-muted or delivers three seconds of exact digital silence. A very quiet room is different from an unavailable microphone. For playback, check your media volume and the displayed output route, including connected headphones. Make another sound check before relying on extended monitoring.

Which recording mode should I use?

Continuous keeps the full captured session plus detected and manually marked event excerpts in saved storage. Original storage sections appear together in Full recordings. The temporary-history budget does not cap or expire these recordings; they remain until you delete their originals. At 48 kHz, audio uses about 350 MB/hour, plus about 0.9 GB/hour for optional video. Check power and free storage. This is not a promise of unlimited storage or uninterrupted capture.

Events only saves detected or manually marked events. Add optional rolling history if you want to revisit other captured sound within a chosen temporary storage budget. Both extended modes require Pro; the free sound check records for up to 30 seconds.

In either Pro mode, choose 0–30 seconds before the first detected sound and a quiet countdown of 1–120 seconds after the latest detection or manual mark (defaults: 5 and 8 seconds). A new detection before expiry restarts the full countdown, so repeated banging can stay in one event. Below-threshold sounds do not extend it. Long events continue in linked parts of about 60 seconds, without a 60-second total-event limit. A sound after the prior countdown completes begins another event; chosen lead-in may overlap previous audio. Lead-in only includes audio captured since monitoring began. Marking does not stop the full continuous source. Stopping, interruptions or low storage can end capture early.

Can I find a sound that event detection missed?

For a full saved session, use Continuous and Full recordings. For temporary retention within a chosen budget, choose Events only and enable Keep rolling audio & video history before Pro monitoring. History is off by default and applies only to Events only; it does not cap or expire Continuous recordings. All captured microphone audio and optional camera video are kept in temporary sections of about 30 seconds. Choose a storage limit from 256 MB to 20 GB (default 1 GB). The limit counts temporary history, with 64 MB reserved inside it for active capture. Oldest finalized sections expire as it fills; saved events and their matching video are kept separately outside this cap. Protected, unfinished or unknown files are preserved, and monitoring stops if the reserve cannot be maintained safely. Changing the limit applies when the next monitoring session starts; turning history off keeps existing history.

Stop monitoring and choose Review rolling history & save missed events. Review audio, RMS levels and optional inline video. Separate timeline lanes show retained coverage, automatic events, manual events, continuous recordings and your selection. Save up to 10 minutes across complete sections with no gaps. The app creates a lossless manual excerpt with source hashes and sample ranges and preserves matching camera originals separately. Temporary source history may later expire. Existing events are marked so you can see what is already saved; an exact duplicate range is rejected. Sound or footage never captured cannot be recreated.

Why does monitoring no longer beep when an event is saved?

A speaker beep or vibration can enter the microphone, create another event and repeat. Monitoring therefore stays silent, including if an older version had saved-event sound enabled. The saved-event indicator and optional color pulse still confirm that event audio and its log were saved. There is no feedback-related pause in automatic detection, and original audio is not filtered or changed. Save confirmation does not mean that a camera segment still being recorded has finished.

Can I keep the monitoring screen dark and see when an event is saved?

Choose Dark screen during monitoring for a black background, dim recording status and an accessible Exit dark screen control. Entering or exiting it leaves capture running. On iPhone and iPad the app keeps the display awake while this view is active; manually locking the device still stops optional camera video.

Enable optional saved-event visual feedback and choose mint, amber, red, blue or white, with adjustable relative brightness. Device brightness itself is unchanged. Newly saved events can produce a single gentle fade, at most once every three seconds. Hidden, disabled or earlier saves are not replayed. The color pulse is off by default. Monitoring does not beep or vibrate.

Can recording continue with the screen locked?

On iPhone and iPad, active audio recording is configured to continue when the screen locks. Optional camera video stops when the screen locks or the app leaves the foreground. Calls, audio-input changes, power loss, force-quitting and system restrictions can stop it. Test a locked-screen recording on your own device, keep it powered and check the app's status when you return.

On Mac, keep the lid open. Noise Monitor requests prevention of idle system sleep while monitoring, but deliberate sleep and lid closure can stop capture. Closing the window leaves monitoring running; quitting stops and finishes recording.

Why is there no recording, or why did monitoring stop?

Confirm microphone access in your device's privacy settings, check the input and free storage, then make another sound check. Without rolling history, Events mode saves only detected or manually marked sounds. Enable rolling history before a session to review captured sounds missed by detection later, or use ordinary Continuous mode to retain all audio without automatic history expiration.

The app requires space to start and stops when storage becomes low. After an interruption, read the status message and explicitly start a new session. A readable unfinished file may be recovered after restart, but recovery is not guaranteed.

How do I export or back up recordings?

Open a saved recording and choose Export recording for its original audio and logs. Use Export all events in Events or Calendar for all included events, regardless of the current date, search, favorite or other filters. Continuous originals, deselected events and temporary history are omitted. Save missed selections first or explicitly export a source or deselected event from its detail view. Choose original WAV audio, matching videos with sound, both, or logs only, then leave Create a ZIP file on and tap Create ZIP, or turn it off and choose Choose folder & export…. Folder export verifies and skips existing matching media, preserves conflicting files, and adds a fresh dated report with current notes and labels. Keep report and shared media folders together. If cancelled or interrupted by an export error, completed files remain and can be reused on the next export. Conflicting files are preserved; choose another folder or move the conflicting item yourself. Choose a local folder to keep exports on-device; a cloud or shared folder may upload or sync through its provider without a separate Share action. Your choices are remembered locally; no export runs in the background and nothing is automatically sent. Videos are event-length compressed viewing copies with event audio, including when separate WAV files are off. Missing camera coverage is black, and events without video are listed in the media index. Logs, notes, flags, labels and checksums are always included. Keep monitoring stopped and the app open; progress and Cancel are available. Standard ZIP files must remain below 4 GB; for larger libraries, turn off ZIP and use folder export. Manually selected excerpts from saved recordings or rolling history are identified as derived copies with source hashes and sample ranges. Exporting only an excerpt does not automatically include its source recording or any interrupted original; temporary history sources may have expired. Linked event parts keep their group identifier and part number. Event favorites retain the existing flag field; custom-label names, stable label IDs, source role, monitoring-session ID and event inclusion decisions are included as your organizational annotations. Full-recording favorites are not exported. Neither a label, selection nor a checksum establishes who caused the sound. AI suggestions, AI learning labels and sound features are excluded. Choose where to save or share it through the system controls. On Mac, the export sheet also offers Save file….

Export and verify a separate copy before deleting originals or removing the app. The current iPhone/iPad library is excluded from automatic device backups. Previous backups, Mac backups and copies you export remain under your control. A prepared ZIP and your local library are separate copies. Recordings and learning data do not automatically sync between devices.

What is free, and what needs Pro?

The 30-second sound check is free. Playing full sessions and saved recordings, selecting or deselecting events, highlighting a section, calendar, favorites, custom labels, RMS tuning, on-device sound learning, notes, tags and exports stay available without Pro, including after a subscription expires. Automatic event monitoring, continuous monitoring and optional rolling history capture require an active monthly or yearly subscription, an eligible active trial, or the lifetime unlock.

Open Plans and choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Connect to the internet if products or restore status do not load. Are the levels a calibrated noise measurement?

No. The displayed levels are dBFS, relative to the device's digital input maximum. Microphone pickup, placement and sensitivity affect results. The app does not measure calibrated environmental dB/dBA, establish who caused a sound, or decide whether noise is unlawful. Notes and tags are your observations.

How can I free storage without deleting saved events?

Stop monitoring and wait for recording to finish. Open Review rolling history & save missed events, then choose Clear temporary history and confirm. First save any missed sounds you want to keep. The app verifies the temporary files and keeps saved events and their matching video separately. Notes, favorites and custom labels remain unchanged. Unrecognized, unfinished or damaged files are preserved with an explanation. If clearing stops partway through, the app reports it; some temporary files may remain. This action is available without Pro. Turning history off alone keeps its existing recordings.

How do I keep my notes and sound tags?

Open a saved sound, edit its notes or sound tag, and choose Save tag & notes. Unsaved changes are marked on screen. Use Close to save, discard or keep editing; a failed save keeps the draft available for retry. Flags and custom labels save immediately and are separate from AI learning examples.

Why do Plans show a pending approval or changed offer?

Apple may require approval for a purchase. A pending request does not unlock Pro, and the app avoids starting another plan while this device’s pending reminder is present. Free features and Restore purchases remain available. Check the requesting Apple Account and approver before clearing a pending reminder; clearing it does not cancel Apple's request, and a later approval can still charge you.

Plans refresh prices and yearly-trial eligibility when you return to the app and before a purchase. If the offer changed, review the updated plan and tap again. No payment starts automatically when an offer changes.

How do I delete a recording?

Open the recording and choose the delete control. This removes that saved recording, its notes and its associated learning examples. Separately saved excerpts remain until you delete them. If learning cleanup fails, a warning explains that those examples are excluded from predictions; use Clear all learning examples to erase remaining labels. Custom label categories, orphan event-favorite/label assignments and full-recording favorite IDs remain separately so restored recordings can recover their organization. Remove assignments before deleting a sound, or delete a custom label to remove it from every event. Copies in exports, cloud destinations or backups must be removed separately. You can keep reviewing and exporting existing recordings without a Pro subscription. Saved full recordings and events remain until deleted; deselecting an event does not delete it. Temporary rolling history in Events only can expire under its selected storage cap, which does not cap Continuous recordings. Save any history excerpt you want to keep before it expires.
