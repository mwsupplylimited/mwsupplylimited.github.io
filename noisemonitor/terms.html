Prices, renewal and offers

US reference prices are USD 1.99 monthly, USD 19.99 yearly and USD 39.99 lifetime. Apple presents the actual price for your storefront and currency. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled through your Apple Account. Any yearly trial is available only when configured and offered by the App Store and your account is eligible. After an applicable trial, the subscription renews at the price shown before purchase unless you cancel.

If you do not want a trial to renew, Apple advises canceling at least 24 hours before it ends. Follow Apple's instructions to manage or cancel a subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Refund requests are handled through Apple's refund process.

One purchase across your supported devices

One Pro purchase covers Noise Monitor on iPhone, iPad and Mac using the same Apple Account. Use Restore Purchases on another supported device with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Audio libraries remain local and do not automatically synchronize.

Use the recorder responsibly

Record only where you are entitled to do so and consider other people's privacy. The app can miss sounds or be interrupted. Digital input levels are not calibrated environmental measurements, and recordings, labels and checksums do not establish a sound's source, intent or legal status.
