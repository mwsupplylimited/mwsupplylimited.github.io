Privacy & your data

Your recordings stay under your control.

MW Supply Limited · Updated 21 October 2026

This policy covers the Noise Monitor apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac and these support pages. For privacy questions, email mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.

Microphone and recording

Noise Monitor uses the microphone after you choose to start recording and grant microphone access. Events only mode continuously reads microphone audio into a short rolling memory buffer and saves clips when a sound is detected or marked. Continuous mode saves all microphone audio in segments. The free sound check records for up to 30 seconds. Stop monitoring ends microphone capture.

Calls, input changes, sleep, power loss and system restrictions can interrupt capture. Sound suggestions do not verify a particular neighbour, speaker, action, physical source location, object's weight or intent.

What is stored on your device

The app saves audio files, device-clock dates and times, time-zone information, room labels, notes, tags, relative digital input levels and monitoring-session records in local app storage. Optional sound learning also stores the labels you confirm, their selected time ranges and compact sound features, linked to the recording and its checksum. It does not automatically synchronize recordings, labels or learning between devices.

On-device sound analysis and learning

When you choose to analyze a saved recording, Apple's built-in sound model processes a selected 2–15 second excerpt locally. The app compares its broad sound patterns and acoustic features with examples you have personally labelled in the same room. Audio, labels and features are not uploaded to Apple for sound analysis or to a developer server. No speech transcription, speaker identification or cloud training is used.

Sound type and likely source are separate suggestions. Source comparison uses examples you say you witnessed inside your home and examples you suspect came from outside. External labels are your assessments, not verified origins. Predictions are not automatically saved as labels, and uncertain or conflicting matches remain uncertain. Suggestions need at least three independent recordings for each of two labels; source suggestions need both source groups. The recording being analyzed and duplicate copies of its original audio are held out.

You can correct or remove examples, clear all learning data, and run a local check that holds out each original recording in turn. Its results measure agreement with your own labels and abstentions, not independently measured accuracy or calibrated probabilities. Learning does not change original audio, timestamps, notes or tags. More labels do not guarantee improved accuracy.

Backups

The current iPhone/iPad app marks its local recording library, including learning data, as excluded from automatic device backups. This does not remove copies in previous backups or destinations you chose for exports. Mac backups and other copies you manage remain under your control; check those settings and remove unwanted copies separately. There is no automatic cross-device sync or developer recovery service.

No developer recording or usage collection

The app has no account system, advertising, tracking, analytics or developer-operated data server. MW Supply Limited does not receive your recordings, notes or in-app usage data through the app.

Apple purchases

Apple processes payments and Apple Account information under Apple's privacy policy. The app checks verified transaction information on your device to determine Pro access. It does not send purchase history to a developer-operated server. One Pro purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac using the same Apple Account. StoreKit may contact Apple for products, purchase verification and restoration; this does not synchronize your audio library. Subscription prices, renewal terms and any eligible yearly introductory offer are shown before purchase.

Exports and sharing

Export creates a separate copy of selected original audio and its log. AI suggestions, learning labels and sound features are excluded from evidence exports. Files leave the app when you choose a destination through system save or share controls. The destination's privacy practices apply. Review recordings before sharing because they may contain conversations and other private sounds. File checksums help detect changes; they do not independently verify the recording's source or time.

Support email

If you email support, MW Supply Limited and the email providers receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, device details and description of the issue. We use it to answer and investigate your request. Private audio is not required for support. Include it only if necessary and you choose to share it.

Retention and deletion

Recordings remain in the library until you delete them or remove the app and its stored data. Deleting a recording in its detail screen removes that local original and its notes and purges associated learning examples. If learning cleanup fails, the app warns you and excludes those examples from predictions; use Clear all learning examples to erase the remaining labels. You can also remove individual examples or clear all learning data while keeping your recordings. An explicit clear can reset an unreadable learning file; otherwise it is preserved rather than silently overwritten.

Separate exports and backups must be removed separately. Unfinished files may be retained for recovery after an interruption; prepared temporary exports may be cleaned up by the app or operating system.

We keep support correspondence while needed to respond and resolve the issue and as necessary for legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you provided to support, subject to applicable requirements. We cannot retrieve or erase recordings stored only on your device or copies you shared with others.
