Privacy & your data

Your recordings stay under your control.

MW Supply Limited · Updated 24 October 2026

This policy covers the Noise Monitor apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac and these support pages. For privacy questions, email mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.

Microphone and recording

Noise Monitor uses the microphone after you choose to start recording and grant access. Continuous stores full captured source audio and separate detected or manually marked event excerpts. The logical session groups original sections locally without concatenating or replacing them. In Events only without rolling history, microphone audio enters a short memory buffer and clips are saved when a sound is detected or marked. Optional rolling history applies only to Events only: it retains captured audio in a separate temporary library, while saved events remain separate. The free sound check records for up to 30 seconds. Stop monitoring ends microphone capture.

Calls, input changes, sleep, power loss and system restrictions can interrupt capture. Sound suggestions do not verify a particular neighbour, speaker, action, physical source location, object’s weight or intent.

Optional camera video

If you enable camera video and grant camera access, the app saves video continuously alongside audio, including in Events only mode. Video stays in local app storage. Continuous keeps it in saved storage; Events only with rolling history keeps it in temporary history with separate matching copies for saved events. The screen stays awake during video capture. On iPhone and iPad, locking or leaving the app stops video while audio monitoring continues; video does not restart automatically. Camera video uses approximately 0.9 GB per hour in addition to audio. Show video by default is a local playback preference, initially on where matching footage exists. It neither grants camera access nor starts recording or autoplay. Camera capture remains a separate opt-in.

Optional rolling history and event feedback

Rolling history is off by default and applies only to Events only mode. If enabled, captured audio and optional camera video are stored on this device in temporary sections. The history budget does not cap or expire Continuous full recordings. You choose a history budget from 256 MB to 20 GB; 64 MB inside it is reserved for active files. Oldest finalized sections expire as the limit fills. Saved events and their separate matching video copies are outside this cap. Monitoring stops if active, protected or unknown files prevent safe retention within the reserve. A new cap applies when the next monitoring session starts; disabling history keeps existing history.

You can review finalized history after stopping, see automatic and manual saved-event markers and save a missed event of up to 10 minutes. The saved excerpt copies PCM samples without re-encoding and records source IDs, hashes and sample ranges. Matching video originals are copied separately without changing their bytes. The temporary source history may later expire. These copies and references do not establish a sound's cause, exact source or legal significance.

Monitoring stays silent: no saved-event beep or vibration is emitted, including when an earlier version saved a sound-on preference. The app does not pause automatic detection for feedback or filter the original audio to remove notification sounds. Visual save confirmation remains on-device; no feedback or recording information is sent to the developer.

Optional Dark screen keeps monitoring visible with a black background and dim controls, and can keep an iPhone/iPad display awake while active. Optional local saved-event pulses use your chosen color and relative brightness, with gentle fades no more often than once every three seconds. They do not change device brightness or transmit save activity. Visual feedback choices stay on the device and are off by default.

What is stored on your device

The app saves audio files, optional camera video, device-clock dates and times, time-zone information, room labels, notes, tags, flags, custom-label categories and assignments, relative digital input levels, event inclusion decisions and monitoring-session records in local app storage. Optional sound learning also stores the labels you confirm, their selected time ranges and compact sound features, linked to the recording and its checksum. It does not automatically synchronize recordings, labels or learning between devices.

Selections from saved recordings

Full recordings presents validated source sections as a logical session. Full-session selections can cross adjoining originals, copying exact PCM samples and retaining each source ID, checksum and sample range. The operation and review metadata stay on-device. Source gaps are disclosed; earlier captures do not acquire invented automatic detections.

Select & save an event creates a separate audio excerpt from a saved recording on this device. It copies complete PCM samples without re-encoding, retains the source ID, checksum and sample range, and leaves the source file unchanged. The new event can have its own notes, flags and category labels. Matching camera files remain separately managed. Source validation and copying happen locally; no selected audio or labels are sent to the developer.

If a source was recovered after an interruption, the excerpt records that recovery context and may be incomplete. Recovery inherited from an earlier source is identified as inherited, not independently reverified for this selection. Exporting only an excerpt does not automatically include its source audio or interrupted original. Linked-part navigation uses saved group and session records locally; it does not establish uninterrupted capture or a sound’s physical origin.

Event inclusion, calendar, flags and custom labels

Event inclusion is a reversible local review choice. Deselecting retains audio, timestamps, notes and labels but removes an event from included-event lists, calendar and all-events export. You can include it again. Reports can record source role, monitoring-session identity and inclusion decisions as user metadata, not source attribution. Continuous source recordings stay available in Full recordings and are omitted from the included-event calendar.

Calendar dates are computed on the device in its current local time zone from saved recording timestamps. A sound overlapping more than one day can appear on each of those days. Flag and label filters use separate local organization metadata; they do not change original capture times or audio. Custom label names and stable IDs are stored on this device. They are your annotations, not AI training examples or verified source findings, and they remain available without Pro.

Renaming a category updates its assignments. Deleting a category unassigns it without deleting recordings or flags. Metadata for a missing recording is retained so it can be used if that recording is restored. Unreadable metadata is preserved and editing/export is blocked until it can be loaded safely. There is no automatic label or calendar sync.

Steady background adaptation

Automatic detection evaluates unboosted levels and peaks locally. A newly louder stable sound is initially captured; after about 15 seconds of stability without detected impacts, it can start becoming background and the event ends when the selected quiet countdown expires. This does not identify a fan or establish a sound’s source. A steady unwanted hum can also become background; Continuous mode or optional rolling history in Events only retains all captured audio independently of detection. Adaptation does not filter or change saved audio.

On-device sound analysis and learning

When you choose to analyze a saved recording, Apple's built-in sound model processes a selected 2–15 second excerpt locally. The app compares its broad sound patterns and acoustic features with examples you have personally labelled in the same room. Audio, labels and features are not uploaded to Apple for sound analysis or to a developer server. No speech transcription, speaker identification or cloud training is used.

Sound type and likely source are separate suggestions. Source comparison uses examples you say you witnessed inside your home and examples you suspect came from outside. External labels are your assessments, not verified origins. Predictions are not automatically saved as labels, and uncertain or conflicting matches remain uncertain. Suggestions need at least three independent recording groups for each of two labels; source suggestions need both source labels. Overlapping selections, shared history audio, duplicate originals and linked event parts count together. The whole group containing the recording being analyzed is held out. This grouping is derived locally from current recording metadata and never uploaded.

You can correct or remove examples, clear all learning data, and run a local check that holds out each whole recording group in turn. Its results measure agreement with your own labels and abstentions, not independently measured accuracy or calibrated probabilities. Learning does not change original audio, timestamps, notes or tags. More labels do not guarantee improved accuracy.

Backups

The current iPhone/iPad app marks its local recording library, including learning data, flags, custom labels and temporary rolling history, as excluded from automatic device backups. This does not remove copies in previous backups or destinations you chose for exports. Mac backups and other copies you manage remain under your control; check those settings and remove unwanted copies separately. There is no automatic cross-device sync or developer recovery service.

No developer recording or usage collection

The app has no account system, advertising, tracking, analytics or developer-operated data server. MW Supply Limited does not receive your recordings, notes or in-app usage data through the app.

Optional rating prompts

The app keeps a small, local count of completed playback visits and up to three distinct days of use to choose a natural pause for Apple’s optional rating prompt. It also remembers when a prompt was attempted and which app versions have already requested one. These settings stay on the device; they are not developer analytics and are not sent to the developer. Eligibility does not depend on payment, sound labels or feedback. Apple decides whether to display its prompt and handles ratings under its own policies.

Apple purchases

Apple processes payments and Apple Account information under Apple's privacy policy. The app checks verified transaction information on your device to determine Pro access. It does not send purchase history to a developer-operated server. One Pro purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac using the same Apple Account. StoreKit may contact Apple for products, purchase verification and restoration; this does not synchronize your audio library. Subscription prices, renewal terms and any eligible yearly introductory offer are shown before purchase.

If Apple reports a purchase awaiting approval, Noise Monitor stores a local reminder containing the product, request time and a random request identifier. It does not store the purchasing Apple Account identity and does not grant Pro access. The reminder helps avoid duplicate purchase requests; it can be cleared explicitly after checking the original request. Clearing a local reminder does not cancel Apple’s request. No reminder or purchase information is sent to the developer.

Exports and sharing

Export recording creates an audio-only ZIP containing the selected saved WAV audio and its logs; it does not include camera video. Export all events includes included events across the library, regardless of the current date, search or filters. It omits continuous source recordings, deselected events and temporary history. Explicitly export a source or deselected event from its detail view when you want that recording. You choose original WAV audio, matching videos with sound, both, or logs only, and whether to create one ZIP or save files in a folder you select. Dates, notes, flags, custom-label names, stable label IDs, source role, monitoring-session identity and event inclusion decisions are included as your organizational annotations. AI suggestions, AI learning labels and sound features are excluded. These choices are remembered on this device; they do not schedule future exports.

Folder export skips existing media only after checking that it matches the current originals and its complete file contents verify. Conflicting or incomplete files are preserved and export stops; they are not overwritten. Each successful export adds a fresh dated report with current notes and labels. Completed media can remain after cancellation or failure and can be reused by exporting to the same folder again. Keep the report and shared media folders together. Exported copies remain outside the app's library and must be deleted separately.

Matching videos are compressed, event-length viewing copies with event sound, even when separate WAV audio is not selected. The separate sound-and-video export also creates a compressed movie aligned by recorded times. Originals remain unchanged. Uncovered portions are black and contain no camera evidence; events without matching footage are listed in the all-events media index. Manually selected excerpts from saved recordings or history are marked as derived copies with source checksums and sample ranges. Exporting only an excerpt does not automatically include its source audio or an interrupted original; temporary source history may have expired.

Files leave the app when you choose a destination through system save or share controls. Choose a local folder to keep exported copies on-device. A cloud or shared folder may upload or synchronize them through its provider, without a separate Share action. That destination's privacy practices and settings apply. The app does not send recordings to the developer or automatically export new events. Review recordings before exporting because they may contain conversations, images and other private activity. File checksums help detect changes; they do not independently verify a recording's source or time.

Support email

If you email support, MW Supply Limited and the email providers receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, device details and description of the issue. We use it to answer and investigate your request. Private audio is not required for support. Include it only if necessary and you choose to share it.

Retention and deletion

Saved full recordings, event excerpts and separately retained camera files remain until you delete them or remove the app and its stored data. Deselecting an event does not delete its audio, timestamps, notes or labels. Temporary rolling history in Events only can expire automatically under its chosen budget, which does not expire Continuous full recordings; save a desired excerpt as an event before it expires. You can also choose Clear temporary history after monitoring stops. This removes known temporary audio and video while keeping saved events and their matching video, notes and labels. Unrecognized, unfinished or damaged files are preserved with an explanation. Clearing can stop partway through if files change or a filesystem operation fails, leaving some temporary files in place. This action remains available without Pro. Deleting a recording in its detail screen removes that saved recording and its notes and purges associated learning examples. Separately saved excerpts remain until you delete them. If learning cleanup fails, the app warns you and excludes those examples from predictions; use Clear all learning examples to erase the remaining labels. You can also remove individual examples or clear all learning data while keeping your recordings. Organization categories and assignments for missing recordings are retained separately; remove assignments on an existing recording or delete a custom label to remove it from every event. An explicit clear can reset an unreadable learning file; otherwise it is preserved rather than silently overwritten.

Deleting an audio recording does not delete camera segments, which can overlap other recordings. Remove video separately using Manage saved camera video. Separate exports and backups must be removed separately. After an interrupted recording, the app may create a playable recovery copy while preserving the unfinished original and documenting both checksums in exports. Unfinished files may be retained for recovery after an interruption; prepared temporary exports may be cleaned up by the app or operating system.

We keep support correspondence while needed to respond and resolve the issue and as necessary for legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you provided to support, subject to applicable requirements. We cannot retrieve or erase recordings stored only on your device or copies you shared with others.
