Privacy & your data

Your recordings stay under your control.

MW Supply Limited · Updated 21 October 2026

This policy covers the Noise Monitor apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac and these support pages. For privacy questions, email mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.

Microphone and recording

Noise Monitor uses the microphone after you choose to start recording and grant microphone access. Events only mode continuously reads microphone audio into a short rolling memory buffer and saves clips when a sound is detected or marked. Continuous mode saves all microphone audio in segments. The free sound check records for up to 30 seconds. Stop monitoring ends microphone capture.

Calls, input changes, sleep, power loss and system restrictions can interrupt capture. The app does not identify speakers, neighbours, a sound's source or intent.

What is stored on your device

The app saves audio files, device-clock dates and times, time-zone information, room labels, notes, tags, relative digital input levels and monitoring-session records in local app storage. It does not automatically synchronize recordings between devices. Device and computer backups may contain app data according to your system settings.

No developer recording or usage collection

The app has no account system, advertising, tracking, analytics or developer-operated data server. MW Supply Limited does not receive your recordings, notes or in-app usage data through the app.

Apple purchases

Apple processes payments and Apple Account information under Apple's privacy policy. The app checks verified transaction information on your device to determine Pro access. It does not send purchase history to a developer-operated server. StoreKit may contact Apple for products, purchase verification and restoration. Subscription prices, renewal terms and any eligible yearly introductory offer are shown before purchase.

Exports and sharing

Export creates a separate copy of selected original audio and its log. Files leave the app when you choose a destination through system save or share controls. The destination's privacy practices apply. Review recordings before sharing because they may contain conversations and other private sounds. File checksums help detect changes; they do not independently verify the recording's source or time.

Support email

If you email support, MW Supply Limited and the email providers receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, device details and description of the issue. We use it to answer and investigate your request. Private audio is not required for support. Include it only if necessary and you choose to share it.

Retention and deletion

Recordings remain in the library until you delete them or remove the app and its stored data. Deleting a recording in its detail screen removes that local original and its notes. Separate exports and backups must be removed separately. Unfinished files may be retained for recovery after an interruption; prepared temporary exports may be cleaned up by the app or operating system.

We keep support correspondence while needed to respond and resolve the issue and as necessary for legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you provided to support, subject to applicable requirements. We cannot retrieve or erase recordings stored only on your device or copies you shared with others.

These support pages

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, contact forms or advertising. They are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses and browser information, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following links to Apple or another provider is subject to that provider's practices.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The revision date appears above. Contact mwsupplylimited@gmail.com with questions about the app or this policy.
