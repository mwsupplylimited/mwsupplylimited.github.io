Wardo Support

Outfit Planner & Closet for iPhone and iPad

Wardo photographs the clothes you already own, cuts out the backgrounds on your device, and puts outfits together from them. Your clothes never leave your device.

Getting started

  • Add a few pieces. Tap + on the Closet tab and photograph or pick an item. Wardo removes the background and guesses the category, colour and formality — every guess is one tap to change.
  • Wardo needs about 5 items before it can put outfits together. Ten or so is where it starts feeling useful.
  • Check Today. The Today tab suggests outfits built only from things you own, and tells you why it picked them.
  • Tap “I’m wearing this.” That one tap records every piece as worn, which is what makes cost-per-wear and your never-worn list meaningful.

Common questions

The background wasn’t removed from my photo

Background removal runs on your device and needs a reasonably clear subject. It works best on a plain, contrasting surface with even light. If it does not find the garment, Wardo keeps your original photo and you can tap Remove background on the item to try again. The feature requires a physical iPhone or iPad — it does not run in the iOS Simulator.

Can I correct the category or colour?

Yes, always. Every attribute Wardo assigns automatically — category, colour, pattern, formality — is one tap to override on the item’s detail screen. Nothing is locked.

Does Wardo sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes, if you are signed in to iCloud, using your own private iCloud database. Sync is automatic. If you are signed out, Wardo works fully and keeps everything on that device.

Why don’t my items appear on my other device?

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is on. The first sync of a large closet can take a few minutes on Wi-Fi. Photos are the largest part, so they arrive after the item names do.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap +, search for Wardo, and choose a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise, then add a widget. The widget shows today’s outfit and lets you log it as worn with one tap. It refreshes when you open the app, and overnight when iOS allows.

The widget says “Open Wardo for today”

The widget builds its suggestions from a summary the app writes. If the app has not run for a while, that summary can be from an earlier day — Wardo tells you so rather than showing you yesterday’s outfit as if it were today’s. Opening the app refreshes it immediately.

What is cost-per-wear?

If you enter what an item cost, Wardo divides it by the number of times you have logged it as worn. It only shows this once there are enough wears for the number to mean something, and it is always framed as “at least” — it can only count wears you logged.

What does Wardo Pro include?

Pro removes all ads and unlocks unlimited saved outfits, unlimited daily suggestions, the full planning calendar, trips and packing lists, and full statistics. Widgets are free forever and will never be moved behind Pro.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings › Wardo Pro in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions, or in the app tap Settings › Wardo Pro › Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the current period ends.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Settings › Export produces a single file containing your items, their photos, your outfits and your wear history. It is yours to keep, and it does not require Pro.

I’m not signed in to iCloud — does the app still work?

Completely. Everything works locally. Only cross-device sync is unavailable, and Settings will tell you plainly that your closet is stored on that device only.
