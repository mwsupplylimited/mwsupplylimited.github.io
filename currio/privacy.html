Currio Privacy Policy

Effective 23 July 2026 · Last updated 2 November 2026

Summary: Currio has no account system and we run no servers of our own. Your currency list and settings stay on your device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, sync through your own private iCloud (Apple's iCloud key-value storage). To show daily exchange rates, Currio fetches an exchange-rate table over the internet without sending your currency list or entered amounts. The free version shows ads served through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), including Unity Ads, whose advertising SDKs process device and ad-interaction data to serve ads. Upgrading to Currio Pro removes all ads.

1. Who we are

Currio is developed by MW Supply Limited (“we”). This policy explains what stays on your device, what is fetched from public rate services, and what is processed by the limited third-party services we use to show ads (free version) and to offer an optional Pro upgrade. The app has no account system and no backend of ours — you can use every feature without signing in to us.

2. Information stored on your device & iCloud

Currio stores the following so the app remembers it between launches:

  • Your chosen currencies, their order, and your input/base currency;
  • Your settings (theme, accent, digit grouping, refresh preference);
  • Any rate alerts you create (a currency pair and a target value);
  • Saved price-comparison rows, including prices, tax, shipping and currency choices;
  • A cached copy of the most recent exchange-rate table, so conversion works offline.

This is stored on your device with Apple's on-device storage. When you are signed in to iCloud, your currency list, settings, alerts, and price-comparison rows also sync through your own private iCloud database using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they stay in sync across your devices and are restored when you set up a new device. This data lives in your personal iCloud account under your Apple ID — it is visible only to you, handled under Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy, and we never receive it or have access to it. You can remove it by deleting the app and, if you wish, deleting Currio's iCloud data in iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud.

3. Exchange-rate data (network)

To show daily exchange rates, Currio fetches a table of exchange rates from a free, public exchange-rate service (open.er-api.com). Historical charts (Pro) fetch published European Central Bank reference rates from a public service (frankfurter.dev). These requests contain only the currency codes needed for the lookup and standard technical information such as your IP address; they include no account, name, location, or list data, and we do not receive them. Exchange rates are indicative and provided for everyday reference only.

4. Advertising (free version)

The free version uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation with Unity Ads. It can show a banner, a limited interstitial after a comparison session, and an optional rewarded video. Completing an available rewarded video can unlock Compare for one hour without starting a subscription.

Advertising providers may process device identifiers, device and operating-system information, IP address and approximate region, and ad impressions or interactions. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is available only when you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency controls. Currio does not provide your currency list, entered amounts, or comparison contents to the advertising SDKs.

See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for the provider's data practices and choices. MW Supply Limited receives aggregate advertising reports such as impressions and estimated revenue.

Your choices. Personalized Ads in Currio Settings defaults to off. Currio reports no personalization consent unless you turn it on; allowing Apple's tracking prompt does not switch it on. You can change the toggle in Currio Settings and tracking permission in iOS Settings. Turning personalization off does not remove advertising or the technical processing needed to deliver it.

Removing ads. Verified Currio Pro subscribers are not shown ads. The app does not start its advertising SDK for an established Pro subscriber and stops requesting ads when Pro is verified during a session. Currio does not include Google AdMob or Google's User Messaging Platform.

5. Currio Pro & payments

Currio Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription (monthly or annual) that removes all ads and unlocks price comparison, historical charts, rate alerts, unlimited currencies, and premium themes. Purchases, billing, renewals, and refunds are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit — we never see or store your name, card number, or Apple ID. To unlock Pro, the app checks your purchase entitlement directly with the App Store on your device; this check does not identify you to us. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

6. Notifications

If you create a rate alert (Pro) and grant notification permission, Currio schedules a local notification on your device when a rate refresh detects that your target has been reached. Rates update daily and background delivery is subject to iOS scheduling. Alerts are evaluated on your device from the rate data described in section 3; no notification data is sent to us or to any third party.

7. Third-party services and diagnostics

Currio uses Apple's StoreKit for purchases, iCloud key-value storage for preferences, public exchange-rate services, and Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads for free-tier advertising. Provider data handling is described above and in the linked provider policies.

Currio has no third-party product-analytics framework. Subscription-flow diagnostics are recorded locally using Apple's system logging, with coarse event names and timing categories. These diagnostics do not contain your entered amounts, comparison contents, receipt data, email, or persistent user identifiers, and Currio does not send them to an analytics server. Apple's system crash and diagnostic sharing is controlled by your iOS Analytics & Improvements settings.

8. Permissions and identifiers

Currio does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or health data. The advertising identifier is used only for ads in the free version, and only if you allow tracking as described in section 4. Notification permission is requested only if you choose to create a rate alert.

9. Children

Currio is a general-audience utility rated 4+ on the App Store. Currio is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Upgrading to Currio Pro removes all ads.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Currio handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy nutrition labels for the affected version.

11. Contact
