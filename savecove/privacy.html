Savecove Privacy Policy

Effective 1 July 2026 · Last updated 1 July 2026

Summary: Savecove has no account and no first-party analytics, and MW Supply Limited runs no servers that receive your data. Videos you save stay on your device (and, if you choose, in Apple Photos, Files, or your own iCloud). Anything you type into the in-app browser — including website logins — stays on your device. The free tier shows ads through Google AdMob and asks App Tracking Transparency permission before using an advertising identifier.

1. Information we collect

Savecove has no account system, no login to us, and no first-party analytics, and MW Supply Limited operates no servers that receive your data. The videos and files you save are stored locally in the app's own storage and are not uploaded anywhere unless you choose to save them to Apple Photos, the Files app, or your own iCloud. The only data processing beyond your own device is done by the advertising provider on the free tier, described in section 6.

2. The in-app browser & website logins

Savecove includes a web browser built on Apple's WebKit. Pages you visit, cookies, and anything you type — including any usernames or passwords you enter to log in to a website — are handled by WebKit and stored only in the app's own storage on your device. This information is used solely to load the pages you ask for and to detect saveable media on them. It is never sent to MW Supply Limited, and we cannot see your browsing or your logins. You can clear the browser's data at any time in the app's Settings.

3. Saving to Photos & Files

When you save a video, Savecove downloads it to a temporary location and then writes it to the app's own library and, if you choose, to Apple Photos or the Files app. Photos access is requested as add-only by default, so the app can add the videos you save without reading the rest of your photo library. No image or video is ever sent to MW Supply Limited.

4. On-device storage

Saved videos and your download history are stored in the app's own sandbox on your device. Your in-app preferences are stored locally using iOS's standard mechanisms and are not transmitted anywhere. You can delete saved videos and clear your history at any time inside the app.

5. Subscriptions & payments

Savecove Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple's In-App Purchase using StoreKit. Your purchase, payment, and renewals are handled entirely by Apple under your Apple Account; MW Supply Limited never sees your name, your Apple ID, or your card or payment details. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled any time in your Apple Account settings.

6. Advertising (free tier only)

The free tier of Savecove shows advertising provided by Google AdMob. If you subscribe to Savecove Pro, the app shows no ads at all and the AdMob SDK is not started.

  • What AdMob may process: a device advertising identifier, approximate (coarse) device information, IP address, and ad-interaction data (which ads were shown or tapped), used to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Your consent: the app asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission before any identifier is used for personalised ads, and shows a consent form where required (for example in the EEA/UK). If you decline, you still get ads — just non-personalised ones.
  • Managing your choices: you can change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • This advertising data is collected and processed by Google as an independent controller, not by MW Supply Limited. Google's practices are described in Google's privacy policy and Google's advertising policy.

7. iCloud

If you enable iCloud sync, Savecove uses Apple's iCloud to mirror your saved-video library and download history across your own devices signed in to the same Apple ID. This data lives in your own iCloud account, is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and is not accessible to MW Supply Limited. You can turn iCloud off for the app in iOS Settings.

8. Third-party content & your responsibility

Savecove is a general-purpose web browser and media saver. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any website or content provider. Savecove does not remove or circumvent digital rights management (DRM), and DRM-protected or paid streaming content cannot be saved. You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to download and use any content you save, and for complying with the terms of service of the sites you visit and applicable copyright law.

9. Children

Savecove is not directed to children under 13 and is rated 4+. The free tier uses Google AdMob with age-appropriate ad settings where required, and the app collects no personal data itself, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Savecove handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy labels for the affected version of the app.

11. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
