Savecove Privacy Policy

Effective 1 July 2026 · Last updated 10 July 2026

Summary: Savecove has no account and no first-party analytics, and MW Supply Limited runs no servers that receive your data. Videos you import stay on your device (and, if you choose, in Apple Photos, Files, or your own iCloud). The free version shows ads through Google AdMob and asks App Tracking Transparency permission before using an advertising identifier.

1. Information we collect

Savecove has no account system, no login to us, and no first-party analytics, and MW Supply Limited operates no servers that receive your data. The videos you import are stored locally in the app's own storage and are not uploaded anywhere unless you choose to export them to Apple Photos or the Files app, or turn on iCloud sync. The only data processing beyond your own device is done by the advertising provider on the free version, described in section 5.

2. Importing videos

Savecove is a personal video library. You bring videos in by choosing them from the Files app or your Photos library; the app copies each selected file into its own on-device storage so you can play it offline. Savecove does not include a web browser and does not download from, or connect to, any third-party website or service. When you import from Photos, access is limited to the videos you pick — Savecove does not read the rest of your photo library.

3. Export to Photos & Files

You can export a copy of any video from your library to Apple Photos or the Files app. Photos access is requested as add-only by default, so the app can add a video without reading your existing photos. No video is ever sent to MW Supply Limited.

4. On-device storage & the private library

Imported videos and your in-app preferences are stored in the app's own sandbox on your device using iOS's standard mechanisms, and are not transmitted anywhere. Videos you move into the Private area are hidden from the main library and unlocked with Face ID (or your device passcode) using Apple's on-device authentication; the private files themselves are not encrypted. You can delete any video at any time inside the app.

5. Advertising (free version only)

The free version of Savecove shows advertising provided by Google AdMob. If you subscribe to Savecove Pro, the app shows no ads at all and the AdMob SDK is not started.

  • What AdMob may process: a device advertising identifier, approximate (coarse) device information, IP address, and ad-interaction data (which ads were shown or tapped), used to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Your consent: the app asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission before any identifier is used for personalised ads, and shows a consent form where required (for example in the EEA/UK). If you decline, you still get ads — just non-personalised ones.
  • Managing your choices: you can change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • This advertising data is collected and processed by Google as an independent controller, not by MW Supply Limited. Google's practices are described in Google's privacy policy and Google's advertising policy.

6. Subscriptions & payments

Savecove Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple's In-App Purchase using StoreKit. Your purchase, payment, and renewals are handled entirely by Apple under your Apple Account; MW Supply Limited never sees your name, your Apple ID, or your card or payment details. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled any time in your Apple Account settings.

7. iCloud

If you enable iCloud sync (a Pro feature), Savecove uses Apple's iCloud to mirror your video library across your own devices signed in to the same Apple ID. This data lives in your own iCloud account, is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and is not accessible to MW Supply Limited. You can turn iCloud sync off for the app in its Settings.

8. Your content & responsibility

Savecove is a personal library for videos you already have on your device. You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to store and use the videos you import, in accordance with applicable copyright law.

9. Children

Savecove is not directed to children under 13. The free version uses Google AdMob with age-appropriate ad settings where required, and the app collects no personal data itself, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Savecove handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy labels for the affected version of the app.

11. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
