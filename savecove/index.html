Savecove

Save videos from the web and watch them offline. Version 1.0

About

Savecove is an in-app web browser and video saver for iPhone and iPad. Browse to a page in the built-in browser, and when a saveable video is there, Savecove offers to save it to your private library so you can watch it later — even without a connection. Everything plays back in a built-in player, and you can export saved videos to Apple Photos or the Files app.

Savecove is not affiliated with any website or content provider. Please only save content that you own or have the right to download, and respect the terms of service of the sites you visit. DRM-protected and paid streaming content cannot be saved.

Getting started

  • Open the browser tab and go to a page that has a video — or paste a link you copied.
  • When Savecove detects a saveable video, a Save button appears. Tap it and choose the quality you want.
  • Your video lands in Library, where you can play it offline, rename it, or export it to Photos or Files.
  • Use folders and search in Library to keep a growing collection organised.

Features

  • Built-in browser with automatic video detection
  • Quality picker — choose the resolution before you save
  • Offline player with background audio and Picture in Picture
  • Library with folders, search, and rename
  • Export to Apple Photos or the Files app
  • Optional iCloud sync of your library across your own devices

FAQ

What can I save with Savecove?

You can save videos that are delivered as ordinary files and that you have the right to download. Savecove does not break or bypass copy protection, so DRM-protected videos and paid or subscription streaming services cannot be saved. Some sites also stream video in protected ways that can't be saved.

Where are my saved videos?

They're in the Library tab, stored privately inside the app. From there you can play them offline, or export a copy to Apple Photos or the Files app to keep it in your main library.

Can it download in the background?

With Savecove Pro, downloads continue in the background while you use other apps, and resume automatically if the connection drops. On the free tier, keep Savecove open while a video saves.

Do I need an account?

No. Savecove has no account and no sign-up. If a website you visit requires a login, you log in to that site in the in-app browser — those credentials stay on your device and are never sent to us.
