Savecove

Your private offline video library. Version 1.0

About

Savecove is a private offline video library for iPhone and iPad. Bring in the videos you already own from the Files app or your Photos library, then play, organize, and keep them — all on your device, with no account required. Everything plays back in a built-in player, you can sort videos into folders, and personal clips can be locked away behind Face ID.

Savecove is a personal media library for videos you already have. It is not a web browser or downloader — videos come in only through Import from your own Files and Photos.

Getting started

  • Open the Library tab and tap Import from Files or Import from Photos.
  • Pick one or more videos. Savecove copies them into your on-device library.
  • Tap a video to play it offline — double-tap to skip, pinch to zoom, swipe between videos.
  • Use folders, search, and sort to keep a growing collection organized, and move personal videos into the Private area to lock them behind Face ID.

Features

  • Import videos you own from Files & Photos
  • Offline player — double-tap to skip, pinch to zoom, swipe between clips
  • Library with folders, search, sort, and rename
  • Private library — hide personal videos behind Face ID
  • Export a copy to Apple Photos or the Files app
  • Optional iCloud sync of your library across your own devices

FAQ

What videos can I add?

Any video files you already have on your device — in the Files app (including iCloud Drive) or your Photos library. Savecove imports a copy so you can keep everything together and watch offline. It does not browse the web or download from other services.

Where are my videos stored?

Privately, inside the app on your device, in the Library tab. You can export a copy to Apple Photos or the Files app any time, and with Pro you can sync your library to your other devices over iCloud.

How does the Face ID private library work?

With Pro, move any video into the Private area and it is hidden from the main grid and unlocked with Face ID (or your passcode). Private videos are hidden behind Face ID on this device; the files themselves are not encrypted.

Do I need an account?

No. Savecove has no account and no sign-up. Your videos stay on your device (and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud).
