Pourfect

A relaxing ball-sort puzzle for iPhone & iPad. Pour, sort, relax. Version 1.0

About

Pourfect is a calm, satisfying color-sorting puzzle. Each tube holds a stack of colored balls all mixed up. Pour balls between tubes until every tube holds a single color. No timer, no pressure — just you, the colors, and that "one more level" feeling. Every level is generated fresh and is always solvable.

How to play

  • Tap a tube to pick up the balls of its top color (they lift up).
  • Tap another tube to pour them in. You can only pour onto the same color, or into an empty tube.
  • Sort every tube into a single color (or leave it empty) to complete the level.
  • Earn coins for each level — faster solves earn more.

Helpers

  • Undo — step back as many moves as you like, free.
  • Hint — reveals a smart next move (costs coins, or watch a short ad).
  • Add Tube — adds an extra empty tube when you're stuck (costs coins, or watch a short ad).

Themes

Open Themes from the menu to change the look of the balls and background. Two themes are free; unlock the rest with coins you earn by playing, or by watching an optional rewarded ad. Your unlocked themes are saved.

Remove Ads & coins

Pourfect is free and supported by ads. A one-time Remove Ads purchase removes the banner and the full-screen ads between levels (optional rewarded ads, like Free Coins, stay available if you choose them). You can also buy a coin pack from the Shop. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings → Restore Purchases.

Your progress & iCloud

Your level, coins, and unlocked themes are saved on your device and backed up to your own private iCloud, so they follow you across your devices when you're signed in to iCloud. We never see this data.

FAQ

Is every level solvable?

Yes. Each level is generated and verified to be solvable before you see it. If you get stuck, use Undo to back up, or Add a Tube for more room.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads.

Open Settings → Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account used for the purchase. Rewarded ads you choose to watch (Hint, Add Tube, Free Coins) are not affected by Remove Ads.

Can I turn off sound or haptics?

Yes — in Settings from the main menu.
