Pourfect Privacy Policy

Effective 1 June 2026 · Last updated 1 June 2026

Summary: Pourfect has no accounts and asks for no personal information. Your progress stays on your device and your own iCloud. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and to do that AdMob (a third party) collects limited device and usage data. You can play with non-personalized ads by declining tracking, or remove ads entirely with a one-time purchase.

1. Who we are

Pourfect is developed by MW Supply Limited ("we"). We do not run our own servers and we have no account system. We cannot identify you personally.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location, and we keep no profile about you. Your level, coins, settings, and unlocked themes are stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, mirrored to your own private iCloud (Apple's key-value store). That data lives in your Apple Account; we have no access to it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pourfect is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To display and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google (as an independent controller) may collect and process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (e.g. the IDFA, only if you allow tracking).
  • Coarse, non-precise information such as general device type, OS, and approximate region.
  • Ad-interaction and app-usage data needed to deliver ads, measure performance, cap frequency, and prevent fraud.

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. App Tracking Transparency

On first launch the app shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, ads may be personalized using your advertising identifier. If you decline (or do nothing), ads are non-personalized and your IDFA is not used for cross-app tracking. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Rewarded and optional ads

Some ads are optional and rewarded — for example, watching a short video for a hint, an extra tube, free coins, or to unlock a theme. These play only when you choose to start them. The same AdMob data practices above apply.

6. In-app purchases

Pourfect offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase and an optional coin pack, processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details.

7. Children

Pourfect is suitable for all ages and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app contains third-party advertising, the App Store age rating reflects that, and ads are served on a non-personalized basis for users who have not consented to tracking.

8. Your choices

  • Decline tracking at the prompt, or in iOS Settings, to keep ads non-personalized.
  • Buy Remove Ads to eliminate banner and full-screen ads.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings anytime.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how Pourfect handles data, we will update this page and the date above, and reflect material changes in the App Store privacy details.

10. Contact

Questions can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
