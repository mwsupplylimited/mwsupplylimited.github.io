AdMob Monitor

Your Google AdMob earnings & statistics, natively on iPhone and iPad — today's revenue at a glance, per-app and per-ad-unit breakdowns, custom reports and payment-threshold tracking. Version 1.0

Not affiliated with Google. AdMob Monitor is an unofficial, independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google LLC. AdMob is a trademark of Google LLC. The app reads your data through the official AdMob API after you sign in with your own Google account.

What it does

AdMob Monitor is a companion to the AdMob web console, organised the same way so everything is where you expect it:

  • Home — Today, Yesterday, This month and Last month estimated earnings, with a daily trend chart and an "as of" timestamp.
  • Apps — every app in your account ranked by earnings, with impressions, CTR, match rate and RPM; tap through for a per-app trend and its ad units.
  • Ad units — per-unit earnings, impressions, RPM and show rate, sortable.
  • Reports — a custom report builder: pick metrics, dimensions, and date ranges, with results you can export as CSV.
  • Payments — estimated unpaid earnings, progress toward your payment threshold, and monthly history.

Signing in

  • Tap Sign in with Google and use the Google account that owns (or has access to) your AdMob account.
  • The app asks for read-only access to your AdMob data — it cannot change anything in your account.
  • Your sign-in happens directly with Google in a secure browser sheet; the app never sees your password.
  • You can disconnect anytime: sign out in Settings, and optionally revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

About the numbers

All earnings are estimates. AdMob finalizes earnings at the end of each month (after invalid-traffic adjustments), recent clicks and impressions can take a few hours to appear, and mediation figures lag further. AdMob Monitor shows exactly what the AdMob API reports — the same data behind the web console — with an "as of" time on every screen.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't today's numbers match the AdMob console exactly?

Both show estimates that update on a delay, so brief differences are normal — especially for the last few hours. The app pulls straight from the official AdMob API and never adjusts the numbers; totals reconcile once AdMob's own reporting catches up.

Is my AdMob account safe?

Yes. Access is read-only — the app cannot edit ad units, mediation, or any account settings. Sign-in uses Google's own OAuth flow; the app never sees or stores your password, and your access token is stored only in your device's Keychain.

Does the app see my payment details?

No. The AdMob API does not expose payment records or bank details, and the app never asks for them. The Payments screen estimates your unpaid balance from reported earnings and clearly says so.

I manage several AdMob accounts — are they supported?

Yes, with Pro you can switch between every account your Google login can access.

Which AdMob account does it show after I sign in?

The account(s) associated with the Google login you used. If you see no data, make sure you signed in with the Google account that opens your AdMob console at admob.google.com.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes — AdMob Monitor works on iPhone and iPad.

How do I cancel Pro?

Manage or cancel anytime on your device under Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.
