Reading your watch

A useful number starts with a good reading.

Acoustic estimates and observations over time answer different questions. Beatfolio keeps them separate.

How do I take a manual reading?
  1. Open your watch and choose to record a measurement.
  2. Set the hour, minute and second your watch is about to display. Choose how the watch was kept.
  3. Check the reference status. Tap Record at the instant the watch reaches the selected time.
  4. Review the captured offset, add a note if useful, and save.

The captured time stays fixed while you review it. If saving fails, the result remains on screen so you can retry.

Why is there no daily rate yet?

An offset is a single comparison; a daily rate needs a change over time. Record at least two readings an hour apart in the same uninterrupted session. Longer observations reduce the influence of small timing differences when you tap.

Start a new session when you reset the watch or it stops. Readings without a confirmed session are kept for review instead of being used to guess a rate.

Why do the acoustic result and observed drift differ?

The microphone estimates how the movement is running during a short capture. Manual readings show how the watch actually gained or lost time between observations. Position, winding, wear, noise and the device can affect the results.

Repeat acoustic readings in a quiet setting with consistent placement. These are estimates, not a professional timing-bench measurement or a certification.

The reference clock cannot sync. What now?

Try another network or cellular connection, then refresh the reference. Some networks block time-service requests. Beatfolio uses public time services from Apple, Google and the NTP Pool.

The screen identifies whether it is using a recent network reference, a previously obtained reference, or your device clock. If it falls back to the device clock, the comparison is only as accurate as that clock.

Microphone access is off. Can I still use the app?

Yes. Manual readings and your collection do not require the microphone. To use acoustic capture, allow microphone access for Beatfolio in your device’s Settings. Live audio is processed on your device; Beatfolio does not save a raw audio recording or upload it.

Your collection, cared for

Keep a copy.
Keep control.

iCloud sync helps your devices stay in step. A backup is a separate copy you can keep somewhere safe.

How do iCloud sync and backups differ?

With iCloud available for Beatfolio, your collection can sync between devices using the same iCloud account. Check the app’s iCloud status for transfer activity. Being signed in does not mean every change has finished uploading.

Use a full JSON backup when you want a separate copy of your watches, photographs and history. Save it outside the app, such as in Files. Spreadsheet exports are useful for analysis; choose the full backup for restoring a collection.

Can I bring a Chronograde collection into Beatfolio?

Yes, by explicitly selecting a compatible Chronograde JSON backup for import. Beatfolio does not open Chronograde’s collection or iCloud storage automatically. Importing a backup copies its contents into Beatfolio and does not modify the original app or backup.

Review the import before confirming. Each archived watch is imported separately by default. Only select an existing destination when you are sure both entries represent the same physical watch. A repeated import may create duplicates.

Chronograde subscriptions, lifetime purchases and other unlocks do not transfer to Beatfolio. Their billing and access remain separate.

What does “Review unlinked readings” mean?

Some readings do not yet have a confirmed timing session. If iCloud is still transferring data, wait for it to finish first. The readings remain in your backup, but they are excluded from rate calculations until their session is known.

If review is still needed, select only readings taken without resetting or stopping that watch between them. Beatfolio creates a new, closed session for your selection and writes a safety backup before changing anything. Export that safety copy from the review screen if you want to keep it outside the app.

What if saving, restoring or opening my collection fails?

Keep any captured result or form open, read the error, and retry when you have addressed it. Check available device storage if space is low. A restore that cannot finish saving does not replace your existing collection.

If the collection will not open, retry or contact support before reinstalling. Deleting the app removes its local data and local safety backups. Keep an exported backup when possible.

Manage Apple subscriptions
How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Use Manage/Cancel Subscription in Beatfolio, or open your device’s Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Auto-renewing subscriptions continue until cancelled. Apple shows the current plan and renewal or expiry information.

If you do not want a trial or subscription to renew, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. See Apple’s cancellation guide and the standard End User License Agreement.

I purchased Beatfolio before. How do I restore access?

Use the same Apple Account that made the Beatfolio purchase, then choose Restore Purchases on the membership screen. A pending purchase may still need Apple or family approval. If access does not return, check the purchase in your Apple account and contact support with the result.

Never send your Apple password, payment-card details or verification codes. A Chronograde purchase is separate and does not restore Beatfolio access.

What are my advertising choices?

Advertising is optional. Where ads are available, Beatfolio requires both your advertising-data consent in the app and Apple’s tracking permission before requesting them. Declining either keeps the app usable. You can turn off advertising consent in Settings, and Pro removes ads.
