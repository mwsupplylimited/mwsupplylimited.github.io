This policy explains how Beatfolio: Watch Accuracy, provided by MW Supply Limited, handles information when you use the app or contact us.

1. Your collection and iCloud

Beatfolio does not require a separate app account. It stores the information you enter or choose to save, including:

  • Watch brand, model, nickname, reference and serial numbers, caliber, movement details, notes and selected photographs.
  • Optional purchase information, wear dates, service history, costs and reminders.
  • Manual readings, their timestamps and offsets, reference status, watch positions and measurement sessions.
  • Saved acoustic results, such as estimated rate, beat error and beat rate, together with their dates, positions and notes.

These records support the features you use, such as your collection, history and calculations. They are stored in the app on your device. When iCloud is available and enabled for Beatfolio, Apple’s CloudKit service can synchronize them with your other devices through your private iCloud account.

We do not operate a server that receives your watch collection. We cannot browse your private iCloud collection or recover its contents for you. Apple operates iCloud under Apple’s privacy policy and your iCloud settings.

The app keeps local preferences and sync status, including transfer dates, counts and errors. It uses an account-scoped identifier locally to keep one iCloud account’s sync history separate from another. This is not a Beatfolio login or an advertising profile.

Beatfolio and Chronograde are separate

Beatfolio uses its own collection and iCloud storage. It does not automatically read or import Chronograde’s data. If you select a compatible Chronograde backup, the imported copy becomes part of your Beatfolio collection and is handled under this policy. The original app’s collection and purchases are unchanged.

2. Microphone, photos and notifications

Microphone
With your permission, acoustic capture analyzes live sound on your device to estimate the movement’s timing. Beatfolio does not save a raw audio recording or upload microphone audio. A saved result contains measurement values and associated details, not the sound itself. Manual readings remain usable if you decline microphone access.
Selected photos
You choose images through Apple’s photo picker. Beatfolio imports the selected image for your watch; it does not scan your photo library. The saved image is part of your collection and may be included in iCloud sync and full backups.
Notifications
If you allow them, the app can schedule reminders on your device. Reminder settings and relevant watch information are used to prepare those notifications. You can disable notifications in your device’s Settings.

You can change microphone, notification and tracking permissions in your device’s Settings. Removing a permission does not remove collection records you have already saved.

3. Network time requests

To obtain a time reference, Beatfolio sends time-protocol requests to time.apple.com, time.google.com and pool.ntp.org. The service operators can receive your network IP address and the timing information needed to answer the request. These requests do not contain your watch collection, photographs, notes or microphone audio.

A time request is separate from advertising. The app shows when a network reference is unavailable and it is using the device clock instead.

4. Purchases and membership

Apple processes in-app purchases through the App Store. Beatfolio uses Apple-provided transaction and subscription information to determine access, display your plan, restore purchases, and show available prices and billing periods. This can include product identifiers, purchase or renewal dates, expiry dates and renewal status.

We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Apple may provide sales, subscription and other App Store reports to us. See App Store & Privacy for Apple’s processing.

You can manage your Apple subscriptions or follow Apple’s cancellation guide. Deleting Beatfolio does not cancel a subscription. The Apple standard End User License Agreement applies.

5. Optional advertising and tracking

Advertising is optional. Where advertising is available, Beatfolio uses Unity LevelPlay and Ad Quality, with advertising supplied through the ironSource Exchange (iSX). Advertising-data consent starts off. The app requires your consent in Beatfolio and Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission before starting advertising services and requesting ads. If you decline either, no ads are requested and the app remains usable. Pro access also prevents ad requests.

When you enable advertising, these services may collect device and installation identifiers, advertising and exchange user identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device, app and connection information, ad views and interactions, and performance or diagnostic information. They use this data to provide and measure advertising, personalize ads, maintain ad quality and prevent fraud. With tracking permission, ad impressions and interactions may be linked with device or exchange user identifiers to deliver ads and measure advertising across apps. Advertising information can also be linked with information from other companies’ apps or websites.

Unity’s Ad Quality service may process device identifiers, performance and other diagnostic information for analytics and service functionality. Beatfolio does not pass your watch records, selected photos, collection notes or microphone audio to these services.

Turn off advertising-data consent in Beatfolio’s Settings to remove the banner and stop further ad requests. You can also withdraw tracking permission in your device’s Settings. Withdrawing permission does not automatically erase data a provider already received. For provider retention, international processing and privacy requests, see Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity’s privacy choices.

6. Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, the message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate problems and improve the app. Email is handled through our email provider. Please share only what is needed, and hide private details in screenshots. A full collection backup may contain serial numbers, purchase information, photos and notes; send one only when needed for an agreed support investigation.

We do not add a separate app analytics or remote crash-reporting service. Apple may make diagnostics or app-usage reports available under your Apple sharing settings. Advertising-related analytics are described separately above.

These support pages use no advertising scripts, embedded analytics, cookies set by our page code, or externally hosted fonts. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request data such as IP addresses and browser information to deliver and protect its services, under the GitHub privacy statement. Following an external link opens a service governed by its own policy.

7. Retention, deletion and your choices

Collection records: You can edit or delete records in the app. Where an item is kept in a recovery bin, it remains recoverable until permanently deleted there. Changes can propagate to your other devices through iCloud. Removing the app deletes its local app data, but does not by itself remove copies already held in iCloud or backups you saved elsewhere.

Backups and exports: You control where you save or share them. A full JSON backup contains the collection’s data and photos. Session recovery also writes a local safety backup before changes. You can export those safety copies; deleting the app removes copies kept only inside it. Delete exported files separately from Files, your chosen cloud provider or any recipient’s storage. Sharing a file makes it available to the destination you select.

Support correspondence: We retain messages as needed to resolve the request, maintain relevant support records and meet applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to access, correct or delete information you sent us. We may need to retain information where a legal requirement or unresolved issue applies.

Third-party services: Apple, Unity and our hosting or email providers have their own retention and international-processing practices. Use their privacy controls for data they hold. We cannot delete information in your private iCloud account, another person’s copy of an export, or a provider’s independent records on your behalf.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise rights for information we hold. We use support information to provide the help you request and maintain the service; optional advertising depends on your consent.
