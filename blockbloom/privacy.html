Block Bloom Privacy Policy

Effective 1 June 2026 · Last updated 1 June 2026

Summary: Block Bloom has no accounts and asks for no personal information. Your game progress stays on your device and your own iCloud. The app does show ads through Google AdMob, and to do that AdMob (a third party) collects limited device and usage data. You can play with non-personalized ads by declining tracking, or remove ads entirely with a one-time purchase.

1. Who we are

Block Bloom is developed by MW Supply Limited ("we"). We do not operate our own servers and we do not run an account system. We cannot identify you personally.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location, and we do not maintain any profile about you. Your high score, coins, settings, and unlocked themes are stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, mirrored to your own private iCloud (Apple's key-value store). That data lives in your Apple Account; we have no access to it and never receive a copy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Block Bloom is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To display and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google as an independent controller may collect and process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (for example, the Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA, only if you allow tracking).
  • Coarse, non-precise information such as general device type, operating system, and approximate region.
  • Ad interaction and app-usage data needed to deliver ads, measure performance, cap frequency, and prevent fraud.

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's policies. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. App Tracking Transparency

On first launch the app shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, ads may be personalized using your advertising identifier. If you decline (or take no action), ads are non-personalized and your IDFA is not used for cross-app tracking. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Rewarded and optional ads

Some ads are optional and rewarded — for example, watching a short video to continue a game, double your coins, or unlock a theme. These play only when you choose to start them. The same AdMob data practices described above apply.

6. In-app purchases

Block Bloom offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Whether you own the purchase is determined by Apple's StoreKit on your device.

7. Children

Block Bloom is suitable for all ages and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app contains third-party advertising, the App Store age rating and content reflect that. Where required, ads are served on a non-personalized basis for users who have not consented to tracking.

8. Your choices

  • Decline tracking at the prompt, or in iOS Settings, to keep ads non-personalized.
  • Buy Remove Ads to eliminate banner and full-screen ads.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings at any time.

9. Data retention & security

Because we do not collect personal data on any server we control, we have nothing to retain or delete on our side. Ad-related data is handled by Google under its own retention and security practices. On-device data can be removed at any time by deleting the app.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Block Bloom handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above, and reflect material changes in the App Store privacy details for the affected version.

11. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to mwsupplylimited@gmail.com.
