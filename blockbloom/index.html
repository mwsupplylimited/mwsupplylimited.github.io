Block Bloom

A relaxing block puzzle for iPhone & iPad. Drop blocks, clear lines, watch them bloom. Version 1.0

About

Block Bloom is a calm, satisfying block puzzle. Drag the three pieces from the tray onto the 9×9 board. Fill a full row, a full column, or a full 3×3 box and it clears in a burst of color. Chain clears together for combos, build streaks for bonus points, and chase your high score. No timer, no pressure — just one more move.

How to play

  • Drag a piece from the tray at the bottom onto the board. A preview shows where it will land.
  • Clear lines by completely filling any row, column, or 3×3 box. Clear several at once for a combo, and clear on back-to-back moves for a streak multiplier.
  • Plan ahead — the game ends when none of your three pieces can fit anywhere on the board.
  • Earn coins every time you clear lines, and spend them on new color themes.

Themes

Open Themes from the main menu to switch the look of the whole game. Two themes are free; the rest can be unlocked with the coins you earn by clearing lines, or by watching an optional rewarded ad. Your unlocked themes are remembered.

Remove Ads

Block Bloom is free and supported by ads. A one-time Remove Ads purchase removes the banner and the full-screen ads between games. Optional rewarded ads (for example, "Continue" after a game over, or "Double Coins") remain available if you choose to watch them — they are always your choice.

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstall the app or get a new device, open Settings → Restore Purchases to restore your Remove Ads purchase at no charge. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account.

Your progress & iCloud

Your high score, coins, and unlocked themes are saved on your device and backed up to your own private iCloud, so they follow you to your other devices when you're signed in to iCloud. We never see this data — it lives only in your Apple Account.

FAQ

The game said "Game Over" but I still had space.

A piece can only be placed if all of its cells fit on empty squares. If none of your three current pieces fit anywhere, the game ends. Tap Continue to watch a short ad and clear some space to keep playing.

How do combos and streaks work?

Clearing two or more lines with a single placement is a combo and scores extra. Clearing at least one line on consecutive placements builds a streak that multiplies your bonus. Break the chain (a move that clears nothing) and the streak resets.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads.

Open Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase. Rewarded ads you choose to watch are not affected by Remove Ads.

Can I turn off sound or haptics?

Yes — open Settings from the main menu to toggle sound effects and haptics.
